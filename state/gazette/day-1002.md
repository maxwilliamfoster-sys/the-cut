*The Cut Gazette — day 1002*

## Bellos accuse police of targeting block  

**Harbor Bar stays noisy.**  The bar remained packed and loud for several hours with no incidents reported.  

**Residents voice grievances.**  Dez, Cassius, Jonah, Lena, Otis and Rufus all declared that “the authorities are unfairly targeting us,” echoing a growing sense of mistrust.  

**Tensions heard at tables.**  Conversations overheard included urgent plans to move, pleas for quiet, and threats to take matters into their own hands, hinting at unease among the patrons.  

Also on the block: the fog looks like it’s trying to hide the sirens.
