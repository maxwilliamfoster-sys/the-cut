*The Cut Gazette — day 1013*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block, Some Absent  

**Power outage:** A six‑hour blackout left the whole block in darkness, prompting residents to huddle by candles and keep appliances off.  

**Jonah Bello:** He learned the money he owes is now due sooner than expected, a fact that raised quiet murmurs among the Bello family.  

**Funeral attendance:** Most neighbors attended the Delmar service, though a few regulars were missing, sparking brief speculation.  

*Also on the block:* Even the streetlights seemed to take a nap during the rain.
