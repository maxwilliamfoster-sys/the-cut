*The Cut Gazette — day 1027*

## Cal’s Death Sparks Block Vigilance  

**Unidentified car** remains parked on the corner for three days, drawing curious glances from passers‑by.  

**Etta and Nia Bello** receive notices that the money they owe is now due earlier than expected, adding pressure to their households.  

**Several residents**, including Dez and Ade Okonkwo, voice fear that “someone killed Cal” and urge the community to stay watchful.  

Also on the block: the cold snap has everyone checking thermostats twice.
