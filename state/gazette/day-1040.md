*The Cut Gazette — day 1040*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block  

**Inspectors on the move** – City inspectors continued their clipboard patrol down Delmar today, checking compliance as the funeral procession passed.  

**Attendance mixed** – Most residents turned out for the Delmar funeral, though several expected guests were absent.  

**Money talks** – Local voices were heard reminding each other of overdue loans, unpaid rent and lingering debts.  

Also on the block: Even the coffee pot seemed quieter than the gossip.
