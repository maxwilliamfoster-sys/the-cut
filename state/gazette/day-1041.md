*The Cut Gazette — day 1041*

## Two Extra Police Cruisers Arrive Delmar  

**Terraces notices:** New flyers appeared on two doors, printed on unfamiliar company letterhead; no resident claimed ownership.  

**Mysterious car:** An unmarked vehicle has been parked on the corner for three days, drawing curious glances but no complaints.  

**Delmar patrol:** Two additional police cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, noted by several locals.  

Also on the block: the rain keeps everyone indoors, but the rumors keep flowing.
