*The Cut Gazette — day 1045*

## Dez Okonkwo Finds Count Short  

**Ledger discrepancy:** Dez Okonkwo reported the day's count fell short again, though only by a small margin.  

**Quiet on the block:** Several residents—Etta, Simone, Nia, Rufus, Cal and Clemmie—were noted sleeping peacefully despite the murmurs about numbers and keys.  

**Low chatter:** Aside from brief exchanges about intake figures, ledger tightness, and a missing silver key, little else was heard on the block.  

Also on the block: the mayor apparently tried to get under Otis Bello’s skin—no one’s sure why.
