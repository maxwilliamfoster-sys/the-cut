*The Cut Gazette — day 1063*

## 9th Kicks Wrong Door on Riverside  

**Door mishap:** The 9th mistakenly kicked a Riverside door, discovering it wasn’t the intended one.  

**Harbor Bar:** The bar stayed packed and noisy for hours, but no trouble was reported.  

**Police presence:** Officers logged modest activity—47 at Riverside, 18 at Delmar, and a handful elsewhere.  

Also on the block: the bell at the bakery rang twice, just to remind everyone it’s still 8 a.m.
