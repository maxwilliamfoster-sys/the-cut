*The Cut Gazette — day 1069*

## 9th Breaks Riverside Door  

**Police reports:** 73 calls on Riverside, 22 on Delmar, 5 on Terraces, 10 on Civic. The mistaken door‑kick drew the most attention.  

**Clinic visit:** Farrah Lindqvist was taken to the clinic; her paperwork listed a different injury than the one she actually sustained.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents noted a missing truck near the print shop and a faint rumor about Marta Doyle’s brother, but otherwise little was heard.  

Also on the block: Rufus Bello’s sudsy sink‑song was the only melody cutting through the fog.
