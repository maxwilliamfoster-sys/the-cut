*The Cut Gazette — day 1071*

## Cassius Accused by Multiple Residents  

**Local accusations:** Dez Okonkwo, Ade Doyle, Amara Bello, Blaise Osei, Booker Lyle and Sgt. Hal Brennan all named Cassius Reyes as the culprit in recent disputes.  

**Police activity:** The block saw modest police presence—90 officers at Riverside, 22 at Delmar, and a handful elsewhere—but no incidents were logged.  

**Quiet corners:** Lena Ferreira was seen checking a window, Tomas Mbeki tallying ledgers, and both Nadia and Marisol Vasquez were preoccupied with maps and a nap, respectively.  

Also on the block: the snow fell, and nobody complained about the cold.
