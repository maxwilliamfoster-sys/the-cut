*The Cut Gazette — day 1088*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in darkness.  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, while other beats report modest activity.  

**Neighborhood watch:** Kwame Lindqvist was seen alone checking the ledger, sighing as the cold snap settled in.  

Also on the block: the toast‑request from Cassius Adeyemi went unanswered, as usual.
