*The Cut Gazette — day 1093*

## Sgt. Brennan’s Favor Costs Brannon  

**Gil Lindqvist learns** Sami Amadi was seen talking to police, prompting quiet speculation among neighbors.  

**An ambulance** whisked an unidentified person out of the Terraces before dawn; no name was released.  

**Sgt. Hal Brennan** says the favor he gave Errol Brannon “will affect us both,” echoing concerns about pension and legal repercussions.  

Also on the block: the barbershop may change hands before anyone’s haircut is even finished.
