*The Cut Gazette — day 1096*

## Detective Ruiz promises to catch suspect

**Detective Yolanda Ruiz** assured the block she’ll “catch him before he slips away.”  
**Several locals** voiced anxiety about family and the law, from Gil Serrano’s brother inquiry to Gus Petrakis’ fear of a shutdown.  
**Night watchers** noted a quiet scene: Kwame Osei, Wes Doyle, Marta Doyle, Sami Serrano and Vic Lindqvist were all asleep, while Cass Deveaux lingered with a drink.

Also on the block: even the streetlights seemed to nap.
