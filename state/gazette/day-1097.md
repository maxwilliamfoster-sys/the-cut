*The Cut Gazette — day 1097*

## Nia Nakamura Faces Accelerated Debt Demand  

**Power outage:** The block was plunged into darkness for six hours, leaving homes and businesses without electricity until service was restored.  

**Mystery car:** A vehicle with no identifiable owner has remained parked on the corner for three consecutive days, drawing curious glances from passers‑by.  

**Tense whispers:** Residents were overheard expressing frustration and resolve about the looming debt, with Nia Nakamura promising “we’ll rise” despite the pressure.  

Also on the block: the unclaimed car finally got a ticket—still no owner in sight.
