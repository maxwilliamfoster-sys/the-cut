*The Cut Gazette — day 1101*

## Marcus Calloway and Otis Bello argue

**Police presence:** Officers patrolled the block, noting 20 at Riverside, 5 each at Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but reported no incidents beyond the dispute.

**Bystanders’ murmurs:** Residents were heard quoting the Bello family—“I want a bigger slice, Tee,” “Why are you crying?”—and others offering quiet commentary, such as “The snow’s beautiful, isn’t it?” despite the rain.

**Neighborhood watch:** Dot Feeney was seen tapping her ledger, eyes sharp, while Simone Adeyemi folded a bandage and hummed, suggesting the block’s routine kept moving.

*Also on the block: the rain kept the sidewalks slick, but nobody seemed to mind the extra shine.*
