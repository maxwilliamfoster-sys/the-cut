## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage** – The entire block lost electricity for six hours, leaving homes and shops in darkness until service was restored this afternoon.  

**Sticks’ debt** – Sticks learned the money they owe is now due ahead of schedule, prompting a tense conversation with creditors.  

**Marta Doyle’s injury** – Marta arrived at the clinic with paperwork that doesn’t match her injury, raising questions that staff are still sorting out.  

Also on the block: the only thing staying fully charged was the neighborhood’s collective curiosity.
