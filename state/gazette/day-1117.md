*The Cut Gazette — day 1117*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block Residents  

**Funeral attendance.** Most of the block turned out for the service on Delmar, though a few expected faces were missing.  

**Mystery car.** An unclaimed vehicle has been idling on the corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity from passers‑by.  

**Sparse news.** Aside from a handful of overheard snippets, the day offered little else to report.  

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than gossip is the street sweeper’s late‑morning route.
