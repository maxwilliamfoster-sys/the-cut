*The Cut Gazette — day 1118*

## Kwame Nakamura and Jonah Bello Clash

**Street argument draws a crowd.**  
Kwame Nakamura and Jonah Bello were heard shouting at each other in the middle of the block, forcing passersby to pause and listen.

**Lena Ferreira receives surprise payout.**  
A payment to Lena Ferreira exceeded expectations, prompting quiet remarks about the “own problem” it creates.

**Police presence noted across the block.**  
Officers were stationed at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic streets, though no incident was reported.

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s umbrellas open and the coffee shop line unusually short.
