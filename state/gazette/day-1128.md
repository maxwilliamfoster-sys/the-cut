*The Cut Gazette — day 1128*

## Reyes and Okonkwo Find Short Count  

**Police patrols** remained light today, with only a handful of officers noted on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic.  

**Finance check** – Both Cassius Reyes and Dez Okonkwo reported that the community fund’s count was “short again, not by much, but enough.”  

**Block chatter** – Residents were heard asking about everything from math tutoring to bike repairs, rent talks and clinic staffing, but offered no further details.  

Also on the block: the laundry‑folding rhythm kept the neighborhood humming.
