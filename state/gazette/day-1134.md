*The Cut Gazette — day 1134*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Most of Block  

**Attendance:** The service on Delmar attracted most neighborhood residents, though a few regulars were noticeably absent.  

**Murmurs:** Snatches of conversation floated by—Cassius Reyes was accused of theft, Milo Adeyemi asked for chips, and June Kowal reminded staff to keep the register locked.  

**Night Watch:** Around the same time, Etta and Dev Bello lay awake, while Jonah, Maren Adeyemi and Dr. Yusuf Amin slept soundly.

Also on the block: the remote is still missing, but the chips are finally making a round.
