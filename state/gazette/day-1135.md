*The Cut Gazette — day 1135*

## Cass Deveaux’s Favor Costs Elias Reyes  

**Power outage:** A six‑hour blackout left the block in darkness, halting work and leaving residents waiting for lights to return.  

**Police presence:** Officers were noted at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, though no incidents were reported.  

**Quiet night:** Most neighbors slept through the rain, with only a few murmurs heard from the Bello family and a request for water from Milo.  

Also on the block: the rain kept the street cleaners as idle as the night‑shift barista.
