*The Cut Gazette — day 1138*

## 9th Breaks Door on Riverside  

**Door incident:** The 9th kicked a Riverside door, later realizing it was the wrong one. “That door’s the wrong one, you hear?” muttered Booker Lyle as onlookers watched.  

**Police and inspectors:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since morning, while city inspectors move down the block with clipboards.  

**Mysterious notices:** Two doors in the Terraces now sport notices from an unfamiliar company, raising eyebrows among residents.  

*Also on the block: the overcast is the only thing staying dry.*
