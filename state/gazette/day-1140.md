*The Cut Gazette — day 1140*

## Priya Raman’s costly favor to Bello  

**Mysterious car:** An unmarked vehicle has been idling at the corner for three days, drawing curious glances but no claimants.  

**Early‑dawn ambulance:** Someone was taken from the Terraces before sunrise; no name was released.  

**The favor:** Ms. Priya Raman helped Clemmie Bello in a way that “cost them something real,” according to block chatter.  

Also on the block: the coffee steam’s the only thing rising faster than gossip.
