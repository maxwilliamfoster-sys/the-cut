*The Cut Gazette — day 1146*

## 9th Kicks Wrong Door on Riverside  

**Door incident:** The resident known as “the 9th” kicked a Riverside door, later discovering it was the wrong one.  

**Medical call:** An ambulance removed an unidentified person from the Terraces before dawn; no name was given.  

**Strange notices:** Two doors in the Terraces now display notices from an unfamiliar company, raising mild curiosity.  

Also on the block: the neighborhood’s “who‑said‑what” chorus continues to echo with more questions than answers.
