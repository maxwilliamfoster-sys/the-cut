*The Cut Gazette — day 1151*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, joining the routine patrols across the block.  

**Street disputes:** Vic Lindqvist and Kwame Bright, then later Sami Serrano and Jonah Bello, shouted at each other in the dark, forcing passersby to pause.  

**Voices in the night:** Residents were heard commenting on everything from “the mess” to “the best brew,” while several locals voiced personal grievances.

Also on the block: the neighborhood’s collective insomnia seems to be getting a new hobby—watching the lights flicker.
