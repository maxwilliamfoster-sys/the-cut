*The Cut Gazette — day 1175*

## 9th Misfires, Kicks Wrong Riverside Door  

**Riverside incident:** The 9th attempted to force entry but struck the wrong door, prompting a brief police presence (27 officers) on Riverside.  

**Delmar watch:** Two additional police cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since early morning, though no further activity has been reported.  

**Terraces notices:** Unfamiliar company letters were posted on two Terraces doors; residents have not identified the sender.  

Also on the block: June Amadi apparently mastered night‑driving with her eyes closed.
