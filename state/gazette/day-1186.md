*The Cut Gazette — day 1186*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block Residents  

**Funeral attendance.** Most of the block gathered on Delmar for a funeral; a few expected guests were absent.  

**Cassius Bello’s debt.** Bello learned the money he owes is now demanded earlier than the original agreement.  

**Reyes’ shortfall.** Cassius Reyes discovered the cash count is slightly low again, though the deficit is minimal.  

Also on the block: the blue key remains missing, and the coffee’s still brewing for the mourners.
