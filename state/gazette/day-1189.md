## Cal Doyle’s costly favor to Milo  

**Favors with a price:**  Both Cal Doyle and Odette Ferreira each did a favor that “cost them something real,” leaving Milo Adeyemi and Iris Lindqvist visibly unsettled.

Also on the block: the new bike shop is the talk of the town, even if nobody’s riding it yet.
