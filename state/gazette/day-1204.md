*The Cut Gazette — day 1204*

## Inspectors Sweep Delmar Street  

**City inspectors** moved down Delmar today, clipboards in hand, checking every storefront.  

**Police presence** was light but noted, with 20 officers near Delmar and a handful on surrounding blocks.  

**Residents** watched quietly; Cassius Bello fidgeted, Lena Brannon logged notes, and Milo Adeyemi stared out the window.  

Also on the block: the coffee beans are officially on a shortage alert.
