*The Cut Gazette — day 1206*

## 9th Breaks Riverside Door  

**Harbor Bar stays noisy but peaceful.**  The tavern was packed and loud for several hours, yet no incidents were reported.

**A mistaken kick sparks a scuffle.**  The 9th kicked what turned out to be the wrong door on Riverside, drawing a brief police presence (30 officers) while neighbors watched.

**Residents mostly silent.**  Aside from a handful of overheard remarks—Elias Reyes questioning “You still think you own this block?” and June Lindqvist asking about numbers—little else was heard.

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s hair in place but not their thoughts.
