*The Cut Gazette — day 1209*

## Two Police Cruisers Deploy to Delmar  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, joining the routine patrols noted in the block’s police tally.  

**City inspection:** Inspectors are moving down Delmar with clipboards, checking businesses and properties as part of the latest municipal sweep.  

**Local chatter:** Residents were heard discussing dock rumors, a returning doctor, and personal debts, but offered little concrete detail.  

Also on the block: the coin‑counter still can’t make the numbers add up.
