*The Cut Gazette — day 1213*

## Detective Ruiz Unseals Long‑Closed File  

**Harbor Bar stays packed.**  The bar was loud and full for several hours, but no incident was reported.  

**Tariq “Tee” Bello warned.**  Someone told him that Kwame Doyle was seen talking to police, prompting quiet speculation among the regulars.  

**Elias under fire.**  Multiple locals—Dez Okonkwo, Ade Doyle, Ade Ferreira, Amara and Blaise Osei—have declared that Elias is a danger and should be locked up, while whispers about the old Lyle file circulate.  

Also on the block: the snow fell, and the only thing that stayed warm was the bar’s jukebox.
