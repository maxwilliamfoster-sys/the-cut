*The Cut Gazette — day 1219*

## Dev Nakamura Seen Talking to Police  

**Cassius Reyes** was warned today that **Dev Nakamura** had been observed in conversation with officers, stirring quiet speculation among the block’s residents.  

**Neighbors chat** – Voices from the Lindqvist and Bello families floated about “new rules,” “changing times,” and “helping the family,” hinting at shifting dynamics under today’s rain.  

**Personal musings** – Several locals declared they felt “trapped” – in a never‑ending coffee shop, a perpetual fountain, or even a pocketknife – a tongue‑in‑cheek way of noting the day’s lingering gloom.  

Also on the block: the rain keeps the benches dry, but not the gossip.
