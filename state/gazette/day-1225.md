*The Cut Gazette — day 1225*

## Detective Ruiz Confronts Tariq Bello  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, and city inspectors are making rounds with clipboards.  

**Heated words:** Witnesses overheard Detective Yolanda Ruiz warning “You’re not getting away from this, Yolanda” and “I’ve seen worse,” while Tariq ‘Tee’ Bello replied, “You think you’re the first?”  

**Quiet night:** Residents—including Jonah, Milo, and Wes Doyle—spent the rainy evening sleeping, eyes glued to TV or waiting for water.  

Also on the block: The rain may be falling, but the gossip isn’t drying up.
