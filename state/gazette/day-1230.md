*The Cut Gazette — day 1230*

## Two Extra Police Cruisers on Delmar  

**Police presence:** Delmar reports the highest activity with 17 units, boosted by two additional cruisers parked since this morning.  

**Night‑time watch:** Residents Gus Petrakis and Tariq “Tee” Bello admit they lie awake listening to rain and the street’s quiet hum.  

**Detective’s focus:** Det. Yolanda Ruiz remains off‑duty, thoughts still on the Lyle investigation.  

Also on the block: the rain may be the only thing keeping the streets from emptying out.
