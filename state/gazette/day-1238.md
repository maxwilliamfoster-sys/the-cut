*The Cut Gazette — day 1238*

## Marisol Vasquez Costs Ade Osei  

**Police patrols increase.** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, bringing the block’s police presence to 19 officers.

**A costly favour.** Marisol Vasquez performed a favour for Ade Osei that “costs them something real,” according to eyewitnesses; details were not disclosed.

**Residents feel besieged.** Several locals—Dez Okonkwo, Ade Doyle, Ade Ferreira, Amara Bello, and Blaise Osei—have all voiced the sentiment that “everyone is targeting us.”

Also on the block: the neighborhood faucet still drips louder than the gossip.
