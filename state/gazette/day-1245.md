*The Cut Gazette — day 1245*

## Block Plunged into Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage** – A citywide outage left the block in darkness for six hours, prompting residents to improvise lighting and shelter.  

**Street dispute** – Fr. Emmanuel Boateng and Chinedu Okafor were heard arguing loudly enough to halt passing traffic.  

**Misdirected kick** – A resident mistakenly kicked the wrong door on Riverside, drawing brief police attention.  

Also on the block: rain kept everyone’s heads low and their socks damp.
