*The Cut Gazette — day 1246*

## Okonkwo and Brennan Clash on Delmar  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since early morning, adding to the already heightened attention on the block.  

**Inventory issue:** Malik Reyes noted the count is “short again,” though he said the discrepancy is minor.  

**Public argument:** Chidi Okonkwo and Sgt. Hal Brennan were heard shouting at each other in the street, drawing a brief crowd.

Also on the block: the only drama today was a chorus of snores from the Bello household.
