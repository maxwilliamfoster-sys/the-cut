*The Cut Gazette — day 1253*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block Together  

**Ambulance** whisked an unnamed patient from the Terraces before sunrise, leaving few details behind.  

**Funeral** on Delmar saw the majority of residents gathered; a handful of expected faces were absent.  

**Police** reported modest activity across the block, with the highest counts at Riverside (17) and equal patrols at Delmar and the Terraces (12 each).  

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than the funeral procession was Chidi’s spaghetti twirl.
