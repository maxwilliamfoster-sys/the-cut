*The Cut Gazette — day 1256*

## Vic Lindqvist Seen Speaking With Police  

**Police presence up:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s usual patrol.  

**Mysterious notices:** Unfamiliar company letterheads were posted on two doors in the Terraces; no one claims responsibility.  

**Quiet night:** Several residents—including Tiny Reyes, Maren Bello and Dr. Yusuf Amin—were observed sleeping or lying awake, underscoring the block’s unusually still evening.  

Also on the block: the only drama today was a whispered “Need a hand?” that never got an answer.
