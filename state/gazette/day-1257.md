*The Cut Gazette — day 1257*

## Amara Bello's Favor Costs Dr. Amin  

**Ambulance rushes from the Terraces before dawn.** No name was given, but the early removal was noted by several residents.  

**Two unknown‑company notices appear on Terraces doors.** The flyers were posted without comment, and no one on the block recognized the letterhead.  

**Overheard snippets hint at ordinary bustle.** Iris Nakamura asked about “new float numbers,” June Kowal requested a box moved near the register, Wes Doyle confirmed “same as before,” and Nyla Bello sought plant‑care help.  

*Also on the block:* the water‑drinking Lena Ferreira looked like she’d just discovered coffee.
