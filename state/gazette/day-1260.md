*The Cut Gazette — day 1260*

## Ambulance Removes Patient from Terraces  

**Police activity:** 24 calls on Delmar, ten extra cruisers have been stationed there since morning, while the Terraces saw a single emergency transport before dawn.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents heard Chidi Okonkwo offer project help, Booker Lyle remind Wes Doyle of an overdue loan, and Nadia Vasquez ask about the mayor’s note.  

**Quiet corners:** Several locals—Jonah Bello, Jonah Adeyemi, Wes Doyle, Kwame Nakamura, and the Lindqvist brothers—were observed resting or sleeping in various spots around the block.  

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the silence was a handful of change changing hands.
