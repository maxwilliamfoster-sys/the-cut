*The Cut Gazette — day 1261*

## Sami Amadi’s favor costs Amara Bello  

**Police reports:** 31 calls logged across Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic; no incidents reported beyond the rain.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents discussed a broken pipe, looming frost, and a few unfinished repairs, but overall “little” was heard beyond routine complaints.  

**Quiet on the block:** Several locals—including the Bello family, the Adeyemi brothers, and Dr. Yusuf Amin—were seen lying still as the rain drummed on the roofs.  

Also on the block: the rain may be the only thing falling apart today.
