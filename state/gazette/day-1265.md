*The Cut Gazette — day 1265*

## June Amadi and Blaise Mbeki Clash  

**Harbor Bar packed:** The bar stayed full and loud for hours, but no trouble was reported.  

**Inspectors on Delmar:** City inspectors walked the street with clipboards, checking businesses one by one.  

**Quiet whispers:** Residents were heard coordinating lock‑downs, bike moves and hidden watch‑posts, hinting at a tense undercurrent on the block.  

Also on the block: The only thing quieter than the police radios was the bar’s karaoke machine.
