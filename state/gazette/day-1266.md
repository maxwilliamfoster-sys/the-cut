*The Cut Gazette — day 1266*

## Kwame Nakamura Faces Early Debt Demand  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s usual patrol.  

**Tense words:** Residents were overheard trading sharp remarks—Milo Adeyemi wanted “more than just a corner,” while Det. Yolanda Ruiz warned “you’re a threat, I’ll see you.”  

**Quiet corners:** Etta Adeyemi, Etta Bello, and others were seen listening or hugging, but little else was said.  

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the cruisers was the sigh of a neighbor polishing their porch swing.
