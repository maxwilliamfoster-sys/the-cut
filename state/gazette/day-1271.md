*The Cut Gazette — day 1271*

## Booker Lyle’s costly favor to Ferreira  

**Power outage:** The block was plunged into darkness for six hours, leaving streetlights flickering and residents waiting for the lights to return.  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the routine patrols noted at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic.  

**Quiet on the block:** Aside from the occasional whispered comment, little else was heard as residents endured the blackout.  

Also on the block: Even the streetlights seemed to need a coffee break.
