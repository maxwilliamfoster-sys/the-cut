*The Cut Gazette — day 1274*

## Block Plunged into Six‑Hour Darkness  

**Power outage:** The whole block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents in the dark and prompting complaints from the likes of Errol Brannon and the Bello family.  

**Milo Bello’s surprise:** A payment to Milo Bello exceeded expectations, creating a new dilemma for him that no one on the block could quite explain.  

**Early‑dawn ambulance:** An ambulance removed an unidentified person from the Terraces before sunrise; no name was given, and the incident passed quietly.  

Also on the block: the heat kept everyone’s fans on standby, but the silence was louder than usual.
