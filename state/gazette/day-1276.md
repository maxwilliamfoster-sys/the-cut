*The Cut Gazette — day 1276*

## Hector Delgado Favors Ade Doyle  

**Construction continues** – A new building went up on the block today, adding another storefront to the streetscape.  

**A costly favor** – Hector Delgado did a favor for Ade Doyle that “cost them something real,” according to witnesses. No further details were given.  

**Neighborhood chatter** – Residents were heard talking about low cash supplies, a missing spray bottle, and the precinct’s coffee “a crime scene,” but otherwise kept to themselves.  

Also on the block: the only drama was a blanket‑tugging, ceiling‑staring contest among the Bello clan.
