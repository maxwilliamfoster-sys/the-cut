*The Cut Gazette — day 1279*

## Gus Petrakis Seen Talking to Police  

**Tip to Ade Ferreira.** A passer‑by informed Ade Ferreira that Gus Petrakis had been observed in conversation with police officers, though no further details were given.  

**Street argument.** Cassius Bello and Errol Osei shouted at each other on the sidewalk, drawing a brief crowd before the dispute cooled.  

**Quiet night.** Most residents—Ade Doyle, Gil Bright, Rufus Adeyemi, Tiny Reyes, Tomas Lindqvist and Rey Vasquez—were noted sleeping as the rain fell.  

Also on the block: the rain may have been heavy, but the town’s gossip was still light.
