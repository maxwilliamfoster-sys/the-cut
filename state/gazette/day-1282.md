*The Cut Gazette — day 1282*

## Sgt. Brennan Unseals Old Case File  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s already‑busy patrol count.  

**File mishap:** Sgt. Hal Brennan retrieved a long‑closed file and, unable to replace it, left the documents on his desk for all to see.  

**Local scene:** June Lindqvist was spotted counting change behind the till while her brother Kwame lingered at the door, eyes on the cold snap outside.  

Also on the block: the coffee shop’s thermostat finally gave up the ghost.
