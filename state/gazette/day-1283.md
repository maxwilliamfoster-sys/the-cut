*The Cut Gazette — day 1283*

## Officer Kohl Reopens Long‑Closed File  

**Power outage stalls block.** A six‑hour blackout left homes and businesses in the dark, prompting complaints about coffee, shelves, and slippery back doors.  

**Emergency transport at dawn.** An ambulance removed an unidentified patient from the Terraces just before sunrise; no further details were given.  

**Old case resurfaces.** Officer Brady Kohl retrieved a file that should have been sealed years ago and was unable to return it to its proper place.  

Also on the block: even the static on the TV seemed louder than the chatter.
