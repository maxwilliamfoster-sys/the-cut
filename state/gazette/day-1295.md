*The Cut Gazette — day 1295*

## Two Police Cruisers Parked on Delmar  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, joining the modest block patrol.

**Residents protest coffee ban:** Dez Okonkwo, Ade Doyle, Ade Ferreira, Amara Bello and Blaise Osei say the new coffee ban is a tool to push them out.

**Late‑night sleepers:** Several block members—Blaise Osei, Iris Lindqvist, Marta and Cal Ferreira, Cassius Bello, and Gil Bright—were spotted sleeping, some keeping an eye on the door.

Also on the block: The rain kept the coffee beans dry, but not the complaints.
