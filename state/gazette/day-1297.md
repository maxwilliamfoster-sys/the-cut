*The Cut Gazette — day 1297*

## Officer Kohl Misplaces Old Case File  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the already‑heightened activity on the block.  

**Officer Kohl:** While reviewing records, Officer Brady Kohl pulled a file that should have been closed years ago and was unable to replace it, prompting a brief internal inquiry.  

**Neighborhood murmur:** Aside from a few whispered comments, residents reported little else of note today.  

Also on the block: the cold snap has everyone reaching for an extra sweater—except the socks, which Etta Bello folds with a steady hum.
