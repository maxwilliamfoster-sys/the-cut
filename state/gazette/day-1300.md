*The Cut Gazette — day 1300*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block Residents  

**Terraces notices:** Two doors now display letters from an unfamiliar company; no one on the block recognizes the name.  

**Delmar funeral:** Most neighbors attended, though a few expected faces were missing.  

**Harbor Bar:** The bar stayed packed and noisy for hours, but reported no incidents.  

Also on the block: the night shift still can’t find a barber with spare wood.
