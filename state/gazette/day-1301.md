*The Cut Gazette — day 1301*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Power outage:** The block was plunged into darkness for six hours, leaving homes and businesses without electricity.  

**Ambulance departure:** An ambulance removed an unidentified person from the Terraces before dawn.  

**Cash register short:** Cassius Reyes found the count again a little short, though the deficit was modest.  

Also on the block: even the night seemed to take a coffee break.
