*The Cut Gazette — day 1303*

## 9th Breaks Riverside Door by Mistake  

**Terraces notices** – Two doors received unfamiliar company letters; no one knows the sender.  

**Harbor Bar** – The bar stayed packed and noisy for hours, with no incidents reported.  

**Riverside mishap** – A resident of the 9th mistakenly kicked a Riverside door, later realizing it was the wrong entry.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s umbrellas indoors and the town’s roofs a little leaky.
