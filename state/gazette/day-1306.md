*The Cut Gazette — day 1306*

## Cal Doyle costs Ade Osei  

**Favors with a price:** Both Cal Doyle and Rufus Bello each did a neighbour a favour that turned out to be a real cost to the recipient, Ade Osei and Blaise Mbeki respectively.  

**Evening chatter:** Residents were heard saying, “Your tab’s still open, Hal,” “You got any extra cash?” and “We need a plan, Gus,” hinting at lingering debts and pending schemes.  

**Quiet moments:** Cal Ferreira stared from a window, Iris Lindqvist checked the lock, and Sgt. Hal Brennan rolled over to listen to his comrades’ snores.

Also on the block: the night’s most dramatic event was a pillow‑adjusting prayer.
