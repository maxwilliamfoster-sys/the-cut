*The Cut Gazette — day 1309*

## Power Outage Darkens Block  

**Funeral on Delmar draws most residents.**  A service held today saw the majority of the block in attendance, though a few regulars were absent.  

**Six‑hour blackout leaves streets quiet.**  Lights went out early, and the block coped in darkness until power returned in the evening.  

**Neighbors discuss looming storm.**  Voices like Sami Serrano’s “We need to leave before the next storm hits” and Tomas Lindqvist’s “The rain makes everything feel lighter” floated through the night.  

Also on the block: the only thing staying awake was the coffee machine.
