*The Cut Gazette — day 1342*

## Block Plunged into Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The block lost electricity for six hours, leaving homes and shops in darkness and halting street activity.  

**Rufus Bello dispute:** Two men both named Rufus Bello were heard arguing loudly in the street, drawing the attention of passersby.  

**Quiet chatter:** Aside from a handful of muttered comments, residents reported little else happening during the blackout.  

Also on the block: even the streetlights seemed to need a coffee break.
