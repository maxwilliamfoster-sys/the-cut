*The Cut Gazette — day 1348*

## Errol Lindqvist and Chidi Okonkwo Clash  

**Street dispute draws a crowd.**  The two men argued loudly on the block’s main thoroughfare, forcing passers‑by to pause and listen.  

**Unfamiliar notices appear.**  Two doors in the Terraces now sport letters from a company no resident recognizes.  

**Whispers of watchfulness.**  Voices from nearby—Rey Vasquez, Lena Bright, and others—mutter about being monitored and the “clock ticking.”  

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than the gossip is the overcast sky.
