*The Cut Gazette — day 1350*

## Mayor’s Threats Whispered on Block  

**Police patrols** reported on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but no incidents were logged.  

**Residents** were heard murmuring about the mayor’s threats and a missing box, yet nothing materialized.  

**The night** found several locals—including Jonah Bello, June Amadi and Kwame Bright—fast asleep on their porches or in front of the TV.  

Also on the block: the coffee stays steady while the gossip drifts on.
