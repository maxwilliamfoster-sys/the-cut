*The Cut Gazette — day 1363*

## Police Deploy Extra Cruisers on Delmar  

**Debt notices arrive early.** Both Cal Kowal and Theo Bello were told the money they owe is due sooner than expected.  

**Ledger shortfall noted.** Dez Okonkwo reported another modest deficit in the count, just enough to raise eyebrows.  

**Extra patrols linger.** Two additional police cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, drawing quiet curiosity.  

Also on the block: the new ordinance is the hottest gossip, even though nobody can remember what it actually says.
