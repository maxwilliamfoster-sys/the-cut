*The Cut Gazette — day 1364*

## Law Targets Bello Family  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, a noticeable increase in patrol for the block.  

**Mysterious vehicle:** A car no one recognizes has remained parked on the same corner for three days, drawing curious glances from residents.  

**Local chatter:** Residents repeatedly voice concern that “the law is targeting the Bello family,” a sentiment echoed by several members of the Bello household.  

Also on the block: the unattended car has become the unofficial meeting spot for the neighborhood watch.
