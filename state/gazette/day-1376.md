*The Cut Gazette — day 1376*

## Jonah Bello Receives Unexpected Payout  

**Extra police cruisers** have been parked on Delmar since this morning, a routine presence noted by the block’s watch.  

**Tariq “Tee” Bello** was told the money his family is owed is needed sooner than originally agreed, adding pressure to the already‑surprising payout to his brother Jonah.  

**Local chatter** remains sparse; residents were heard making everyday remarks about sugar, gauges and a looming storm, but no further developments emerged.
