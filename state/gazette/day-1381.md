*The Cut Gazette — day 1381*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Mystery car stays put.** An unrecognised vehicle has been parked on the corner for three days, drawing puzzled looks from residents.  

**Power out, lights out.** The block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in darkness until service was restored.  

**Tense words linger.** Overheard snippets—from “You think you own this place?” to “You’re the only one who’s ever crossed me”—suggest lingering disputes among neighbors.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift barista finally learned the difference between “steady” and “drained.”
