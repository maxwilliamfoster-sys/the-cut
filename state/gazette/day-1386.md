*The Cut Gazette — day 1386*

## Boateng and Doyle Clash on Main  

**Street argument draws a crowd.** Fr. Emmanuel Boateng and Cal Doyle were heard shouting at each other, halting passers‑by for several minutes.  

**Police patrols keep watch.** Officers were noted in four neighborhoods (riverside 5, delmar 13, terraces 5, civic 5), but no incidents were reported.  

**Harbor Bar stays noisy but uneventful.** The bar remained full and loud for hours, with no trouble recorded.  

Also on the block: the locals keep debating bans and ordinances while wondering who’s really pulling the strings.
