*The Cut Gazette — day 1394*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents in darkness and prompting several to sleep on the streets.  

**Maren Adeyemi:** She was told the money she owes is now due earlier than agreed, adding tension to the already strained night.  

**Nia Nakamura:** Declares the new Bench Snow Gaze Regulation a “trap” designed to force people out of the block.  

Also on the block: Even the rain seemed to whisper, “Stay put.”
