*The Cut Gazette — day 1399*

## Power Outage Darkens Block for Six Hours  

**Extra police cruisers:** Two additional units have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, though no incident has been reported.  

**Mystery car:** A vehicle with no recognizable plates has remained parked on the corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity from passersby.  

**Residents in the dark:** Neighbors reported lingering in dim hallways, with Cassius Bello noting the lingering cash shortage and Ot O. Bello urging silence until lights return.  

Also on the block: the only bright spot was a whispered promise of drinks repayment.
