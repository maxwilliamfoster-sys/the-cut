*The Cut Gazette — day 1404*

## Rosa Kowal’s Favor Costs Cassius Bello  

**Funeral draws most of the block.** A service on Delmar saw the majority of residents in attendance, though a few regulars were absent.  

**Police add two cruisers to Delmar.** Officers have been stationed on the street since this morning, bringing the total visible presence to sixteen.  

**Tensions surface in whispered remarks.** Several locals were overheard exchanging veiled comments about “plans,” “lapses,” and “being stuck,” hinting at unease after the favor’s fallout.  

Also on the block: the neighborhood cats seem to have formed a nightly council—no one’s sure what they’re deciding.
