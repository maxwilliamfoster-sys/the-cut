*The Cut Gazette — day 1405*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block

**Harbor Bar stays noisy but peaceful.**  
The bar was full and loud for a few hours, but no incidents were reported.

**Community gathers for Delmar funeral.**  
Most residents attended the service; a handful who were expected did not appear.

**Quiet night for sleepers.**  
Gil Osei, June Lindqvist, Lena Petrakis, Nia Nakamura, Chidi Okonkwo and Kwame Bright were all found sleeping in various rooms around the block.

Also on the block: the police logged a dozen calls, but nothing worth a headline.
