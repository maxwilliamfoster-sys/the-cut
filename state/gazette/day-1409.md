*The Cut Gazette — day 1409*

## Rufus Bello Receives Unexpected Payout  

**Two extra cruisers** have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, drawing curious glances from passers‑by.  

**The Harbor Bar** stayed full and noisy for several hours; no incident was reported.  

**Marta Doyle** was heard asking, “Did you hear anything about my brother?” while **Sami Serrano** replied she only knows rumors.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift crew counted more sleepers than coffee cups.
