*The Cut Gazette — day 1411*

## Father Boateng’s Favor Costs Cal Osei  

**Two extra cruisers** have been parked on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s usual police presence.  

**Sgt. Hal Brennan** admits he pulled an old case file that should have been closed years ago and can’t locate it again.  

**Fr. Emmanuel Boateng** performed a favor for Cal Osei that “cost them something real,” according to witnesses.  

Also on the block: the radio‑talkers seem louder than the weather.
