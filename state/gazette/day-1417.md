*The Cut Gazette — day 1417*

## Ivy Calloway’s favor costs Jonah Bello  

**Police presence:** Officers patrolled Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic blocks as rain hammered the streets, but reported no incidents.  

**Harbor Bar:** The bar stayed packed and noisy for hours; nothing untoward was noted.  

**Community chatter:** Residents voiced concerns about bans on coffee, bedtime and public haircuts, and rumors of an “exit plan” while the rain drummed on windows.  

Also on the block: the rain may be loud, but the neighborhood gossip is louder.
