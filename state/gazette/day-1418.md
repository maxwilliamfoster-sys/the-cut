*The Cut Gazette — day 1418*

## Two Police Cruisers Parked on Delmar  

**Police presence:**  Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s already modest patrol count.  

**Mayor’s remarks:**  Several locals reported hearing the mayor’s shouted comments repeatedly throughout the day, prompting a few muttered jokes about his “vendetta.”  

**Quiet night:**  Most residents—Tomas and Ada among them—were asleep, their dreams punctuated only by distant noise.

Also on the block: the mayor’s volume may be high, but the neighborhood’s snores are louder.
