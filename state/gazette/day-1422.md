## Power Outage Darkens Block  

**Six‑hour blackout** left residents stumbling in the dark, with lights flickering back just after the rain eased.  

**Extra police cruisers** have been parked on Delmar since morning, a point of quiet curiosity among locals.  

**Neighbors chat debts and law** – June reminds Farrah of a ledger balance, while Lena and Nia discuss a pending law that could free up space for the community.  

Also on the block: the rain’s the only thing that’s staying on schedule.
