*The Cut Gazette — day 1429*

## Rufus Bello Costs Blaise Mbeki  

**Street argument:** Tariq “Tee” Bello and Marta Doyle shouted at each other on the sidewalk, drawing a brief crowd despite the rain.  

**Unclear favor:** Rufus Bello helped Blaise Mbeki in a way that now “cost them something real,” according to witnesses.  

**Side notes:** Gil Bright noted a delivery boy left without signing a damaged freight slip, and Errol Brannon mentioned a drawer still short at closing.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s socks soggy but the gossip stayed dry.
