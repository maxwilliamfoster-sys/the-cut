*The Cut Gazette — day 1438*

## Inspectors Sweep Delmar Street  

**Harbor Bar stays noisy** – The bar was packed and loud for several hours, but no incidents were reported.  

**City inspectors patrol** – Teams with clipboards moved methodically down Delmar, checking compliance at local businesses.  

**Block’s night shift** – Several residents—Elias Reyes, Nadia Vasquez, Simone Adeyemi, Cal Serrano, Wes Doyle—were observed asleep, while Booker Lyle counted a lone float.  

Also on the block: the only drama heard was a chorus of “I’ll prove myself” and “I’m waiting for a call that never comes.”
