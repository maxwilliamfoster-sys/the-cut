*The Cut Gazette — day 1451*

## Harbor Bar Remains Rowdy, No Trouble  

**Evening crowd stays loud.**  The Harbor Bar was packed and noisy for several hours, but police reports show no incidents.  

**Patrons chat about debts and cuts.**  Tomas Ferreira asked for quiet, while Gil Brannon asked for clippers and Kwame Bright bragged about a bigger haircut tomorrow.  

**A rumor of fire circulates.**  June Lindqvist mentioned hearing about a fire at the bar, but no confirmation followed.  

Also on the block: the ledger‑keeping Dot Feeney still avoids eye contact.
