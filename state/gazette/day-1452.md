*The Cut Gazette — day 1452*

## Rufus Bello’s Favor Costs Rosa

**Dez Okonkwo** discovered the cash register was short again, though only by a little.  

**Terraces notices** appeared on two doors, printed on stationery from an unfamiliar company.  

**Six‑hour blackout** left the block in darkness, prompting residents to keep watch.

Also on the block: the power’s back, but the radios are still tuning in to static.
