*The Cut Gazette — day 1453*

## Odette Lindqvist and Rufus Bello argue  

**Street scene:** Their loud dispute forced several passers‑by to pause, though no police were called.  

**Quiet corners:** Iris Lindqvist waited for water at Delmar Cottages while Sami Doyle, Milo Adeyemi and others were dozing in front of the TV.  

**Family night:** Tiny and Femi Adeyemi, plus the Vasquez household, spent the evening sleeping together.  

Also on the block: the fog kept the river silent, but the gossip was thinner.
