*The Cut Gazette — day 1467*

## Mysterious Car Stalls Corner for Days  

**Bar buzzes:** The Harbor Bar stayed packed and loud for hours, but no incidents were reported.  

**Unclaimed vehicle:** A car with no identifiable owner has been parked on the same corner for three straight days, drawing casual curiosity but no police action.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents were heard asking about change, signing papers, and debating whether the parked car “means something,” while several locals napped nearby.  

Also on the block: the mayor’s threats are the only thing people claim to have actually heard.
