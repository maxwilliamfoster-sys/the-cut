*The Cut Gazette — day 1470*

## Mysterious Car Stalls on Corner  

**Police patrols:** Officers from Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic precincts have kept a watchful eye on the unclaimed vehicle since it appeared three days ago.  

**Neighbors watch:** Kwame Doyle and other residents have been spotted lingering nearby, listening to the rain while the car remains untouched.  

**No word yet:** With no owner coming forward and nothing else reported, the block remains in quiet suspense.  

Also on the block: the rain may finally wash the mystery away.
