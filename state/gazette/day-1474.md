*The Cut Gazette — day 1474*

## Two Extra Cruisers Park on Delmar  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s modest police tally.  

**Local chatter:** Residents were overheard exchanging terse remarks—Rey Vasquez challenged “Cal,” while Dot Feeney reminded a sergeant that “your tab’s up.”  

**Quiet corners:** Sgt. Hal Brennan lingered motionless, Cal Serrano clutched his blanket tighter, and Lena Bright stared at the ceiling, all while the block remained unusually still.  

Also on the block: the wind finally stopped arguing with the streetlights.
