*The Cut Gazette — day 1475*

## Six‑Hour Power Outage Hits Block  

**Power loss:** The block was plunged into darkness for six hours, leaving homes and businesses without electricity.  

**Harbor Bar stays lively:** The Harbor Bar remained full and noisy throughout the blackout, with no complaints heard.  

**Residents chat:** Overheard snippets mention missing kitchen keys, new signage, and post‑lunch paperwork while the lights were out.  

Also on the block: Someone finally found a kitchen key—still missing.
