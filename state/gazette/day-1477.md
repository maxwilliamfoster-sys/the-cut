*The Cut Gazette — day 1477*

## Block Plunged Into Six‑Hour Darkness  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents in the dark and halting routine activities.  

**Police chatter:** A resident reported that Errol Brannon was seen speaking with police, a detail relayed to Marta Petrakis during the outage.  

**Local concerns:** Dez Okonkwo voiced suspicion that the court may be targeting the block to force residents out.  

Also on the block: the coffee shop finally got its Wi‑Fi back just in time for the morning rush.
