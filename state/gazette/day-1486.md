*The Cut Gazette — day 1486*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents in darkness and halting routine activities.  

**Mystery notices:** Two doors on the Terraces displayed letters from an unfamiliar company, prompting curiosity but no further explanation.  

**Shortage discovered:** Elias Reyes reported the cash count was again slightly low, though the discrepancy was minor.  

Also on the block: Even the night owls stayed up, claiming the silence was louder than usual.
