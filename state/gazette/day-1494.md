*The Cut Gazette — day 1494*

## Inspectors Sweep Delmar Street  

**City inspectors** are methodically patrolling Delmar today, clipboards in hand, checking compliance as rain pounds the block.  

**Police activity** is noted at several corners—five officers each at Riverside, Terraces, and Civic, and eleven at Delmar—though no incidents were reported.  

**Residents** were heard discussing school grades, diner management, and record‑keeping, hinting at routine community concerns amid the downpour.  

Also on the block: the rain may wash away more than just the streets, but not the gossip.
