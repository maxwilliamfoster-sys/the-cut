*The Cut Gazette — day 1501*

## Harbor Bar Packs House, No Incidents

**Full house, loud music:** The Harbor Bar stayed packed and noisy for several hours, but nothing untoward was reported.  

**Quiet whispers:** Patrons were heard muttering about bitter brews, overcharging, and upcoming inspections, but no further details emerged.  

**Sleepy scene:** Several locals were spotted sleeping nearby, from Cassius Bello to Farrah Mbeki, with eyes half‑closed and tools at hand.  

Also on the block: the night was as uneventful as a Tuesday tax audit.
