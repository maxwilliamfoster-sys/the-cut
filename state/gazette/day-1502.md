*The Cut Gazette — day 1502*

## Sgt. Brennan revives old police file  

**File resurfaced.** Sgt. Hal Brennan pulled a long‑closed case file and, after three decades, couldn’t reseal it, prompting quiet speculation among the precinct.  

**Mystery car.** An unmarked vehicle has sat at the corner for three days, drawing puzzled looks from residents who can’t identify its owner.  

**Wes Doyle mentioned.** A passerby warned Cal Mbeki that Wes Doyle had been seen chatting with police, a detail that has yet to surface in any official report.  

Also on the block: the rain may be falling, but the only thing really soaking the street is the town’s collective curiosity.
