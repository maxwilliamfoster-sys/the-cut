*The Cut Gazette — day 1510*

## Detective Ruiz Retrieves Long‑Closed File  

**Ruiz’s old case resurfaces.** The detective pulled a file that should have been sealed years ago and was unable to replace it, prompting a quiet stir among the precinct.  

**Extra patrols on Delmar.** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, though no incidents have been reported.  

Also on the block: the fog is thick enough to make a street‑lamp look like a lighthouse.
