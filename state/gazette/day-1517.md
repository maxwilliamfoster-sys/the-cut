*The Cut Gazette — day 1517*

## Dot Feeney Spotted Talking to Police  

**Police patrols** reported on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but no incidents were recorded.  

**Nia Mbeki** was told by an unnamed source that Dot Feeney was seen conversing with officers, prompting quiet speculation among neighbors.  

**Rain‑bound residents** such as Iris Petrakis, Iris Nakamura, Marta Doyle and Milo Adeyemi spent the evening awake or asleep to the steady downpour.  

Also on the block: the mayor’s note remains as elusive as the fog Maren Adeyemi mentioned.
