*The Cut Gazette — day 1520*

## Ambulance Removes Unnamed Patient from Terraces  

**Police patrols:** Officers logged modest activity today—five each on Riverside, Delmar, and Civic, and thirteen on the Terraces.  

**Morning emergency:** An ambulance arrived before dawn and took an unidentified person away from the Terraces; no further details were given.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Tomas and Vic Lindqvist were heard murmuring about sleep and peace, while Rey Vasquez warned “Don’t think this is over, Cal.”  

Also on the block: the night was so quiet even the water seemed to be listening.
