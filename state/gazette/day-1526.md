*The Cut Gazette — day 1526*

## Ambulance Whisks Person From Terraces  

**Mysterious car:** An unmarked vehicle has remained parked on the corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity from residents.  

**Quiet emergency:** Early before dawn an ambulance removed an unnamed individual from the Terraces; no details were given by witnesses.  

**Block chatter:** Voices on the block ranged from Simone Adeyemi’s “We keep the doors closed, not the hearts” to Cal Serrano’s warning, “You keep cutting cars, I’ll cut you.”  

Also on the block: the rain keeps everyone indoors, but the gossip keeps flowing.
