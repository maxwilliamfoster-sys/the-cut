*The Cut Gazette — day 1528*

## Block Residents Voice Concerns Over Issues  

**No incidents reported.** The police logged routine patrols on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but nothing noteworthy occurred.  

**Overheard remarks.** A chorus of local voices floated through the streets—from Booker Lyle’s call to lock down the pawn shop, to Ms. Raman’s praise of Nadia’s scholarship, and Cassius Adeyemi’s restless insomnia.  

**Quiet streets.** Aside from a solitary sighting of Cassius Adeyemi lying awake, the block remained largely uneventful.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone indoors, and the only drama was a soggy umbrella.
