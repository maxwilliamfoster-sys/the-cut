*The Cut Gazette — day 1536*

## Two Police Cruisers Added to Delmar  

**Patrol boost:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, raising the precinct’s visible presence amid the heavy rain.  

**Quiet night:** Several locals—including Cal Kowal, Iris Petrakis, and Kwame Lindv​ist—were noted asleep or dozing as the storm drummed on.  

**Community chatter:** Few remarks were heard beyond the weather; residents simply noted the lights left on and the lingering thunder.  

Also on the block: the rain may be washing away more than just puddles, but the police are still here.
