*The Cut Gazette — day 1547*

## Elias Reyes Finds Inventory Short Again  

**Funeral on Delmar draws most of the block.** A handful of expected attendees were absent, though the ceremony proceeded without incident.  

**Elias Reyes reports a modest shortfall in the count.** The discrepancy is small but noticeable, prompting a quick recount.  

**Residents whisper about a possible water leak.** Kwame Osei and others note odd dampness, while Lena Bright wonders if the water will “wash us clean.”  

Also on the block: the fog makes everyone’s shoes a little soggier than usual.
