*The Cut Gazette — day 1550*

## Outage Sends Gil Bright to Clinic  

**Blackout:** The block was without electricity for six hours, leaving shops and homes in darkness.  

**Gil Bright:** After the lights went out, Bright visited the clinic; the paperwork and his injury description don’t match.  

**Neighbors:** Rufus Bello pledged to keep his door shut, while Rey Vasquez urged keeping the shop running despite the loss of power.  

Also on the block: the only thing that stayed lit was the neighborhood gossip.
