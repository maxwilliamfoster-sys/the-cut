*The Cut Gazette — day 1553*

## Ambulance Removes Unknown Patient from Terraces  

**Terraces emergency** – An ambulance escorted an unnamed person off the Terraces just before sunrise; no further details were given.  

**Harbor Bar** – The bar stayed packed and noisy for several hours, but nothing untoward was reported.  

**Late‑night sleepers** – Several residents, including Errol Amadi and Sgt. Hal Brennan, were seen sleeping in various homes and cottages around the block.  

Also on the block: the night‑owl crowd finally got a quiet moment to hear the bar’s jukebox.
