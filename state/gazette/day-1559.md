*The Cut Gazette — day 1559*

## Inspectors Sweep Delmar With Clipboards  

**Police reports:** 14 officers stationed on Delmar, 25 on the riverside, 5 each on Terraces and Civic.  

**Local chatter:** Farrah Osei praised the flow of business, while June Petrakis confirmed the shipment code remains “42‑B,” unchanged from last week.  

**Oddities:** Dez Okonkwo declared he feels “watched” yet refuses ownership, and several residents—including Iris Nakamura—were noted awake through the foggy night.  

Also on the block: the city’s “no unattended scissors” ban may soon be as unnoticed as the sleeping crowd.
