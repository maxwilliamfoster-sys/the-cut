*The Cut Gazette — day 1570*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power loss stalls the block.**  Six hours of darkness left shops and homes without electricity, prompting improvised light and whispered complaints.  

**Mystery notices appear in the Terraces.**  Two doors now display letters from an unfamiliar company, raising more questions than answers.  

**Neighbors scramble for a fix.**  Nadia Vasquez suggested checking the basement for the fuse, while Lena Ferreira asked if anyone had heard from the generator.  

Also on the block: the only thing staying bright was Gil Bright’s tally at the till.
