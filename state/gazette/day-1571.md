## June Mbeki Spotted Talking to Police  

**Car mystery:** A nameless vehicle has been idling on the corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity from passers‑by.  

**Quiet night:** An ambulance whisked an unnamed person away from the Terraces before dawn; no further details were offered.  

**Block gossip:** With little else to report, residents simply watch the streetlights and listen to distant sirens.  

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than the rumors is the parking meter’s unpaid minutes.
