*The Cut Gazette — day 1574*

## Two patients taken from Terraces pre‑dawn  

**Ambulance runs:** Two separate calls pulled unidentified residents from the Terraces just before sunrise; no names were released.  

**Police presence:** Officers logged 19 calls on the Terraces, the highest concentration on the block today, while Riverside, Delmar, and Civic saw modest activity.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents were heard exchanging cryptic remarks about “books,” “ledgers,” and “moving before they notice,” hinting at unfinished business on the block.  

Also on the block: the local garage’s car‑tuning session doubled as the block’s most audible soundtrack.
