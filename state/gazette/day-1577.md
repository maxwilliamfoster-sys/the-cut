*The Cut Gazette — day 1577*

## June Mbeki and Tomas Lindqvist Clash  

**Street argument:** June Mbeki and Tomas Lindqvist stopped passers‑by with a loud dispute that drew onlookers to the curb.  

**Power outage:** A six‑hour blackout left the block in darkness, halting most activity until service was restored.  

**Quiet corners:** With most residents sleeping, the block stayed unusually still despite the commotion.  

Also on the block: the night shift coffee shop finally ran out of beans.
