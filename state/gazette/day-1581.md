*The Cut Gazette — day 1581*

## Unknown Company Posts Notices on Terraces  

**Notice mystery** – Two doors in the Terraces were suddenly covered with notices bearing the letterhead of a firm no one on the block recognizes.  

**Evening chatter** – Residents were overheard swapping brief remarks about walls, tomorrow’s plans and staying “low,” hinting at a shared, low‑key project.  

**Quiet observation** – A handful of locals, from Farrah Mbeki glued to the TV to Kwame Osei listening to the radio, watched the scene unfold without comment.  

Also on the block: the night’s drama was louder than the street’s usual hum, but still mostly “just another night.”
