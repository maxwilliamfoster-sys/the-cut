*The Cut Gazette — day 1582*

## Extra Police Cruisers Park on Delmar  

**New fines discussed.**  Residents Dev Amadi and Cal Serrano were heard talking about recently issued fines, while Milo Bello asked for a demonstration.  

**Watch tightened.**  Sgt. Hal Brennan said the department needs to “tighten watches” as extra cruisers remain on Delmar.  

**Quiet concerns.**  Dez Okonkwo voiced a feeling of being watched, and Theo Bello said he hopes to make his mother proud.

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than the cruisers is the morning coffee line.
