*The Cut Gazette — day 1599*

## Inspectors Sweep Delmar Street  

**Inspectors on the move** – City officials were seen marching down Delmar with clipboards, checking each storefront in turn.  

**Quiet tensions** – Voices on the block hinted at lingering debts and mistrust: Iris Nakamura reminded Dot of a owed favor, while Errol Serrano warned Rey to keep his distance.  

**Neighborhood watch** – Residents kept to themselves: Odette Amadi scrolled lowly through orders, and Vic Lindqvist sat alone reviewing old files.  

Also on the block: the rain may be the only thing moving faster than the gossip.
