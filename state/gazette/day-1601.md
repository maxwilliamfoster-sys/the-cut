*The Cut Gazette — day 1601*

## Simone Adeyemi Spotted Talking to Police  

**Police activity:** 37 officers were noted on patrol across Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic blocks, the highest concentration this month.  

**Local chatter:** Residents were overheard discussing a “back‑room” and urging a “lock‑down tonight,” while several locals—June Lindqvist, Blaise Osei, Gil Bright and others—were seen sleeping on the streets.  

**Community concerns:** Questions about a missing person and mounting debts were raised, but no further details were offered.  

Also on the block: the rain may be worsening, but the night‑shift coffee shop stays open.
