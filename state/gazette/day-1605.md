*The Cut Gazette — day 1605*

## Ivy Calloway, Maren Adeyemi clash  

**Street argument draws a crowd.**  The two women were heard shouting loudly enough to halt passersby on the rain‑slick block.  

**Police presence noted.**  Officers were stationed at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic (5‑24‑9‑5) as the downpour continued, though no arrests were reported.  

**Neighbors voice unease.**  Cassius Reyes and others muttered about safety and secrecy, while several residents reiterated personal grievances about recent bans and “being watched.”  

Also on the block: the rain keeps the lights on longer than the gossip.
