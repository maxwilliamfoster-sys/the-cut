*The Cut Gazette — day 1607*

## Milo Bello Told June Met Police  

**Police presence modest.** Officers logged a handful of calls today—five at Riverside, sixteen on Delmar, five each at Terraces and Civic.  

**Neighborhood quiet.** Gil, Marta and Farrah Osei were found asleep; Dev Amadi was awake, while Blaise Brannon fetched a glass of water and Farrah Serrano dozed in front of the TV.  

Also on the block: the only gossip was a whisper that June Petrakis was chatting with the police.
