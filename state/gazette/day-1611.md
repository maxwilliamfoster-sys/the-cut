*The Cut Gazette — day 1611*

## Iris Lindqvist Seen Talking to Police  

**Police attention:** Officers patrolled Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic as rain hammered the block.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents exchanged brief remarks about pallets, rent, and a vague lead, but “little” was said about the police visit.  

**Quiet corners:** Several locals—Dev Brannon, Farrah Mbeki, Iris Petrakis, June Lindqvist, Marta Ferreira, and Elias Reyes—were noted “lying still” while the storm drummed their windows.  

Also on the block: the rain may be falling faster than the gossip.
