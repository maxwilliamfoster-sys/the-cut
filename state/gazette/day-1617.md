*The Cut Gazette — day 1617*

## Errol Amadi’s Favor Costs Ade Osei  

**Power outage** – A six‑hour blackout left the block in darkness, prompting whispered complaints and a few flashlight requests.  

**Amadi’s favor** – Errol Amadi helped Ade Osei with a task that turned out to cost both of them something real, though no details were given.  

**Block chatter** – Residents muttered about “charging for ambience” and wondered whether the cut‑off was a “good break.”  

Also on the block: even the sofa‑cat seemed less startled than the neighbors.
