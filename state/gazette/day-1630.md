*The Cut Gazette — day 1630*

## Block Plunged Into Six‑Hour Darkness  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, silencing the Harbor Bar’s music and leaving residents stumbling in the dark.  

**Neighborhood mood:** As lights flickered back, locals voiced their thoughts—Gil Bright feels watched, Kwame Doyle says he’s being accused, Rufus Bello notes he’s finally taken seriously, among other personal reckonings.  

Also on the block: even the streetlights seemed to need a coffee break.
