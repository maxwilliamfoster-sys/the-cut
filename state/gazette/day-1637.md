*The Cut Gazette — day 1637*

## Tomas Amadi's Favor Costs Odette  

**Power outage stalls block.** The entire neighborhood was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents to navigate darkness and a flickering TV.  

**Amadi’s favor has a price.** Tomas Amadi helped Odette Lindqvist, an act that “cost them something real,” according to the only comment recorded.  

**Quiet night, quiet talk.** Few voices were heard beyond the outage, with residents noting the lights went out and the usual hum of conversation stayed low.  

Also on the block: the plants got watered, but the floor stayed damp.
