*The Cut Gazette — day 1645*

## Sami Nakamura’s Favor Costs Chinedu  

**Quiet dispute:** Wes Doyle and Tomas Mbeki shouted in the street, drawing a brief crowd that soon dispersed as rain fell.  

**Bold claim:** Dez Okonkwo announced he “is owed by everyone and must be respected,” a statement met with a few raised eyebrows.  

**Neighborhood note:** Several residents—Etta Adeyemi, Etta Bello, and Theo Bello—spoke of trying to earn their mothers’ approval, echoing a common block‑wide ambition.  

Also on the block: the rain may be heavy, but the gossip’s still lighter than a puddle.
