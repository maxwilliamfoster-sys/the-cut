*The Cut Gazette — day 1653*

## Jonah Bello confronts block leadership  

**Ambulance rushes from the Terraces** before dawn, whisking an unidentified person away.  

**Heated words echo in the rain** – Jonah Bello warns “You think you can run the block? We’re just family,” while Tariq “Tee” Bello muses about letting “her” run things, and Iris Nakamura complains about unpaid debts.  

**The night stays quiet** as Rey Vasquez rests at home, Iris Lindqvist sleeps in the cottages, and the Adeyemi family drifts off in separate rooms.  

Also on the block: the rain keeps everyone indoors, so the only drama is the echo of old arguments.
