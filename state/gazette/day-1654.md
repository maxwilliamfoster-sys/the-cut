*The Cut Gazette — day 1654*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and the streetlights in darkness until service was restored.  

**Mystery car:** A vehicle with no known owner has remained parked on the corner for three days, drawing curious glances from residents.  

**Quiet concerns:** Iris Amadi warned that a new ordinance could jeopardize her shift, while Dez Okonkwo mused that the block might hold clues to his brother’s disappearance.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone indoors, perfect for humming and laundry‑folding.
