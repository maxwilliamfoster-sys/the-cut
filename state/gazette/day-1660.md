*The Cut Gazette — day 1660*

## Mayor's Threats Echo Across Block  

**Police patrols** logged 28 officers on the block, with the highest presence at Riverside (11).  

**Neighborhood chatter** centered on the mayor’s ominous remarks, echoed in comments from Tariq “Tee” Bello and Nadia Vasquez.  

**Heat‑driven slumber** kept many residents—Chidi Okonkwo, Sami Osei, Tomas Mbeki, Elias Reyes, and Iris Lindqvist—dozing or staring at ceilings through the swelter.  

Also on the block: the only thing hotter than the weather is the gossip about unpaid paint jobs.
