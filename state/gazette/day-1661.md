*The Cut Gazette — day 1661*

## 9th Breaks Riverside Door  

**Police report:** Officers logged 32 calls on Riverside after the 9th mistakenly kicked a door, while Delmar, Terraces and Civic saw only a handful of reports.  

**Neighbors chat:** Residents were heard discussing rent, mortgage pooling, and the cannery schedule, but little else was said about the incident.  

**Street activity:** Errol Brannon signed for a delivery, Gil Bright stocked canned goods, and Iris Amadi stapled reports as rain kept most of the block indoors.  

Also on the block: the rain may have softened the door’s bite, but not the neighborhood’s gossip.
