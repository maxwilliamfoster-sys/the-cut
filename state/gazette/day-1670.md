*The Cut Gazette — day 1670*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block Attendance  

**Funeral draws crowd.** Most residents turned out for the service on Delmar, though a handful of expected faces were absent.  

**Tip to June Ferreira.** A passerby informed her that Rosa Reyes has been seen in conversation with police.  

**Quiet night observed.** June Kowal, Kwame Nakamura, Nia and Cal Doyle, Dev Brannon and Rey Vasquez were each spotted sleeping quietly around the block.  

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the overcast sky was the collective snore.
