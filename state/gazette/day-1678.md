*The Cut Gazette — day 1678*

## Rosa Reyes Receives Unexpected Payout  

**Ambulance lifts unknown patient from the Terraces before dawn.** No name was released, and the block woke to the quiet hum of a night‑time rescue.  

**Two extra police cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning.** Officers have not commented on their purpose, but the presence is noted by several residents.  

**Unfamiliar company notices appear on two Terraces doors.** Neighbors, including Tiny Reyes and June Lindqvist, suspect a landlord‑related flyer, though details remain unclear.  

Also on the block: the only drama today was a stray hair‑clip concert by Kwame Bright.
