*The Cut Gazette — day 1690*

## Extra Police Cruisers Parked on Delmar  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, joining the routine patrols logged for the block.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents were heard commenting on the traffic jam on Delmar, the new fare schedule, and the ever‑present rain, while a few kept to themselves.  

**On the streets:** Blaise Osei trimmed his hair while watching Cal, and Gil Bright counted pennies glancing out the window as the storm intensified.  

Also on the block: the rain may be heavy, but the gossip is still light.
