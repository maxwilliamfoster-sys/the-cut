*The Cut Gazette — day 1705*

## Ambulance Removes Patient from Terraces  

**Lena Ferreira demands payment today.** She pressed Tomas Lindqvist for the money they owe, insisting the deadline be moved up.  

**Harbor Bar stays lively.** The bar was full and noisy for several hours, but no incidents were reported.  

**Morning quiet on the block.** Aside from the ambulance run, residents were heard adjusting blankets, snorting in sleep, and noting bus delays—nothing else noteworthy.  

Also on the block: the rain may be louder than anyone’s sermons, but it’s still dry out here.
