*The Cut Gazette — day 1708*

## Nia Mbeki Pressed for Early Payment  

**Funeral draws most of the block.**  A service on Delmar saw heavy attendance, though a few expected faces were absent.  

**Two police cruisers linger on Delmar.**  Officers have been stationed there since early morning, likely related to the funeral crowd.  

**Debt reminder reaches Nia Mbeki.**  A creditor contacted her today, asking that the owed sum be paid sooner than originally arranged.  

Also on the block: the rain keeps everyone humming the same tune.
