*The Cut Gazette — day 1712*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Power loss:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in darkness and prompting residents to wait it out together.  

**Police presence:** Five officers patrolled Riverside, Terraces, and Civic, while thirty‑three cruisers monitored Delmar, where two extra units have been stationed since morning.  

**Neighbors’ reactions:** Otis and Rufus Bello checked on each other, Iris Nakamura commented on the cold water, and Ms. Raman promised to resume scholarship talks once the lights returned.  

Also on the block: the rain kept the streets slick enough that even a nail‑tapping session sounded like a symphony.
