*The Cut Gazette — day 1718*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power cut:** The whole block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in the dark until service was restored this afternoon.  

**Unexpected payout:** Tomas Lindqvist received a payment larger than anticipated, a situation the paper notes “its own problem.”  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the routine patrols logged across the neighborhood.  

Also on the block: even the rain can’t wash away the habit of whispering about “the remote.”
