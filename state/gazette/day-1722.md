*The Cut Gazette — day 1722*

## Block Quiet as Rain Falls  

**Police patrols noted** modest activity at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but no incidents were reported.  

**Residents were heard** making low‑key remarks—Errol Doyle urged “stay quiet,” Farrah Lindqvist claimed she’ll run “this room,” and Kwame Bright asked how to “make a difference.”  

**No notable sightings** were reported around the block today.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s gossip as dry as the streets.
