*The Cut Gazette — day 1723*

## Inspectors Sweep Delmar Street  

**Police reports:** 25 calls on Delmar, plus five each on Riverside, Terraces and Civic.  

**Community voices:** Residents were heard arguing over space, scholarships and lingering fines as inspectors moved down the block.  

**Local concerns:** Dez Okonkwo says he’ll stay “quiet and unseen,” while Lena Ferreira searches for her brother hidden “in the bench ban.”  

Also on the block: the rain kept the gossip soggy but the clipboard clacking dry.
