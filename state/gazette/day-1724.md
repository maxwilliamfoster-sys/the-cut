## Two Patrol Cars Arrive on Delmar  

**Unidentified car** has been sitting on the corner for three days, drawing curious glances from passersby.

**Community chatter** is quiet; residents report hearing little beyond routine murmurs.  

Also on the block: the candle‑lit wall seems to be the hottest real‑estate in town.
