*The Cut Gazette — day 1740*

## Two Extra Police Cruisers Patrol Delmar  

**Harbor Bar** stays packed and noisy, but no incidents were reported.  

**Terraces notices** appeared on two doors, bearing the letterhead of an unfamiliar company.  

**Block residents**—Cassius Adeyemi, Cassius Bello, Lena Petrakis, Rosa Amadi and Sami Serrano—voice a shared sense of being watched and restrained.  

Also on the block: the cold snap has everyone huddling closer to the bar’s jukebox.
