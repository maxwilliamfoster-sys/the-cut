*The Cut Gazette — day 1745*

## Etta Bello Feels Seen by Adults  

**Etta Bello** announced she finally feels noticed by grown‑ups and believes her mother will be proud.  

**Kwame and Sami Doyle** each declared they want to fade away – Kwame wants no eyes on him, Sami feels invisible.  

**Several residents** were spotted sleeping on the sidewalks, among them Tomas and Vic Lindqvist, Tomas Amadi, Marta Osei and Odette Amadi.  

Also on the block: The first snow fell quietly, and even the alley cat seemed to go unnoticed.
