*The Cut Gazette — day 1761*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Power loss:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in darkness until service was restored.  

**Tensions in the night:** Residents exchanged terse remarks—Elias Reyes and Cassius Reyes blamed each other for an unnamed dispute, while Sgt. Hal Brennan promised to “settle this later” with Dot Feeney.  

**Police activity:** Officers were noted at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces, and Civic, but no incidents were reported.  

Also on the block: the night was “cold,” according to Ada Bello, who seemed relieved the lights were finally back.
