*The Cut Gazette — day 1764*

## Lena Mbeki’s Favor Costs Priya Raman  

**Favor with a price:** Lena Mbeki helped Ms. Priya Raman, but the aid left the two of them owing something tangible, according to the brief report.  

**Short‑changed tally:** Cassius Reyes noted the ledger is again a little low; the shortfall is small but enough to raise eyebrows among the staff.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s heads down, but the bells still rang for the night shift.
