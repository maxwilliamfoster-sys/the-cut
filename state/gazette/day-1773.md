*The Cut Gazette — day 1773*

## Gil Bright and Marta Ferreira Clash  

**Street argument:**  Gil Bright and Marta Ferreira were heard shouting at each other, drawing a small crowd on the corner.  

**Second dispute:**  Around the same time Clemmie Bello and Iris Lindqvist exchanged loud words, likewise pausing passers‑by.  

**Other notes:**  City inspectors continued their clipboard tour down Delmar, and an unidentified car has remained parked on the corner for three days.  

Also on the block:  fog makes the parked car look even more mysterious.
