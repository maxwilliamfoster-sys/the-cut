*The Cut Gazette — day 1782*

## Milo Adeyemi’s Favor Costs Errol Doyle  

**Ambulance** – Early this morning a paramedic crew lifted an unnamed patient from the Terraces and sped off before sunrise.  

**City inspectors** – Teams with clipboards were observed walking down Delmar, checking compliance at several storefronts.  

**Quiet night** – Aside from the above, residents reported little else of note on the block.  

Also on the block: the fog kept the waterfront’s usual joggers at home.
