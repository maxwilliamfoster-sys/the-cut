*The Cut Gazette — day 1789*

## Power Outage Darkens Block for Six Hours  

**Harbor Bar stays packed.**  The noisy crowd lingered unchanged until the lights flickered out, leaving patrons in the dark for six hours.  

**Cassius Reyes notes a small cash shortfall.**  He reports the register is again missing a modest amount, enough to raise a comment but not halt business.  

**Neighbors chat in the blackout.**  Voices on the street mention traffic jams, waiting on generators, and a shared urge to “keep heads down” until power returns.  

*Also on the block: the night‑time clock seems to run slower when the lights are out.*
