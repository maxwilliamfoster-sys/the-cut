*The Cut Gazette — day 1792*

## Cal Ferreira and Amara Bello Clash  

**Street argument draws crowd.**  Ferreira and Bello were heard shouting in the middle of the block, forcing passersby to stop and listen.  

**Officer Kohl mishandles old case file.**  While on patrol, he retrieved a long‑closed file and was unable to replace it, prompting a brief pause in his duties.  

**Nia Nakamura mentioned to Cal Doyle.**  A passerby told Doyle that Nakamura had recently been seen speaking with police, though no further details were given.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s umbrellas up, but the gossip stayed low.
