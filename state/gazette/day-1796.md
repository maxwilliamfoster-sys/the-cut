*The Cut Gazette — day 1796*

## Nadia Vasquez’s favor costs Ade  

**Favor with a price:** Nadia Vasquez helped Ade Brannon, a deed that left them both owing something tangible.  

**Quiet night:** Kwame Doyle, Cassius Bello (twice noted), and Etta Adeyemi were all seen asleep, while Errol Brannon was up for a glass of water and Femi Adeyemi dozed in front of the TV.  

**No gossip:** Nothing else noteworthy was heard or said on the block today.  

*Also on the block: the night was as uneventful as a library after hours.*
