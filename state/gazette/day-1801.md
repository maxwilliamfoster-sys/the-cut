*The Cut Gazette — day 1801*

## 9th Kicks Wrong Door on Riverside  

**Reyes brothers flag inventory shortfall.**  
Elias and Malik Reyes each report the count is “short again,” enough to raise concern but not a major discrepancy.

**Police patrols increase.**  
Units are stationed at Riverside (29), Delmar (12), Terraces (5) and Civic (5) as rain continues to hammer the block.

**Neighbors note quiet tension.**  
Kwame Doyle sighs at the rain while others hunker down; the atmosphere feels “too loud” for some and “too quiet” for others.

Also on the block: the rain’s drumming seems to keep everyone’s doors shut—except the 9th’s.
