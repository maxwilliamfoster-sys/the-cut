*The Cut Gazette — day 1803*

## Booker Lyle and Odette Mbeki Clash  

**Argument stalls traffic.** The two were heard shouting in the street, forcing passers‑by to pause while they debated.  

**Blaise Mbeki receives urgent payment notice.** A messenger told him the money owed is now due ahead of schedule.  

**Block is unusually quiet.** Aside from a few murmurs about inquiries and “moving the kids,” little else was heard on the block today.  

Also on the block: even the streetlights seemed to sigh at the drama.
