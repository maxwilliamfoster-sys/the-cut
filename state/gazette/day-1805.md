*The Cut Gazette — day 1805*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block  

**Attendance:** Most residents turned out for the Delmar service, though a handful of expected mourners stayed away.  

**Rumors:** Conversations around the block touched on everything from a “scissors ban” to a collapsed harbor‑bar roof, but no concrete news emerged.  

**Side‑note:** Tariq “Tee” Bello was seen tapping his fingers at the table while others went about their chores.  

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the sink’s splash was the silence about the mayor’s inquiry.
