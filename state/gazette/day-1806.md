*The Cut Gazette — day 1806*

## 9th Breaks Riverside Door in Mishap  

**Harbor Bar stays noisy but uneventful.**  Patrons filled the venue for hours; no incidents were reported.  

**Door‑kick causes a brief stir.**  The 9th mistakenly kicked the wrong door on Riverside, drawing a quick check from police who logged five incidents there.  

**Neighborhood chatter is quiet.**  Aside from the door incident, residents were heard murmuring about bills and minor grievances, but no further activity was noted.  

Also on the block: rain kept everyone’s roofs humming a steady beat.
