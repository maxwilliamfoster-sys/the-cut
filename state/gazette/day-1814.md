*The Cut Gazette — day 1814*

## Ade Osei Told Debt Due Early  

**Harbor Bar stays lively.** The venue was packed and noisy for several hours, but no incidents were reported.

Also on the block: the night owls—Cassius and Kwame—proved the bar’s lull isn’t a lull at all.
