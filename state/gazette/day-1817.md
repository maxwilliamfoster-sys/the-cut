*The Cut Gazette — day 1817*

## Gil Osei’s Favor Costs Tiny Reyes  

**Harbor Bar buzzes:** The bar stayed packed and noisy for hours, but no incidents were reported.  

**Police patrols:** Officers logged 27 stops across Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, keeping a steady watch on the block.  

**Community mood:** Several locals—including Gil Bright, Jonah Bello and Rufus Adeyemi—said they feel safe with the group, while Dez Okonkwo noted a lingering sense of being watched.  

Also on the block: the night ended with more people falling asleep on benches than standing up for a cause.
