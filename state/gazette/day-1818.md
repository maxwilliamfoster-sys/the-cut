*The Cut Gazette — day 1818*

## 9th Breaks Door on Riverside  

**Inspectors patrol Delmar.**  City officials moved down the block with clipboards, checking compliance amid the rain.  

**Cal Doyle hears rumor.**  A passerby told him that Odette Amadi has been seen talking to police, but no details were given.  

**Residents settle in.**  Both Marta Petrakis and Marta Amadi announced they have found a spot on the block and intend to stay.  

Also on the block: the rain’s the only thing moving faster than the gossip.
