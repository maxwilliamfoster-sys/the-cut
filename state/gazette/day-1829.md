*The Cut Gazette — day 1829*

## Six‑Hour Power Outage Halts Block  

**Inspectors on Delmar.** City inspectors continued their clipboard tour down Delmar while the lights were out, noting no safety hazards despite the darkness.  

**Six‑hour blackout.** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving shops and homes in the dark until power was restored late afternoon.  

**Quiet murmurs.** Residents were heard trading low‑key comments about paint, paperwork and sticky counters, but little else was reported.

Also on the block: the only thing brighter than the return of lights was a handful of yawns.
