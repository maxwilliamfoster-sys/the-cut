*The Cut Gazette — day 1830*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Power loss:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents stumbling in the dark and the streetlights off.  

**Police activity:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, and Kwame Nakamura was reported to be speaking with officers.  

**Quiet night:** Most households reported everyone asleep—June Amadi, Marta Petrakis, Cal Serrano, June Mbeki, Lena Ferreira, and Lena Mbeki were all noted as sleeping.  

Also on the block: the night was so dark even the coffee cups were “slippery.”
