*The Cut Gazette — day 1833*

## Block Skips Funeral, Attendance Sparse  

**Delmar funeral draws few neighbors.** Most of the block stayed home, and several who were expected did not appear, prompting comments about the unusually low turnout.  

**Quiet watch observed.** Cassius Bello, Etta Adeyemi, Marta Ferreira, Rosa Kowal, Sami Serrano and Theo Bello were all seen standing still with eyes closed, a behavior noted by several onlookers.  

**Neighborhood chores continue.** Amid the somber mood, Lena Doyle asked for three pallets, Nyla Bello offered sink duties, and Cassius Adeyemi requested a sponge, showing daily life pressing on.  

Also on the block: the overcast sky matches the block’s collective sigh.
