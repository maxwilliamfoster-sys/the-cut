*The Cut Gazette — day 1834*

## June Petrakis Owes Rosa Reyes Money  

**Favour with a price:** June Petrakis helped Rosa Reyes, but the aid left June short a few dollars, as he admitted later.  

**Another costly help:** Errol Brannon’s assistance to Tomas Lindqvist likewise resulted in a real expense for Brannon.  

**Rent pressure:** Ade Lindqvist was reminded that the amount he owes is due sooner than expected, adding to the block’s cash‑flow worries.  

Also on the block: the rain may be heavy, but it’s the missing change that’s keeping everyone on their toes.
