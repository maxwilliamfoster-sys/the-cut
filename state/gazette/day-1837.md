*The Cut Gazette — day 1837*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents in darkness and halting all street activity.  

**Debt warning:** June Mbeki was told the money owed is now due earlier than the original agreement, prompting a flurry of urgent calls.  

**Police chatter:** A neighbor informed Vic Osei that Farrah Osei has been seen speaking with police, adding to the day's unease.  

*Also on the block: the snow fell silently while everyone argued about the new ordinance.*
