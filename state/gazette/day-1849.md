*The Cut Gazette — day 1849*

## Landlords Plan Evictions on the Block  

**Unidentified car** remains parked on the corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity from passers‑by.  

**New notices** appeared on two Terraces doors, bearing the letterhead of a company no resident recognizes.  

**Construction activity** was reported elsewhere on the block, but details on the project or the land involved were not provided.  

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than the rumors is the ceiling fan in Kwame Bright’s office.
