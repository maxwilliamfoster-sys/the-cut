*The Cut Gazette — day 1858*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** A six‑hour loss of electricity left the block in darkness, halting work and leaving residents with only dim lamps.  

**Cash shortfall:** Cassius Reyes discovered the cash count was again slightly under, prompting quiet concern among the staff.  

**Quiet streets:** With snow falling and most on the block asleep or dozing, little else was observed beyond the occasional window stare.  

Also on the block: even the snow seems to be trying to keep the lights off.
