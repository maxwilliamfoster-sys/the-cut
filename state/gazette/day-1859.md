*The Cut Gazette — day 1859*

## Ambulance Removes Unnamed Person from Terraces  

**Emergency transport:** Before dawn an ambulance pulled up at the Terraces and whisked an unidentified individual away; officials offered no name or reason.  

**Police chatter:** Marta Osei was told that Cal Ferreira was seen talking to police, but no further details were released.  

**Quiet night:** Aside from the rain, most residents were noted sleeping, dozing, or keeping to themselves.  

Also on the block: The downpour left puddles everywhere, yet nobody seemed to mind the extra splash.
