*The Cut Gazette — day 1861*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block  

**Inspectors patrol Delmar.** City officials moved down the street with clipboards, checking on homes and businesses as the funeral proceeded.  

**Attendance uneven.** Most residents turned out for the service, but several expected faces were absent, a point noted by onlookers.  

**Quiet murmurs.** Conversations nearby hinted at restless thoughts of leaving the neighborhood, though few voiced them aloud.  

Also on the block: foggy streets make pouring a mysterious art.
