*The Cut Gazette — day 1862*

## Dez Okonkwo Discovers Cash Shortfall  

**Shortage noted:**  Dez Okonkwo reported the till was again under‑funded, a discrepancy small enough to raise eyebrows but not yet explained.  

**Night ambulance:**  An unidentified patient was taken from the Terraces just before dawn; no name was released.  

**New signs appear:**  Two doors on the Terraces now display notices from an unfamiliar company, prompting quiet chatter among locals.  

Also on the block: the extra cruisers on Delmar are just “standing by” for the morning coffee.
