*The Cut Gazette — day 1863*

## Unknown Company Posts Notices on Terraces  

**Notices appear on two Terraces doors.**  The flyer bears the letterhead of a firm no resident recognizes, and the same posting was noted twice in the morning log.  

**City inspectors and police patrol Delmar.**  Inspectors move street‑by‑street with clipboards while two extra cruisers have been stationed there since early today.  

**Neighbors chat about small grievances.**  Nia reminds Rey of a dent, Odette asks Wes to balance a drawer, and Etta complains to Maren about burnt coffee.  

Also on the block: the only thing that stays still is the clock on the cafe wall.
