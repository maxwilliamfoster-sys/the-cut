*The Cut Gazette — day 1867*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Reyes spots short count again.**  Cassius Reyes reports the register is low, “not by much, but enough,” prompting a quick tally at the end of the shift.  

**9th knocks on wrong door.**  Members of the 9th unit attempted to enter a Riverside building, only to discover they’d battered the wrong entryway.  

**Ambulance departs Terraces pre‑dawn.**  An unnamed resident was taken away from the Terraces before sunrise; no further details were given.  

Also on the block: even the night seemed to need a flashlight.
