*The Cut Gazette — day 1868*

## Gil Nakamura's favour costs Milo

**Vic Osei receives an unexpected payout**, which officials note may create its own complications.  

**City inspectors continue their Delmar sweep**, clipboards in hand, checking compliance one block at a time.

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than the gossip is the afternoon traffic on Riverside.
