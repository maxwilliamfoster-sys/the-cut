*The Cut Gazette — day 1869*

## Elias Reyes Flags Short Cash Count  

**Notice boards appear** on two Terraces doors, posted by an unfamiliar company.  

**Construction continues** on the block despite the first snow, with police noting modest activity at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic.  

**Elias’s tally** remains slightly low, prompting Milo Bello to ask if it will finally balance.  

Also on the block: the snow’s the only thing falling faster than the rumors.
