*The Cut Gazette — day 1875*

## Cassius Bello and Sami Nakamura Clash  

**Count discrepancy:** Dez Okonkwo reports the block’s tally is again a little short, though not dramatically.  

**Street argument:** Cassius Bello and Sami Nakamura were heard arguing loudly enough to stop pedestrians in the middle of the rain‑soaked street.  

**Quiet moments:** Amid the bustle, Cassius quietly feeds a stray cat while turning down the volume, and Gil Brannon leans against the wall rubbing his eyes.  

*Also on the block: the rain might finally wash away the mayor’s overdue cutter payment.*
