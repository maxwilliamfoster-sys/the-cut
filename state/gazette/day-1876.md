*The Cut Gazette — day 1876*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in darkness and prompting a flurry of questions about when the lights would return.  

**Residents’ response:** Neighbors were heard asking about a generator truck, sharing meds, and noting the rain’s sound over the silent TVs.  

**Unusual sighting:** Several locals—including Farrah Lindqvist, Iris Nakamura, Lena Ferreira, Lena Petrakis, and Errol Serrano—were observed lying still during the outage.  

Also on the block: The fog kept the waterfront as quiet as the power‑less streets.
