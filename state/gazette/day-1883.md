*The Cut Gazette — day 1883*

## Cassius Reyes Discovers Short Cash Count  

**Cash discrepancy** – Cassius Reyes reported that the block’s money count was again a little short, though the shortfall was minimal.  

**Street argument** – Sami Doyle and June Lindqvist were heard arguing loudly enough to pause nearby pedestrians.  

**Quiet observers** – Gil Bright, Iris Amadi, Jonah Bello, Milo Adeyemi and Vic Lindqvist spent the afternoon mostly asleep or watching the scene from their windows.  

Also on the block: The overcast sky stayed as mute as the neighborhood gossip.
