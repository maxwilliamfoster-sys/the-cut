*The Cut Gazette — day 1884*

## Sami Osei Hospitalized After Mysterious Injury  

**Clinic visit reveals discrepancy.**  Paperwork lists one cause for Sami Osei’s injury, but the doctor notes something different, prompting a quiet inquiry among neighbors.  

**Delmar funeral draws most of the block.**  Residents gathered for the service, though a handful who were expected did not appear, leaving a few unanswered questions.  

**Quiet murmurs in the rain.**  Conversations about tools, ordinances, and personal ambitions floated past the windows as the storm held the block in a damp hush.
