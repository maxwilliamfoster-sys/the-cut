*The Cut Gazette — day 1889*

## Dez Okonkwo Flags Short Cash Count  

**Harbor Bar stays packed.**  The tavern remained noisy and full for several hours with no incidents reported.  

**Ledger worries surface.**  Conversations around the block hint at missing money and upcoming police raids, as several residents discuss keeping books straight and doors locked.  

**Nighttime rest uneasy.**  Several locals were observed sleeping poorly—counting money, drumming fingers, or scrolling ledgers—suggesting the cash discrepancy is on many minds.  

Also on the block: the only quiet thing was the clinic’s radio.
