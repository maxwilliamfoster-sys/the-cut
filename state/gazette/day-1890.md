*The Cut Gazette — day 1890*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Most of Block  

**Funeral attendance:** Most residents turned out for the Delmar funeral, though a handful of expected names were absent.  

**Police chatter:** A tip reached Marcus Calloway that Lena Brannon has been seen talking to police, stirring low‑key talk among neighbors.  

**Harbor Bar:** The Harbor Bar stayed packed and noisy for hours, with nothing out of the ordinary reported.  

Also on the block: even the fog couldn't mute the bar’s karaoke.
