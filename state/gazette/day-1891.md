*The Cut Gazette — day 1891*

## Farrah Osei Faces Early Debt Call  

**Debt pressure:** Farrah Osei was told the money owed is needed sooner than originally agreed.  

**Ledger shortfall:** Elias Reyes discovered the count is again a little short, though not dramatically.  

**Harbor Bar:** The Harbor Bar stayed full and noisy for hours, with no incident reported.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift crew counted more yawns than yodels.
