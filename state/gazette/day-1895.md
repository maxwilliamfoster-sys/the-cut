*The Cut Gazette — day 1895*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block Attendance  

**Funeral gathers neighbors:** A service on Delmar saw most residents turn out, though a few expected faces were absent.  

**Police patrols noted:** Officers were spotted on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, keeping a routine watch as the community gathered.  

**Quiet murmurs:** Ada Bello urged unity while Cassius Bello repeated “Numbers don’t lie,” and others offered brief, subdued comments as the day unfolded.  

Also on the block: the local laundromat’s folding line doubled as a makeshift condolence board.
