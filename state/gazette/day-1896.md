*The Cut Gazette — day 1896*

## Reyes brothers discover missing funds  

**Cash count off.** Cassius and Elias Reyes each reported the block’s cash tally fell short again, though only by a small amount.  

**Harbor Bar buzzes.** The bar stayed packed and noisy for hours, but no incidents were noted.  

**Mystery car lingers.** An unrecognizable vehicle has occupied the same corner for three days, drawing curious glances from passersby.  

Also on the block: the snow’s first whisper has everyone checking their boots for extra warmth.
