*The Cut Gazette — day 1897*

## Police Deploy Extra Cruisers on Delmar  

**Extra patrols:** Two additional police cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, raising the block’s police count to fifteen.  

**Mayor’s whispers:** Several residents reported hearing the mayor’s threats and promises in hushed conversations, prompting a quiet, tense atmosphere.  

**Fog‑bound day:** With fog pressing against windows, many on the block were noted half‑asleep or staring into the dark, noting little else.

Also on the block: the fog seems to be the only thing that’s moving faster than the rumors.
