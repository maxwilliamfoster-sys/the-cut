*The Cut Gazette — day 1901*

## Ambulance Removes Unknown Patient from Terraces  

**Police patrols** reported modest activity across the block, with the highest presence on the Terraces (12 officers).  

**Rebuilding work** continued downtown; no interruptions were noted despite the rain.  

**Rumor mill**: Theo Bello was told that Maren Adeyemi was seen speaking with police, though no further details emerged.  

Also on the block: the night’s most lively debate was over who finally paid the overdue tab.
