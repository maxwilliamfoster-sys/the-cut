*The Cut Gazette — day 1902*

## Marta Petrakis Helps Gil Osei  

**Favour with a price:** Marta Petrakis asked Gil Osei to cover her shift, a favor that left both of them owing something tangible.  

**Another costly aid:** Sticks returned the same kindness to Farrah Mbeki, a trade that likewise exacted a real cost.  

**Street showdown:** Rosa Kowal and Marta Kowal were heard arguing loudly enough to pause passersby.  

Also on the block: even the pallets seem to be getting heavier these days.
