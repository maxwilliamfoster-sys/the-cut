*The Cut Gazette — day 1903*

## Power Outage Darkens Delmar Block  

**Extra police cruisers** have been parked on Delmar since this morning, a routine presence noted by the block’s watchful eyes.  

**City inspectors** moved down Delmar with clipboards while crews continued **rebuilding** work, all under six hours of darkness caused by the outage.  

**Residents** such as Odette Amadi and Errol Serrano were observed lying still or staring at the ceiling as the lights stayed out.  

Also on the block: the coffee line survived the blackout better than the power grid.
