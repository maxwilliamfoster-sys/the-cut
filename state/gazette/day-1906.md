*The Cut Gazette — day 1906*

## Officer Kohl Misplaces Historic File  

**Harbor Bar** – The bar stayed packed and noisy for hours, but no incidents were reported.  

**Malik Reyes** discovered the cash register was short again, “not by much, but enough,” he said.  

**Iris Petrakis** was told her owed payment is needed sooner than the original agreement.  

Also on the block: Theo Bello seems to be auditioning for a statue—standing still, watering a plant, then tapping his foot in perfect rhythm.
