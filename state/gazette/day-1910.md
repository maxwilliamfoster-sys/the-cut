*The Cut Gazette — day 1910*

## Vic Lindqvist costs Elias Reyes favor  

**Police patrols** logged modest activity across the block, with the highest count at Riverside (42).  

**Harbor Bar** stayed packed and noisy for hours, but reported no incidents.  

**June Petrakis** was told Cal Mbeki was seen talking to police, a detail she noted with quiet concern.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s hair in place, but not their patience.
