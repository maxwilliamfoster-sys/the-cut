*The Cut Gazette — day 1911*

## Ada Bello Confronts Gil Bright  

**Extra police cruisers** have been parked on Delmar since early morning, a quiet reminder of the heightened patrols.  

**City inspectors** paced the block with clipboards, noting violations as they moved down the street.  

**The Harbor Bar** stayed packed and noisy for hours, but no incidents were reported.  

Also on the block: the fog seems to be the only thing that isn’t keeping anyone awake.
