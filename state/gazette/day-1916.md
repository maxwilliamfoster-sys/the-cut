*The Cut Gazette — day 1916*

## Ada Bello and Sami Doyle Clash  

**Street argument draws a crowd.**  Ada Bello and Sami Doyle were heard shouting at each other on the block, pausing passersby long enough for a few heads to turn.

**A costly favour.**  Odette Mbeki helped Marta Kowal with a favour that “cost them something real,” according to witnesses.

**Inspectors on Delmar.**  City inspectors continued their clipboard‑tour down Delmar, noting nothing out of the ordinary amid the rain.

Also on the block: even the rain seemed to need a coffee break.
