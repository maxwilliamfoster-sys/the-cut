*The Cut Gazette — day 1917*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Darkness  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in the dark until service was restored early morning.  

**Ambulance run:** An ambulance removed an unidentified person from the Terraces before dawn; no further details were given.  

**Quiet chatter:** Residents exchanged mundane remarks about hair, clients, and shift coverage, while several voiced a vague sense of being watched.  

Also on the block: the barbershop’s dust now has a name—Farrah.
