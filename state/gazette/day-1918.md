*The Cut Gazette — day 1918*

## June Aids Blaise, Costs Both  

**Ambulance rushes Terraces resident before dawn.** No name was released, but the early call drew a brief crowd.  

**Overheard at the office:** “You’ll need a new folder for that case,” muttered Marta Ferreira, while Vic Osei reminded Blaise to “keep the tools clean.” The snippets suggest a busy day of paperwork and tight budgets.  

**June Mbeki was seen sleeping in the back room** while neighbors Tara ‘Tee’ Bello and several families rested together in the fog‑filled block.  

Also on the block: the fog made the streetlights look like they were on a permanent coffee break.
