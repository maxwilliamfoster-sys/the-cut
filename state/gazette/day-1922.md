*The Cut Gazette — day 1922*

## 9th Kicks Wrong Door on Riverside  

**Police report:** Officers recorded the 9th’s mistaken door‑kick on Riverside, noting no injuries and a prompt response.  

**Local voices:** A handful of residents were overheard exchanging terse remarks, but few commented beyond the brief tension in the air.  

**Community note:** Aside from the door incident, there was little else reported on the block today.  

Also on the block: the overcast sky seemed to match the mood—nothing else to break the monotony.
