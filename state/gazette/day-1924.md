*The Cut Gazette — day 1924*

## 9th Breaks Wrong Riverside Door  

**Police patrols:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, part of the block’s heightened watch.  

**Misdirected entry:** The 9th unit attempted a forced entry on Riverside, only to discover they were at the wrong door.  

**Mystery vehicle:** A car no one recognizes has remained parked on the corner for three days, prompting quiet curiosity among locals.  

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than the rain is the gossip about that immobile car.
