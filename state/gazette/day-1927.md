*The Cut Gazette — day 1927*

## Sgt. Hal Brennan Reopens Old File  

**Riverside mishap:** The 9th kicked a door on Riverside—only to discover it was the wrong one.  

**Police paperwork:** Sgt. Hal Brennan retrieved a long‑closed file and, unable to reseal it, left it open on his desk.  

**Quiet night:** Aside from a few dozing residents, little else stirred on the block.  

Also on the block: the night was so still even the hair clippers stayed mute.
