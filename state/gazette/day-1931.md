*The Cut Gazette — day 1931*

## Inspectors Sweep Delmar Amid Tensions  

**City inspectors** marched down Delmar today, clipboards in hand, checking compliance as rain hammered the streets.  

**Rosa Amadi** reported feeling uneasy, suspecting someone was secretly staying in her room, though no further details emerged.  

**Local chatter** floated about the possible closure of the Blue Spoon, with Jonah Bello wondering if the city would actually shut it down.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone indoors, so the neighborhood’s drama stayed comfortably dry.
