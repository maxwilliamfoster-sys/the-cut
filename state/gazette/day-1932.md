*The Cut Gazette — day 1932*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Most of Block  

**Construction continues.**  New building work was noted today, though no further details were given.  

**Police reports modest.**  Activity tallied 40 at Riverside, 31 on Delmar, 5 each on Terraces and Civic; no incidents reported.  

**Funeral attendance uneven.**  Most residents attended the Delmar service, but a few expected participants were absent.  

Also on the block: the sirens were the loudest lullaby.
