*The Cut Gazette — day 1936*

## Sgt. Hal Brennan Misplaces Old File  

**File resurfaced:** Sgt. Hal Brennan pulled a long‑closed case file and, unable to replace it, left it out on the precinct floor, sparking murmurs about the missing paperwork.  

**Mysterious notices:** Two doors in the Terraces now display letters from an unknown company, prompting curiosity but no clear explanation from residents.  

**Block chatter:** Voices on the block trade thin warnings—“Give me that spot,” “You’ll get the room if you stop screwing around,” and “Keep your head down”—suggesting a scramble for limited space.  

Also on the block: someone’s blanket is apparently the most drama‑free accessory today.
