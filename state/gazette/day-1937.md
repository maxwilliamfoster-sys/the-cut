*The Cut Gazette — day 1937*

## Harbor Bar damaged after loud night  

**Quiet pleas ignored:** Rosa Amadi asked staff to keep the door shut, but the bar stayed full and noisy until damage was reported.  

**Murmurs of surveillance:** Several residents voiced frustration with recent bans on nightstands, benches, and public haircuts, seeing them as tools of control.  

**Local chatter:** Cal Doyle searched for his missing brother, while Marta Amadi hinted at speaking up soon, and Vic Osei warned the streets were “rough tonight.”  

Also on the block: the night’s excitement left the bar bruised but the rest of the block stayed oddly uneventful.
