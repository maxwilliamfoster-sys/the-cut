*The Cut Gazette — day 1942*

## Ambulance Removes Unnamed Patient from Terraces  

**Ambulance departure** – An ambulance pulled an unidentified person out of the Terraces before dawn; no name or cause was disclosed.  

**Mysterious notices** – Two doors on the Terraces now bear letters from a company no one on the block recognizes, prompting quiet speculation.  

**Residents exchange warnings** – Voices on the block warned each other about cash, scissors, sharp hair and other hazards, suggesting a low‑key tension among neighbors.  

Also on the block: even the rain seems to be listening to Vic Osei’s tilted head.
