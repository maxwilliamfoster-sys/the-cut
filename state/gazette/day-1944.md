*The Cut Gazette — day 1944*

## Reyes brothers discover short count  

**Power outage:** The block endured a six‑hour blackout, leaving homes and shops in darkness.  

**Family audit:** Both Malik and Elias Reyes reported a modest shortfall in the ledgers, noting “enough” to raise eyebrows.  

**Funeral on Delmar:** Most residents attended the service; a handful of expected guests stayed away.  

Also on the block: Even the water tastes different when you’re half‑asleep.
