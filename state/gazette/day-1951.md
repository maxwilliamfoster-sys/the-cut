*The Cut Gazette — day 1951*

## Theo Bello Demands Early Payment  

**Theo Bello receives urgent notice** that his debt must be paid ahead of schedule, prompting a flurry of nervous activity among his relatives.  

**Neighbors overhear tensions** as Rey Vasquez, Cassius Reyes, Cal Serrano, Elias Reyes and Dot Feeney each voice lingering grievances about owed favors.  

**Local residents note odd behavior**: Ada and Cassius Bello fidget by the nightstand, while Rufus Adeyemi watches the clock and Errol Doyle lies still, eyes shut.  

Also on the block: the nightstand rumor still fuels whispered theories.
