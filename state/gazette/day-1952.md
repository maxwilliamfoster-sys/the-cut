*The Cut Gazette — day 1952*

## Ambulance Removes Patient from Terraces  

**Power outage:** A six‑hour blackout left the block in darkness, prompting residents to huddle around candles and flashlights.  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since early morning, adding to the already‑visible patrols on Riverside, Terraces, and Civic.  

**Local voices:** Residents voiced frustration and ambition—from Wes Doyle’s warning about Lyle to Nia Doyle’s pledge to run the place someday.  

Also on the block: the rain sounds like a city‑wide applause for the drama.
