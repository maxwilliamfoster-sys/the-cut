*The Cut Gazette — day 1954*

## 9th Kicks Wrong Door on Riverside  

**Door mishap:** The 9th knocked on a Riverside door, only to learn it was the wrong entrance.  

**Harbor Bar:** The bar stayed full and noisy for hours, but nothing untoward occurred.  

**Quiet night:** Maren, Nia, Milo, Nyla and Otis Bello spent the rainy evening asleep or awake, while Marta Petrakis sipped water by the window.  

Also on the block: rain kept everyone indoors, and the only vibrations were from a restless ceiling.
