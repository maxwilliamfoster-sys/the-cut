*The Cut Gazette — day 1955*

## Power Outage Darkens Block Six Hours  

**Harbor Bar stays packed.**  Patrons kept the noise up even as the lights flickered out, and the crowd remained lively through the blackout.  

**Residents slept through the dark.**  Several locals—Tomas Lindqvist, Tomas Amadi, Tomas Ferreira, Vic Osei, and Ade Lindqvist—were noted dozing while the block was without electricity.  

**Quiet chatter persists.**  Despite the silence, a few voices were overheard discussing till duties, market news, and a missing razor, but no major news emerged.  

Also on the block: the fog kept the waterfront looking like a misplaced movie set.
