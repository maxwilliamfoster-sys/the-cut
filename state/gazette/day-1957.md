*The Cut Gazette — day 1957*

## 9th Kicks Wrong Door on Riverside  

**Door mishap:** The 9th kicked a door on Riverside, only to discover it was the wrong one.  

**Funeral attendance:** A funeral on Delmar drew most of the block, though a few regulars were missing.  

**Inspectors on patrol:** City inspectors keep moving down Delmar with clipboards in hand.  

Also on the block: the night’s only drama was a door that got more of a kick than a knock.
