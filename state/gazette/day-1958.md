*The Cut Gazette — day 1958*

## Block Plunged Into Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power loss:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents stumbling in the dark and delaying routine chores.  

**Emergency transport:** An ambulance removed an unidentified person from the Terraces before dawn; no further details were given.  

**Family favor:** Clemmie Bello performed a favor for Ada Bello that “cost them something real,” according to block chatter.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift TV kept more eyes open than the streetlights.
