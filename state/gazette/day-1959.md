*The Cut Gazette — day 1959*

## Detective Ruiz Reopens Long‑Closed Case  

**Ruiz’s surprise file** – Detective Yolanda Ruiz retrieved a case file that should have been sealed years ago and was unable to replace it, prompting a brief police‑department scramble.  

**Doyle’s overdue bill** – Marta Doyle was informed that the payment she’s been waiting on is now demanded earlier than the original agreement.  

**Quiet corners** – Aside from a few murmurs, the block reports little else of note today.  

Also on the block: the TV’s glow keeps more residents awake than the night‑shift gossip.
