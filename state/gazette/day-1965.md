*The Cut Gazette — day 1965*

## Two Extra Cruisers Patrol Delmar  

**Harbor Bar stays noisy but peaceful.** Patrons filled the bar for hours with no incident reported.  

**Police presence spikes on Delmar.** Two additional cruisers have been stationed there since morning, reflecting heightened attention.  

**Residents exchange uneasy chatter.** Snippets heard include Blaise Bright asking about “him,” Odette Lindqvist urging restraint, and Iris Lindqvist promising a plan.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift lights stay on, just in case.
