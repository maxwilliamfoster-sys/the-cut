*The Cut Gazette — day 1966*

## Two Residents Evacuated from Terraces  

**Police report:** 5 officers at Riverside, 13 at Delmar, 18 on the Terraces, 5 at Civic. The Terraces received the most attention.  

**Residents heard:** Lena Ferreira asked for the books back “before they close,” while Marta Petrakis demanded new forms “now.” Several locals muttered about plans, trouble, and keeping things low.  

**Around town:** Otis Bello and Rosa Amadi were glued to radios; Iris Nakamura sorted the post; Rosa Kowal watched TV.  

Also on the block: the radios were louder than the gossip.
