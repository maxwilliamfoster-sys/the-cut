*The Cut Gazette — day 1967*

## Extra Police Cruisers Park on Delmar  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the routine patrols noted across the block.  

**Night watch:** Residents reported a quiet evening; several neighbors—Priya Raman, Marta Osei, Blaise Serrano, Errol Doyle, Lena Brannon, and Ade Lindqvist—were seen sleeping or keeping a low‑key watch through the night.  

**Community chatter:** Aside from the police activity, there was little else being said on the block today.  

*Also on the block:* The night was so still even the ceiling seemed to listen.
