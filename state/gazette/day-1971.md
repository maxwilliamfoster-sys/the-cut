*The Cut Gazette — day 1971*

## Payout Granted to Nia Mbeki  

**Police activity:** Officers recorded minor incidents today—five at Riverside, seventeen on Delmar, five on the Terraces, and five near Civic. No arrests were made.  

**Neighborhood watch:** Gil and Marta Brannon were seen dozing in front of the TV, while Kwame Bright slept on a cot and Marta Kowal on the couch. Kwame Brannon lay awake, eyes open, and Nia Brannon remained asleep on the bunk.  

**Overheard remark:** Rey Vasquez was heard telling someone, “Do not look at me. Answer them.”  

Also on the block: the fog may be thick, but the block’s snoozers are thicker.
