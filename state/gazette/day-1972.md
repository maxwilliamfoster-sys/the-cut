*The Cut Gazette — day 1972*

## Rosa Kowal Receives Unexpected Payout

**Payout surprise:** Rosa Kowal was paid more than anticipated, prompting murmurs about the source and its “own problem.”  

**Debt pressure:** Lena Bright learned her owed amount is now due sooner than the original agreement, a fact already buzzing among neighbors.  

**Bar buzz:** The Harbor Bar stayed packed and noisy for several hours, but no incident was reported.

Also on the block: the town’s biggest mystery remains why the water keeps leaking behind the café.
