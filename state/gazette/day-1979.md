*The Cut Gazette — day 1979*

## Kwame Osei Receives Unexpected Payout  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s usual patrol count.  

**Local chatter:** Residents heard a string of uneasy remarks—Errol Mbeki lamenting misunderstanding, Cal Serrano recalling “that night,” and Cassius Bello urging someone to “share some of that luck.”  

**Neighborhood scene:** The Bello family was spotted shifting on the couch, while Jonah and Rufus woke quietly; Marta Brannon turned in her sleep, and Dev Doyle snorted awake briefly.  

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the police radios was the couch creaking under a sudden payout.
