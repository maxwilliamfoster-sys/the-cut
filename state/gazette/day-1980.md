*The Cut Gazette — day 1980*

## Tomas Ferreira Helps Wes, Pays Price  

**Tomas Ferreira** was seen talking to police, according to a tip delivered to **Chidi Okonkwo**.  

**Wes Doyle** received a favor from Tomas that “costs them something real,” prompting murmurs on the block.  

**Nyla Bello** learned her debt is due sooner than expected, a fact that left her muttering about the coffee bench being a trap.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift crowd still debates who stole the last donut.
