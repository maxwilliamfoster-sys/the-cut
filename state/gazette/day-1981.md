*The Cut Gazette — day 1981*

## Unknown Company Posts Notices on Terraces  

**Terraces notices:** Two doors in the Terraces now display letters from a company no one recognizes.  

**Delmar inspection:** City inspectors are making their rounds down Delmar, clipboards in hand.  

**Residents uneasy:** Several locals report feeling watched or ignored, from Dez Okonkwo to Sami Doyle.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s hair‑cuts and dreams equally damp.
