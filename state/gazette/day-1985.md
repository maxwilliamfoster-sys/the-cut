*The Cut Gazette — day 1985*

## Mysterious Car Occupies Corner for Days  

**Police patrols** report heightened activity on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, though no incident tied to the parked vehicle has been confirmed.  

**Local chatter** captured a tense exchange between Cassius and Elias Reyes, each calling the other a “joke,” while Nadia Vasquez pressed for any leads on the 1985 case.  

**Nightwatch** noted a quiet scene: Nia Brannon, Simone Adeyemi (sheltered from the rain), Jonah Adeyemi, June Amadi, Sami Osei and Nia Doyle were all found asleep around the block.  

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than the rain is the gossip.
