*The Cut Gazette — day 1986*

## Farrah Mbeki Signs for Mobster  

**Street dispute:** Theo Bello and Odette Ferreira were heard arguing loudly enough to pause passersby.  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, adding to the routine patrol count.  

**Local concerns:** Dez Okonkwo notes the cannery shutdown has left his future unstable, while Gil Bright says he caught a clerk cheating and now trusts no one.  

Also on the block: the trash cans are full again, according to Iris Petrakis.
