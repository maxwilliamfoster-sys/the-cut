*The Cut Gazette — day 1987*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage stalls the block.**  Six hours of darkness left homes without lights, phones and refrigeration; crews are still investigating.  

**Mysterious car remains.**  An unclaimed vehicle has sat on the corner for three days, drawing curious glances but no owner’s claim.  

**Residents stay up.**  Vic Osei, Ade Brannon, Blaise Osei, Priya Raman and others were spotted awake or napping at odd hours, likely coping with the outage.  

Also on the block: Someone finally admitted they’re “publicly humiliated” by Wes.
