*The Cut Gazette — day 2007*

## Two Extra Cruisers Park on Delmar  

**Rebuilt projects** continue downtown, though no new details were offered.  

**Police presence** remains high, with officers noted at Riverside, Terraces, Civic and the two added cruisers on Delmar since this morning.  

**Local chatter** stays quiet; a few residents were seen watching TV, checking clocks, or simply waiting in the tenements.  

Also on the block: the rain keeps everyone indoors, but the streets stay oddly empty.
