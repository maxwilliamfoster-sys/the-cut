*The Cut Gazette — day 2008*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Police patrols** reported increased activity on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, with five to seven officers each.  

**Residents voiced** frustration in the dark; several cited feeling ignored, invisible, or a nuisance while the power was out.  

**Sleepers on the block**—Marta, Gil, Etta, and Farrah—were noted dozing despite the rain‑soaked night.  

Also on the block: the rain finally gave everyone a legitimate excuse for staying indoors.
