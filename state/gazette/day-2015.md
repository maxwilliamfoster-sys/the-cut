*The Cut Gazette — day 2015*

## 9th kicks wrong door at Riverside  

**Police note:** 29 calls on Riverside today, the highest on the block, while Delmar, Terraces and Civic each logged under 15.  

**Residents heard:** Vic Osei warned “Not today” as tensions rose, and Tomas Lindqvist reminded a colleague to “keep the lights on.”  

**Around the block:** Farrah Mbeki jotted ledger notes on a napkin; Wes Doyle stared at a flickering TV; Dev Petrakis was asleep on a bench.  

*Also on the block:* The fog was thick enough to hide the missing door knob.
