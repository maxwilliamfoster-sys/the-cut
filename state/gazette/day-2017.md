## Errol Lindqvist and Clemmie Bello Clash  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, reflecting heightened attention on the block.  

**Local dispute:** Errol Lindqvist and Clemmie Bello were heard arguing loudly in the street, drawing a brief crowd that paused their errands.  

**Financial pressure:** Jonah Adeyemi was told the debt owed is now due sooner than previously agreed.
