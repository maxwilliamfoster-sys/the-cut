*The Cut Gazette — day 2022*

## Mysterious Car Stalls on Corner  

**Unidentified vehicle** has sat on the same street corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity but no official comment.  

**Tomas Lindqvist** was spotted napping nearby, apparently unfazed by the stationary car.  

**Police patrols** reported normal activity on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, with no incidents linked to the parked car.  

Also on the block: the neighborhood’s most persistent mystery remains the missing driver’s license plate.
