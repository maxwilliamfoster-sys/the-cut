*The Cut Gazette — day 2042*

## Sami Osei Spotted Talking to Police  

**Extra cruisers patrol Delmar.** Two additional police cars have been stationed on the street since early morning, drawing curious glances from passers‑by.  

**Threats murmur in the hallway.** Residents overheard several tense remarks—“Keep an eye on him; I’ll have my revenge,” “You’re up next on the list,” and similar warnings—while the block’s usual chatter remained minimal.  

**Cal Kowal relays the sighting.** After hearing the rumor, Kowal told others that Sami Osei had indeed been seen in conversation with officers.  

Also on the block: the rain kept the gossip as soggy as the sidewalks.
