*The Cut Gazette — day 2059*

## Block rebuilt after heavy rain  

**Police patrols** reported at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but noted no incidents.  

**Residents** were largely asleep—Dot Feeney, Ada Bello, Cassius Adeyemi, Nadia Vasquez, June Ferreira and June Mbeki were all seen dozing.  

**Local chatter** was minimal; “little” was the only comment recorded.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone in bed, but the streets stayed clean.
