*The Cut Gazette — day 2061*

## Cassius Reyes Finds Inventory Short Again  

**Notice posted on Terraces doors:** A little‑known company’s letterhead now hangs on two entryways; no one on the block recognizes the firm.  

**Reyes’ frustration echoed:** He’s warned that “people don’t respect my seriousness,” while Kwame and Lena both claim they’re “not valued here,” and Milo says he’s “blocked from progress.”  

**Rain‑soaked murmurs:** Residents were heard debating safety, raids, and quiet tactics as the downpour hammered the streets.  

Also on the block: the rain may be the only thing moving faster than the gossip.
