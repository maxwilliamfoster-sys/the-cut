*The Cut Gazette — day 2068*

## Book Theft Rumor Circulates at Harbor Bar  

**Bar crowd stays loud:** The Harbor Bar remained packed and noisy through the rain, with no incidents reported.  

**Whispers of theft:** Blaise Serrano mentioned “Someone’s been stealing from the books,” while others muttered about games, storms, and plans to move once the weather clears.  

**Quiet observers:** June Lindqvist signed a delivery, Wes Doyle kept watch at the door, and Farrah Mbeki counted coins, all under the steady drizzle.  

Also on the block: the rain may be cleaning the streets, but it isn’t washing away the gossip.
