*The Cut Gazette — day 2069*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Finance shortfall:** Cassius Reyes reports the block’s count is again under, though only slightly.  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, raising resident curiosity.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** With lights out, several locals—Nyla Bello, Rufus Adeyemi and others—feel “unfairly singled out” while searching for flashlights and the cause.  

Also on the block: the TV’s flicker made the shadows look more dramatic than the news.
