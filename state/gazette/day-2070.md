*The Cut Gazette — day 2070*

## Detective Ruiz Unseals Long‑Hidden Mayor File  

**File resurfaced:** Detective Yolanda Ruiz opened a decades‑old case file that the mayor tried to keep hidden; she was unable to replace it.  

**Mysterious notices:** Two doors in the Terraces now display letters from an unfamiliar company, duplicated on both entries.  

**Rain‑bound chatter:** Locals heard snippets about covering shifts, a missing float, and the vanished file while staying indoors under the heavy rain.  

Also on the block: Even the raindrops seem to be timing their drips to the town’s gossip.
