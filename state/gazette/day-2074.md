*The Cut Gazette — day 2074*

## Two Extra Cruisers Park on Delmar  

**Harbor Bar stays busy.**  The bar was loud and full for several hours, but no incident was reported.  

**Police presence noted.**  Officers were counted at Riverside (5), Delmar (16), Terraces (5) and Civic (5); the extra cruisers on Delmar have been there since this morning.  

**Residents whisper.**  A handful of block residents were overheard discussing personal matters—missing ledger pages, family searches, and vague plans—but offered no concrete news.  

Also on the block: the rain keeps everyone’s umbrellas in a permanent state of rehearsal.
