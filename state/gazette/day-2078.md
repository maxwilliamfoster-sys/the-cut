*The Cut Gazette — day 2078*

## Two Police Cruisers Parked on Delmar  

**Unidentified car** remains on the Delmar corner, untouched for three days, prompting curious glances from passers‑by.  

**Mysterious notices** appeared on two Terraces doors; the stationery bears a company name no resident recognizes.  

**Quiet night** on the block as several locals—Sami Doyle, Vic Lindqvist, Vic Osei, Ade Lindqvist, Nia Amadi, and Odette Mbeki—were seen sleeping outdoors.  

Also on the block: the streetlights keep flickering, but nobody’s willing to call an electrician.
