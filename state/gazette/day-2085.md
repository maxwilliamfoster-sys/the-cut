*The Cut Gazette — day 2085*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and shops in darkness until service was restored this afternoon.  

**Mysterious car:** An unmarked vehicle has sat at the same corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity but no clear explanation.  

**Quiet observations:** Residents were seen in modest activities—Lena Petrakis scrolling through photos, Rosa Kowal folding a blanket and praying, and Vic Osei tuning a comb—while the snow fell.  

Also on the block: the police kept a low profile, counting heads at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic.
