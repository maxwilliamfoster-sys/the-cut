*The Cut Gazette — day 2087*

## Six‑Hour Power Outage Plunges Block  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the usual patrols.  

**Mystery notices:** Unfamiliar company letters appeared on two Terraces doors during the blackout, drawing curious glances from neighbors.  

**Lights out:** The six‑hour loss of power left residents—Ade Bright, Cal Kowal, and others—watching flickering TV screens in the dark, while a few offered to help restore illumination.  

Also on the block: the blackout gave everyone a chance to finally hear the street’s usual hum.
