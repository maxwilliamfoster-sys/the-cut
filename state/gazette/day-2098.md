*The Cut Gazette — day 2098*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Funeral on Delmar draws most residents.**  The ceremony proceeds despite a few expected absences, and the crowd gathers quietly as the power flickers out later in the day.  

**Harbor Bar stays noisy until the lights go out.**  Patrons enjoy a loud evening, but the sudden outage forces an early close and a scramble for flashlights.  

**Whispers about sealed files and ledger gaps linger.**  Several locals are heard discussing confidential documents and financial discrepancies while the darkness holds the block in suspense.  

Also on the block: even the dogs seem to hum a lullaby when the lights fail.
