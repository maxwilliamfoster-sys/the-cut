*The Cut Gazette — day 2099*

## Sami Mbeki and Jonah Bello Clash  

**Harbor Bar:** The tavern stayed packed and noisy for hours, but no trouble was reported.  

**Street dispute:** Sami Mbeki and Jonah Bello’s argument drew a crowd as passersby halted to listen.  

**Evening quiet:** Aside from a few sleepy residents, the block was otherwise calm.  

Also on the block: the night‑route ledger is off by a cent, but nobody seems awake enough to fix it.
