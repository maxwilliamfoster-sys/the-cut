*The Cut Gazette — day 2102*

## Raman, Bright Told Debt Due Early  

**Debt pressure:** Priya Raman and Vic Bright were each notified that the money they owe must be paid sooner than the original agreement.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Marta Ferreira lamented being “stuck here,” while Nia Lindqvist urged “a move to a better place,” and several others tried to keep peace between rival shops.  

**Quiet corners:** Maren Bello was seen washing dishes at Terrace B, Milo Adeyemi napped on a sofa at Terrace A, and Blaise Osei tended the Pier Market till.  

*Also on the block: The only thing moving faster than the gossip is the plant‑watering schedule.*
