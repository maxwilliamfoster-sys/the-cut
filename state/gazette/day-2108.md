## Errol Kowal and Farrah Mbeki Clash  

**Argument stalls traffic:** Kowal and Mbeki were heard shouting in the street, forcing passersby to pause while they debated.  

**Quiet elsewhere:** Aside from the dispute, most residents were asleep—Lena Ferreira, Lena Mbeki, Maren Adeyemi, and Milo Adeyemi (twice noted) were all observed napping.  

**Local chatter:** Brief remarks floated by, from Cassius Reyes about heat to Gil Bright asking about invoices, but no further details emerged.  

Also on the block: the fog kept the river silent, but the gossip was anything but.
