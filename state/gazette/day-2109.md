*The Cut Gazette — day 2109*

## Block Plunged into Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in darkness and delaying routine tasks.  

**Street argument:** Blaise Osei and Jonah Bello were heard shouting at each other in the street, loud enough to halt nearby pedestrians.  

**Quiet corners:** Several residents—including Marta Kowal, Sami Lindqvist and Theo Bello—were noted asleep or dozing as the lights went out.  

Also on the block: the new bedtime ban is already being called a “threat to freedom.”
