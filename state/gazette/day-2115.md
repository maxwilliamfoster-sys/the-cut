*The Cut Gazette — day 2115*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Power loss** left the block in darkness for six hours, halting work and leaving residents relying on flashlights and candles.  

**Funeral on Delmar** drew most of the neighborhood, though a few expected attendees were absent, prompting quiet murmurs among those who remained.  

**Hiring notices** appeared in the morning, but no further details were given.  

Also on the block: even the water‑seeking Lena Ferreira finally found a glass—just as the lights flickered back on.
