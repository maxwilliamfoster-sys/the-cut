## Amadi and Adeyemi Clash on Street  

**Unidentified car:** A mystery vehicle has sat on the corner for three days, drawing occasional glances but no claims of ownership.  

**Loud argument:** Tomas Amadi and Milo Adeyemi were heard shouting at each other, halting nearby pedestrians for a few minutes.  

**Shift chatter:** Workers on the block muttered about keeping shelves steady, covering checkouts, and “leaving me out of the family drama.”  

Also on the block: even the overcast can’t hide the fact that no one knows who built or hired the unseen car.
