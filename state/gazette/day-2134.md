*The Cut Gazette — day 2134*

## Malik Reyes finds cash count short  

**Harbor Bar stays packed.**  The neighborhood tavern was noisy and full for several hours, but no incident was reported.  

**Reyes notes a shortfall.**  Malik Reyes discovered the register was again a little under the expected total, enough to raise a comment but not enough to halt business.  

**Block voices echo.**  Residents were heard muttering about cooperation, trust, and “staying low,” with several mentioning future plans and personal worth.  

Also on the block: the snow‑watching machine still runs, even when no snow falls.
