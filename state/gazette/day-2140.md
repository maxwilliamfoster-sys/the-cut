*The Cut Gazette — day 2140*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power loss:** The neighborhood was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes in darkness and halting most activity.  

**Debt notice:** Iris Brannon was told the money she owes is now due earlier than the original agreement.  

**Quiet night:** Several residents—Errol Doyle, Farrah Lindqvist, Errol Mbeki, Ade and June Nakamura—were observed sleeping soundly despite the outage.  

Also on the block: The only buzz was a handful of whispered requests for ledgers and blankets.
