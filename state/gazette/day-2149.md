*The Cut Gazette — day 2149*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Most Block Residents  

**Ambulance rushes from Terraces.**  An unnamed patient was taken away before dawn, leaving the neighbourhood quietly unsettled.  

**Rosa Reyes receives unexpected payment.**  The settlement exceeded expectations, prompting a brief note that the extra sum “is its own problem.”  

**Priya Raman promises action.**  “Got it, I’ll make it happen,” she was heard saying, though no further details were offered.  

Also on the block: a parade of sleepers—Sami Mbeki, Sami Osei, Tomas Mbeki, June Lindqvist, Kwame Nakamura and Lena Ferreira—proved the fog was more soporific than sinister.
