*The Cut Gazette — day 2154*

## Nadia Vasquez and Dev Petrakis Clash  

**Street argument draws a crowd.**  Nadia Vasquez and Dev Petrakis were heard shouting at one another, stopping passersby in their tracks.  

**Harbor Bar stays lively.**  The bar remained full and noisy for several hours, but no disturbances were reported.  

**Quiet night for most residents.**  Aside from the argument, families were seen sleeping together and a few locals dozed in front of the TV.  

Also on the block: the police kept a routine watch, but nothing needed their attention.
