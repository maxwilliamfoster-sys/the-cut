*The Cut Gazette — day 2164*

## Unknown Company Posts Notices on Terraces  

**Notice boards:** Two doors in the Terraces now display letters from a firm no one recognizes, sparking curiosity among neighbors.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Several residents were heard reminding each other of unpaid debts and missing items—Tariq to Errol, Dot to Farrah, and Wes to Odette, among others.  

**Quiet corners:** A handful of locals were seen adjusting blankets, shifting on couches, muttering prayers, tapping armrests, or simply sighing, keeping the block’s pulse steady.  

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the notices was the collective sigh of the morning.
