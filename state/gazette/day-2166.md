*The Cut Gazette — day 2166*

## Tomas Amadi’s costly favor to Iris  

**Notice posted:** Two Terraces doors display letters from an unknown company, drawing curious glances from residents.  

**Ambulance departure:** Before dawn an ambulance removed an unidentified person from the Terraces; no name was given.  

**Local whisper:** Gil Bright was overheard muttering, “We short a ten, you see?” – the meaning remains unclear.  

Also on the block: the neighborhood cats seem to have formed a union and are now demanding more sunny spots.
