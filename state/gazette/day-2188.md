*The Cut Gazette — day 2188*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Power loss:** A six‑hour blackout left the block in darkness, halting work and leaving residents scrambling for flashlights and spare batteries.  

**Residents’ chatter:** In the gloom, Nia Serrano warned “Watch the back,” while Sami Mbeki muttered “You still think you own this hallway?” and Ade Lindqvist asked for spare parts, underscoring the uneasy atmosphere.  

**Quiet corners:** Farrah Osei perched on a pillow, Gil Nakamura scanned the ceiling, and Ada Bello tallied numbers on a scrap of paper as the lights stayed out.  

Also on the block: The mayor’s threats were the loudest rumor nobody could verify.
