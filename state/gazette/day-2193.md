*The Cut Gazette — day 2193*

## Booker Lyle’s Favor Costs Tomas Amadi  

**Funeral on Delmar.** Most block residents attended, though a few expected faces were missing.  

**Quiet night.** Several locals—Errol Serrano, Lena Petrakis, Maren Adeyemi, Marta Ferreira and Milo Adeyemi—were found asleep in various homes, while Marta Osei stayed awake listening to traffic.  

**Whispers about the mayor.** Iris Nakamura was heard asking, “Did you hear about the mayor?” amid the otherwise low‑key chatter.  

Also on the block: the neighborhood’s collective nap schedule seems to be winning over any gossip.
