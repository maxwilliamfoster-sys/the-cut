*The Cut Gazette — day 2199*

## Marta Doyle’s favour hits Iris  

**Marta Doyle helped Iris Petrakis**, but the aid came with a tangible price for Iris.  

**New notices appear** on two Terraces doors, printed on letterhead from an unfamiliar company; no one on the block recognizes the name.  

**Residents whisper about ledgers** and secret meetings, with Gil Brannon promising anonymity and Sami Nakamura urging the books be locked tonight.  

*Also on the block: the rain’s so steady even the squirrels are filing for overtime.*
