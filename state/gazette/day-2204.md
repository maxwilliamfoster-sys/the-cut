*The Cut Gazette — day 2204*

## Reyes, Ferreira clash on street  

**Argument stalls traffic** – Elias Reyes and Lena Ferreira were heard shouting at each other in the middle of the block, drawing a brief crowd before they dispersed.  

**9th mis‑hits Riverside door** – A member of the 9th unit tried to force a door on Riverside, only to discover it was the wrong one.  

**Quiet night for most residents** – Otis and Tariq Bello, Vic and Odette Lindqvist, Iris Amadi, Tomas Mbeki and others were found sleeping in their homes or shops.  

Also on the block: the overcast sky kept the gossip to a whisper.
