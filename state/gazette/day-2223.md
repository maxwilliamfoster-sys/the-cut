*The Cut Gazette — day 2223*

## Ambulance Removes Unnamed Patient from Terraces  

**Police patrols** reported modest activity across the block, with 15 officers at Riverside, 12 on the Terraces, 7 on Delmar, and 5 near Civic.  

**Morning emergency** saw an ambulance whisk a patient out of the Terraces before sunrise; no name was released.  

**Local chatter** included Farrah Mbeki noting “the ledger still shows a loss” and Cassius Bello offering “drink before you cry,” while residents complained about the snow‑slowed traffic.  

Also on the block: the snow’s still falling, but the block’s gossip machine isn’t.
