*The Cut Gazette — day 2225*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Most Block  

**Funeral attendance.** A service on Delmar saw the majority of residents turn out, though a few regulars were noticeably absent.  

**Quiet murmurs.** Overheard comments ranged from “I won’t let this go on” to “Just… don’t bother me,” suggesting lingering personal concerns among neighbors.  

**Block activity.** June Lindqvist was seen reviewing mail, while Blaise Osei kept a radio on and others gathered around televisions as the rain fell.  

Also on the block: the rain kept the streets soggy, but nobody complained about the puddles.
