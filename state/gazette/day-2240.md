*The Cut Gazette — day 2240*

## 9th Misfires Door Kick on Riverside  

**Riverside mishap:** The 9th attempted to kick a door on Riverside, only to discover it was the wrong one. No injuries were reported.  

**Terraces notices:** Two doors in the Terraces now display letters from an unfamiliar company; residents are puzzled but no further action has been taken.  

**Delmar funeral:** A funeral on Delmar drew most of the block, though a few expected attendees were absent.  

Also on the block: the mayor’s threats are the hot topic at the water cooler.
