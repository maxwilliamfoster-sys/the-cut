*The Cut Gazette — day 2241*

## Theo Bello’s Favor Costs Both Parties  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, augmenting the already modest patrols on the block.  

**Favors with a price:** Theo Bello told Errol Brannon, “You know what I did for Errol? It costs us both,” and Lena Bright made a similarly costly favor for Kwame Osei.  

**Cal Kowal uneasy:** Cal Kowal, eyes on the ledger, says he feels watched and “was taken in,” trembling as he reviews the books.  

Also on the block: The neighborhood cats staged a synchronized nap on the fire hydrant.
