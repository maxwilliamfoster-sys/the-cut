*The Cut Gazette — day 2242*

## 9th Breaks Riverside Door  

**Power outage:** A block‑wide blackout lasted six hours, leaving homes and businesses in the dark.  

**Harbor Bar:** The bar stayed packed and noisy throughout the outage, with no reports of trouble.  

**On the street:** Vic Bright paced while scrolling his phone, Marta Brannon lingered by the door with water, and Errol Bright waited for a call as rain fell.  

Also on the block: the storm made even the streetlights look sleepy.
