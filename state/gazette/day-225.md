*The Cut Gazette — day 225*

## Two Patrol Cars Hold Delmar Spot  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, joining the five units already counted there.  

**Community chatter:** Wes Doyle worried about losing his job, Junie Marsh said “maybe tomorrow will be different,” and Det. Yolanda Ruiz said the department needs more evidence.  

**Nighttime sightings:** Gus Petrakis, Wes Doyle and Marcus Calloway were noted sleeping or half‑asleep; Malik Reyes, Amara Bello and Booker Lyle were alone, watching the snow or handling a delivery.  

Also on the block: The snow kept the streets quiet, but the coffee shop lights stayed on.
