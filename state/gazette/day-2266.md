*The Cut Gazette — day 2266*

## 9th Kicks Riverside Door  

**Snow blankets the block.**  The first snowfall arrived with police checks at Riverside (27), Delmar (15), Terraces (5) and Civic (5).  

**Harbor Bar stays lively.**  Despite the cold, the bar was full and noisy for several hours, with staff wiping counters and patrons watching the TV.  

**Office chatter continues.**  Errol Bright asked about intake forms while Iris Serrano noted the mayor’s mutterings, and residents complained of recurring power flickers.  

Also on the block: the snow makes even the bar’s glass wipes look like winter work.
