*The Cut Gazette — day 2270*

## Blaise Mbeki Spotted With Police  

**Funeral draws crowd.** Most residents attended the Delmar service, though a few expected faces were absent.  

**Construction activity.** New buildings went up and several locals were hired for the projects, a modest boost to the block’s labor market.  

**Rumors circulate.** Conversations about fresh fines, a moving cannery and a proposed haircut ban floated through the streets, keeping neighbors chatty.  

Also on the block: the only thing the weather didn’t dampen was the town’s gossip.
