*The Cut Gazette — day 2277*

## Ambulance Removes Patient from Terraces  

**Blackout hits the block.**  A six‑hour power loss left homes and shops in darkness before dawn, prompting a chorus of comments from residents who noted the quiet and the lingering lights in the clinic.  

**Clinic stays open.**  Despite the outage, Lena Bright and Ade Bright reported that the local clinic kept its lights on and continued seeing patients, while staff like Simone Adeyemi brewed coffee for the waiting crowd.  

**Floats counted in the dark.**  Blaise Brandon was seen tallying the cash float by flashlight, and Cassius Reyes calmly held a person’s hand as the streetlights flickered out.  

Also on the block: the night’s silence made everyone appreciate the glow of a single coffee shop lamp.
