*The Cut Gazette — day 229*

## No Incidents Reported on Block  

**Police presence:** 24 officers were logged across Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but no crimes or arrests were recorded today.  

**Residents’ whispers:** Conversations floated about needing rides, staying safe, and keeping honest, yet no concrete plans emerged.  

**Late‑night watch:** Several locals—including Wes Doyle, Simone Adeyemi and Tariq “Tee” Bello—were seen awake or napping on the streets, keeping an eye on the neighborhood.  

Also on the block: the only traffic jam was a line of smokers watching the world go by.
