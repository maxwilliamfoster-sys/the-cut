*The Cut Gazette — day 230*

## 9th Breaks Riverside Door by Mistake  

**Mysterious car remains** – A vehicle with no plates has been idling on the corner for three days, drawing curious glances but no claims of ownership.  

**Police presence grows** – Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, joining the routine patrols at Riverside, Terraces and Civic.  

**Door‑knocking blunder** – The 9th knocked on a Riverside door, only to discover it was the wrong address, prompting polite apologies from the resident.  

Also on the block: even the streetlights seem to be taking a nap.
