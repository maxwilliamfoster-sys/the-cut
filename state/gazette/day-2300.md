*The Cut Gazette — day 2300*

## Two Police Cruisers Parked on Delmar  

**Police activity:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s modest police attention tally.  

**Local chatter:** Several residents—Marta Kowal, Lena Doyle, Maren Bello, and others—were heard worrying that the upcoming audit might catch them, while a separate group mentioned the new shop law and possible fines.  

**On the scene:** Errol Brannon was seen tallying coins behind the till, Farrah Serrano smoking by the door, and Odette Osei monitoring the machine’s screen.  

Also on the block: the only drama today was a ceiling‑flicker contest in Cassius Bello’s bedroom.
