## Officer Kohl Misplaces Long‑Closed File  

**Two extra cruisers** have been parked on Delmar since this morning, drawing extra police attention to the block.  

**A file resurfaced** when Officer Brady Kohl pulled a case that should have been closed years ago and was unable to replace it, prompting murmurs among colleagues.  

**“These laws keep choking us,”** muttered Blaise Petrakis, echoing the neighborhood’s growing frustration with recent bans.  

Also on the block: the night shift’s insomnia club reports a surplus of sleepers and one very awake Nia Doyle.
