*The Cut Gazette — day 2311*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The entire block lost electricity for six hours, leaving homes in darkness while rain hammered the streets.  

**Residents’ murmurs:** In the gloom, Maren Adeyemi asked if anyone noticed them, Nia Amadi wondered about her promotion, and Milo Adeyemi requested help with a sum.  

**Quiet observations:** Farrah Mbeki lay with eyes closed, Nyla Bello twitched as the TV flickered, and Rufus Bello listened to distant thunder.  

Also on the block: The rain may have powered the gossip, but not the lights.
