## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Power failure:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents in darkness and halting all routine activities.  

**Police presence:** Officers patrolled Riverside, Delmar, Terraces, and Civic areas, though no incidents were reported during the outage.  

**Residents’ night:** Cal Serrano adjusted his blanket, Gil Nakamura tightened his sweater, Iris Lindqvist hummed while settling in, Iris Petrakis checked the heater, Iris Nakamura covered her hands, and Jonah Bello listened as the building settled.  

Also on the block: the night was so quiet even the streetlights seemed to be napping.
