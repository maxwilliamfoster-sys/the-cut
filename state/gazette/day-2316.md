*The Cut Gazette — day 2316*

## 9th Breaks Door on Riverside  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, while patrols remain light on Riverside, Terraces and Civic.  

**Local chatter:** Residents were heard questioning the future of the block, from Blaise Petrakis wondering about leaving, to Cassius Bello lamenting his “loudness” and lack of uniqueness.  

**Sleepless nights:** Several locals – Lena Nakamura, Lena Petrakis, Marta Ferreira and others – were noted lying awake in the heat or clinic rooms.  

Also on the block: the only thing hotter than the weather is the rumor that the spill made the water taste like coffee.
