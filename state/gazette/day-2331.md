*The Cut Gazette — day 2331*

## Mysterious Car Stalls on Corner  

**Harbor Bar buzzes:** The bar stayed packed and noisy for several hours despite the heavy rain, with no incidents reported.  

**Unidentified vehicle:** A car that no one recognizes has remained parked on the same corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity from passers‑by.  

**Residents hunker down:** Several locals—Cal Kowal, Cassius Bello, Etta Adeyemi, Etta Bello, Farrah Lindqvist, and Iris Nakamura—were noted sleeping or dozing as the rain drummed on roofs.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s hair in place, but not their patience.
