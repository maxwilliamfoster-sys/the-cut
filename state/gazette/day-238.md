*The Cut Gazette — day 238*

## Block Loses Power, Six Hours Dark  

**Power outage:** A six‑hour blackout swept the block, leaving homes and shops without electricity until service was restored this evening.  

**Residents’ murmurs:** In the gloom, voices rose—from Malik Reyes questioning a barber’s role to Officer Brady Kohl worrying about his paycheck, and several others exchanging terse remarks about plans and control.  

**Quiet corners:** Few were seen talking; Simone Adeyemi tended a patient in whispers, while the Okonkwo family lingered in the silence, and June Kowal drove on, eyes fixed on the road.  

Also on the block: the only thing brighter than the candles was the neighborhood’s collective patience.
