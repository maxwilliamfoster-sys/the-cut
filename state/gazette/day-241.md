*The Cut Gazette — day 241*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:**  The block lost electricity for six hours, leaving homes and storefronts in darkness.  

**Junie Marsh:**  She told neighbors she’s “betraying herself to escape the cycle,” a decision she says is breaking her apart.  

**Simone Adeyemi:**  She remarked, “I’m paid, yet exhaustion still dominates my life.”  

Also on the block: the clouds stayed as gloomy as the power grid.
