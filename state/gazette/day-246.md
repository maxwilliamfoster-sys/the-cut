## Malik Reyes Finds Short Cash Count  

**Police patrols:** 16 officers patrolled Riverside while five each covered Delmar, Terraces and Civic.  

**Evening scene:** Cass Deveaux was nursing a drink alone; Det. Yolanda Ruiz and her crew kept watch; families—including Femi Adeyemi and Nadia Vasquez—slept soundly.  

Also on the block: the night was quiet enough that even the gossip was on a coffee break.
