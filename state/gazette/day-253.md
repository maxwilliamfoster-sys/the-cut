*The Cut Gazette — day 253*

## Block Blackout Leaves Residents Dark  

**Harbor Bar stays lively.**  The bar was packed and noisy for hours, and nothing untoward was reported before the lights went out.  

**Power outage lasts six hours.**  The entire block was without electricity, forcing everyone from diners to detectives into candlelight and conversation.  

**Quiet whispers fill the dark.**  Voices like Malik Reyes’ “I don’t know what to do next” and Officer Brady Kohl’s “You got the receipts for the last batch?” drifted through the night, while several locals were seen dozing or sleeping in unusual spots.  

Also on the block: The only thing brighter than the blackout was the bar’s neon sign still flickering stubbornly.
