*The Cut Gazette — day 266*

## Calloway’s costly favor to Deveaux  

**Mystery car lingers.** A vehicle no one claims has occupied the same corner for three days, drawing curious glances from passers‑by.  

**Police presence grows.** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the already‑noted patrols on Riverside, Terraces and Civic.  

**Cold snap whispers.** Residents report fog thickening and a chill off the water, prompting warnings from Det. Yolanda Ruiz and nervous chatter about “the new rule.”  

Also on the block: the unattended car still hasn’t received a parking ticket—or a driver.
