*The Cut Gazette — day 342*

## Dez Okonkwo vows not to leave  

**Inspectors on Delmar.** City inspectors marched down Delmar with clipboards, noting the ongoing hiring spree.  

**Hiring surge.** The log notes eight separate “hired” entries, indicating a brisk round of new hires across the block.  

**Night watchers.** Officer Brady Kohl, Sgt. Hal Brennan, and several residents—Hector Delgado, Chidi Okonkwo, Iris Amadi—were observed sleeping or listening to the rain, while Ms. Ivy Calloway kept the store till to herself.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone wet, but not a single umbrella was mentioned.
