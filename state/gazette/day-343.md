## Adaeze Okonkwo slams wrong door  

**Door mishap:** The 9th‑grade Adaeze Okonkwo forced a door open on Riverside, later discovering it was the wrong entry.

**Police watch:** Officer Brady Kohl was seen at Lyle Pawn; Detective Yolanda Ruiz, lingering on Terrace Block A, voiced concerns that Officer Kohl is targeting her.
