*The Cut Gazette — day 344*

## Detective Ruiz Reopens Cold Case File  

**File mishap:** Det. Yolanda Ruiz pulled an old, supposedly closed case file and now can’t replace it, sparking quiet concern among the precinct.  

**Night‑time ambulance:** An unidentified patient was whisked out of the Terraces just before dawn; no name was released.  

**Neighborhood whispers:** Cass Deveaux warned “I’m not buying” the street claim, while Malik Reyes said he can’t trust “what he’s hiding,” and Tariq “Tee” Bello offered a ledger, urging “Let her take the reins.”  

Also on the block: the rain keeps the streets slick, but the gossip stays oddly dry.
