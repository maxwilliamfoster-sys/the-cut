*The Cut Gazette — day 345*

## Dez Okonkwo and Iris Amadi Clash  

**Harbor Bar buzzes:** The bar stays packed and noisy, but no incidents are reported.  

**Street disputes:** Junie Marsh and Booker Lyle argue loudly enough to halt passing traffic, and later Dez Okonkwo and Iris Amadi have a similarly heated exchange.  

**Mystery notices:** Two doors on the Terraces are plastered with letters from an unfamiliar company; no one on the block claims to know the sender.  

Also on the block: the only thing hotter than the heat is the neighborhood’s collective curiosity about that stranger.
