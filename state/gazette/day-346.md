*The Cut Gazette — day 346*

## Okafor Pressed for Early Debt Payment  

**Ambulance rushes out of the Terraces** before dawn, taking an unnamed patient to the hospital; no further details were given.  

**Two extra police cruisers** have been stationed on Delmar all morning, while officers and residents exchange terse comments about protection, money and “the corner.”  

**Residents spotted late‑night:** Malik Reyes brooding on a bench, Tariq “Tee” Bello lying awake, and several locals sleeping at work, eyes and thoughts restless.  

Also on the block: the Harbor Bar stays loud, but the night stays uneventful.
