## Delmar Funeral Draws Block, Some Absent  

**Crowds gathered** at the Delmar funeral, though several regular attendees were missing.

**Payout surprise** – Nadia Vasquez received a larger‑than‑expected payment, prompting quiet speculation among onlookers.  

Also on the block: the Harbor Bar stayed noisy, but nobody noticed the missing rain‑coat.
