*The Cut Gazette — day 348*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in darkness until service was restored.  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the already‑elevated patrols on the block.  

**Dez Okonkwo’s plan:** Dez announced he sees a stranger as a threat and intends to leave the block tonight.  

Also on the block: Even the streetlights seemed to need a coffee break.
