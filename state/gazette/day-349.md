*The Cut Gazette — day 349*

## Sgt. Brennan Pressed for Early Payment  

**Police presence up.**  Patrols were noted on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic streets, reflecting heightened attention on the block.  

**Tensions heard at the checkout.**  Conversations captured at Calloway’s and around the block reveal disputes: Wes Doyle urges “move fast,” Dez Okonkwo promises a “plan tonight,” and Gus Petrakis complains Wes is “threatening my business.”  

**Local leaders on edge.**  Cass Deveaux suspects Dez of undermining her, while Nadia Vasquez says she’ll “dig deeper,” and Tariq “Tee” Bello hints at a forthcoming takeover.

Also on the block: the only thing not moving faster than the snow is the line at the coffee shop.
