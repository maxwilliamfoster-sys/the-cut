*The Cut Gazette — day 351*

## Ambulance Removes Unnamed Patient from Terraces  

**Inspectors patrol Delmar.** City officials continue their checklist walk down the block with clipboards, noting nothing out of the ordinary.  

**Quiet departures at dawn.** An ambulance whisked an unidentified person away from the Terraces just before sunrise; no name was given.  

**Murmurs among officers.** Officer Brady Kohl and Detective Yolanda Ruiz were heard discussing “a favor” and keeping an eye on the front door, while the Vasquez trio plotted their next move.  

Also on the block: Someone finally remembered to pay Dot Feeney’s tab.
