*The Cut Gazette — day 352*

## Rey Vasquez Seen Talking to Police  

**Tipsters alert families.**  Both Femi Adeyemi and Marisol Vasquez were told their relatives – Rey and Tiny Reyes – have been spotted in conversation with officers.  

**Detective chatter.**  Officer Brady Kohl complained about “ruining his shift,” while Detective Yolanda Ruiz warned “stay quiet, we’re not ready for that level yet.”  

**Neighborhood whispers.**  Residents note that Dez Okonkgo believes a stranger’s words confirm an escape plan, and Tiny Reyes boasts that “sticks make me tough enough.”  

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the police radios is the scrolling on Simone Adeyemi’s phone.
