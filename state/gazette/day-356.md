## Power outage sparks tension on block  

**Six‑hour blackout:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents stumbling in the dark and police noting activity at multiple corners.  

**Gus Petrakis at the clinic:** After the lights went out, Gus ended up at the clinic; his paperwork and injury reports don’t match, raising questions among on‑lookers.  

**Dez Okonkwo’s stand:** Dez announced he will not let anyone control his business, even if it costs him, while Cass Deveaux warned that Dez is undermining her authority.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift TV marathon keeps Det. Ruiz awake, but the power never seems to come back.
