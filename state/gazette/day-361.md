*The Cut Gazette — day 361*

## Det. Ruiz Targets Booker Lyle  

**Funeral draws the block.** A service on Delmar saw most residents in attendance, though a few expected mourners stayed away.  

**Debt becomes leverage.** Detective Yolanda Ruiz says Booker Lyle’s owed money will be used to pull him in, while Lyle accuses Ivy Calloway of sabotage.  

**Escape plot thickens.** Dez Okonkwo announced he will slip away tonight, confident no one can stop him.  

Also on the block: the Harbor Bar stayed loud enough to drown out any whispered secrets.
