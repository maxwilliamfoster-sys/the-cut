*The Cut Gazette — day 369*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block, Power Outage  

**Funeral attendance:** Most residents turned out for the Delmar service, though a few expected faces were missing.  

**Six‑hour blackout:** The whole block was without electricity for six hours, leaving everyone in darkness during the rain.  

**Quiet streets:** With Officer Brady Kohl, Simone Adeyemi, Nadia and Rey Vasquez all reported asleep, the night settled into an unusually still hush.  

Also on the block: Even the rain seemed to be counting cash.
