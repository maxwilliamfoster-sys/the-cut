*The Cut Gazette — day 374*

## Officer Kohl Presses Priya Raman  

**Debt dispute escalates.** Ms. Priya Raman was told by Officer Brady Kohl that the money she owes is needed immediately, and she replied that he must keep it safe for everyone.  

**Others weigh in.** Gil Lindqvist offered to hold the funds, while several locals—including Rey Vasquez and Nadia Vasquez—voiced loyalty to one another amid the tension.  

**Both sides claim innocence.** Dez Okonkwo says Officer Kohl is targeting him and plans to flee; Kohl counters that he’s being unjustly accused and must defend himself.  

Also on the block: the overcast sky seems the only thing staying neutral.
