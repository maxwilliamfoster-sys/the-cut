*The Cut Gazette — day 380*

## June Kowal, Sami Amadi clash publicly  

**Argument draws a crowd.** June Kowal and Sami Amadi were heard shouting in the street, forcing passersby to stop and listen.  

**Whispers of the docks.** Residents recalled recent sightings of a mysterious figure near the waterfront, while several locals voiced concern about “Tee” being used as a political pawn.  

**Quiet on the precinct.** Police activity was noted on four streets, but no arrests were reported; the block remains calm aside from the dispute.  

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than gossip is the wind behind the river‑side cafés.
