*The Cut Gazette — day 386*

## Booker Lyle eyes market post‑Tee conviction  

**Construction continues** – A new building went up on the block today, adding fresh storefront space.  

**Mysterious car** – An unmarked vehicle has sat at the corner for three days, drawing curious glances from passers‑by.  

**Tensions linger** – Residents cite Tee’s recent conviction; several locals say it may affect shop security and local business plans.  

Also on the block: the only thing the police seem to have caught is a dozing officer.
