*The Cut Gazette — day 395*

## 9th Kicks Wrong Door on Riverside  

**Incident:** The 9th kicked a door on Riverside, only to discover it was the wrong one; police logged 29 nearby calls.  

**Community reaction:** Residents warned that the city’s crackdown could tighten the law for their sons, stall shop plans and hurt the market.  

**On the scene:** Officers and locals were heard trading remarks about staying sharp, keeping the dock quiet and making their mark.  

Also on the block: the fog was the only thing that didn’t try to get into anyone’s business.
