*The Cut Gazette — day 396*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving shops and homes in darkness.  

**Gus Petrakis' warning:** Petrakis learned the money his group owes is being demanded earlier than expected, raising anxiety among the few who heard.  

**Police watch:** Officers were recorded at Riverside (25), Delmar (5), Terraces (5) and Civic (5) as the neighborhood dealt with the blackout.  

Also on the block: the coffee‑pourer swore the beans tasted better in the dark.
