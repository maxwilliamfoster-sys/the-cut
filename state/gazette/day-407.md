*The Cut Gazette — day 407*

## Maren Adeyemi confronts Fr. Boateng  

**Power outage:** The block was plunged into darkness for six hours, halting traffic and closing the Harbor Bar’s music early.  

**Street argument:** Maren Adeyemi and Fr. Emmanuel Boateng were heard shouting at each other, forcing passers‑by to pause and listen.  

**Quiet after the lights:** By nightfall the bar emptied, a few locals lingered in the dim, and the neighborhood settled back into its usual rhythm.  

Also on the block: Even the streetlights seemed to need a coffee break.
