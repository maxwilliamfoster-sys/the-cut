*The Cut Gazette — day 417*

## Tee Conviction Sparks Blockwide Concern  

**Notice boards:** Unfamiliar company letters appeared on two Terraces doors, drawing curious glances from passers‑by.  

**Funeral gathering:** Most residents attended the Delmar service, though a handful of expected mourners stayed away.  

**Bar buzz:** The Harbor Bar stayed packed and noisy for hours, but no incidents were reported.  

Also on the block: the fog rolled in just in time to hide anyone trying to sneak a quick “fix the door.”
