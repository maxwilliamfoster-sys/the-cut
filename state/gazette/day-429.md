## Rey Vasquez promises payment to Nia  

**Mysterious car lingers.** A vehicle with no registered owner has been parked on the corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity from passersby.  

**Quiet conversations.** Rey Vasquez told Nia Nakamura, “I’ll pay next week,” to which she replied, “Then we are done,” hinting at a settled debt.  

**Detective’s daydream.** Det. Yolanda Ruiz was seen tossing a pillow, apparently musing over a missing piece of evidence.  

Also on the block: the overcast sky gave everyone an excuse to stay indoors and watch the same old reruns.
