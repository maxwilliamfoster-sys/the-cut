*The Cut Gazette — day 430*

## Kwame Osei Calls Out Malik's Debt  

**Debt dispute heats up.**  Kwame Osei warned that Malik Reyes’ public refusal to pay “means I’m not respected on the block,” while Malik promised to keep his word and use the tension to his advantage.  

**Police linger.**  Officers reported modest activity on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but nothing beyond routine monitoring.  

**Bar stays noisy.**  The Harbor Bar remained packed and loud for hours, with no incidents reported.  

Also on the block: the only thing quieter than the back room was the city’s gossip hotline.
