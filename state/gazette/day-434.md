*The Cut Gazette — day 434*

## Kohl convicted; block reacts  

**Notice boards:** Unfamiliar‑letterhead flyers appeared on two Terraces doors today; no one on the block recognized the company.

**Local voices:** Residents voiced a spectrum of relief and ambition – from Dez Okonkwo’s claim that “corruption everywhere” is exposed, to Booker Lyle’s plan to “corner the market” now that Kohl is out of the picture.

**Night watch:** Several locals – Blaise Osei, Sgt. Hal Brennan, Iris Amadi, Odette Ferreira, Nadia Vasquez and Kwame Doyle – were observed dozing in various corners of the block.

Also on the block: the rain kept the streets cleaner than the gossip.
