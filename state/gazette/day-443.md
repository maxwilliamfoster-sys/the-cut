*The Cut Gazette — day 443*

## Errol Osei Told Chidi Met Police  

**Harbor Bar buzzes.**  The bar stayed packed and noisy for several hours, but no incident was reported.  

**A tip spreads.**  Someone whispered to Errol Osei that Chidi Okonkwo had been seen talking with police, sparking quiet speculation among the regulars.  

**Side‑note.**  Officer Brady Kohl wondered, “You think this peace will last?” while others muttered about personal “wars” and “walls closing faster.”  

Also on the block: the night’s lullaby was a solo performance by Maren Adeyemi.
