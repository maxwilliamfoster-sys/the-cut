*The Cut Gazette — day 446*

## June Kowal’s favour costs Errol Osei  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, adding to the routine patrols noted across the block.  

**Rumor mill:** A passerby warned June Kowal that Dez Okonkwo was seen talking to police, a detail that has not been confirmed.  

**Quiet corners:** Several residents—Dev Lindqvist, Femi Adeyemi and others—were observed dozing in front of the TV as the heat lingered.  

Also on the block: the only thing hotter than the weather is the gossip about who’s “handling the backroom.”
