*The Cut Gazette — day 452*

## Rosa's Death Sparks Blockwide Alarm  

**Ambulance rushes out of the Terraces before dawn**, whisking an unnamed patient away as the night ends.  

**Police reports note modest activity** – Riverside 5, Delmar 11, Terraces 14, Civic 5 incidents – but no further details were released.  

**Neighbors voice their worries**: several residents label Rosa a threat, while others pledge to keep shops open and “dig deeper” into the mystery.  

Also on the block: the diner’s “run” is still up for debate.
