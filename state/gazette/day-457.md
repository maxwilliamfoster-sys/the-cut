*The Cut Gazette — day 457*

## Tee Bello Conviction Sparks Blockwide Reactions  

**Clinic confusion:** Tariq “Tee” Bello was taken to the clinic; his paperwork lists one injury while his condition tells another story.  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s already heightened patrol count.  

**Community fallout:** A dozen locals—from Cassius Reyes to Dez Okonkwo—publicly cited Tee’s conviction as the reason for new plans, power moves, or even leaving the city.  

Also on the block: the snow‑covered sidewalks are now a popular nap spot for several residents.
