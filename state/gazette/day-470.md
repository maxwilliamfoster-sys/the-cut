*The Cut Gazette — day 470*

## Officer Kohl’s Favor Costs Calloway  

**Dez Okonkwo** reported the block’s count was short again – “not by much, but enough.”  

**Officer Brady Kohl** granted Marcus Calloway a favor that ended up costing them both something real, while **Nadia Vasquez** did the same for her sister Marisol.  

**Residents** voice growing unease: Amara Bello warns the city will turn against us, and Sgt. Hal Brennan says the system is collapsing.  

Also on the block: The rain keeps the streets soggy, but the gossip stays dry.
