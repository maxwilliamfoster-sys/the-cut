*The Cut Gazette — day 482*

## Amara learns Sticks spoke to police  

**Police chatter:** Officers noted Sticks sitting by the rain‑soaked terrace, watching but not speaking, while the precinct logged increased activity on Riverside and Delmar.  

**Community tension:** Dez Okonkwo announced he will “stay low” after claiming the police are “policing us for nothing.”  

**Neighborhood watch:** Wes Doyle was seen eating and eyeing Booker, and several residents voiced distrust, from Nadia Vasquez’s “can’t let them silence us” to Tariq “Tee” Bello’s warning that “you’re not ready for this yet.”  

Also on the block: the rain kept the sidewalks slick, but no one slipped—just more soggy gossip.
