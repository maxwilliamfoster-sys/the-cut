*The Cut Gazette — day 484*

## Cal Osei Spotted Talking to Police

**Sami Amadi** was warned that **Cal Osei** had been seen in conversation with officers, sparking quiet speculation among block residents.  

**Detective Yolanda Ruiz** was heard saying she’ll “get him when he’s next on the list,” hinting at a pending move.  

**Blaise Osei** maintains his innocence, calling the fine a “political stunt,” while **Amara Bello** says the conviction proves the city’s power and vows to cut ties.

Also on the block: the fog may be thick, but the gossip is even thicker.
