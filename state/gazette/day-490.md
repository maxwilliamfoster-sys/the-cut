*The Cut Gazette — day 490*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Power outage:** The whole block was plunged into darkness for six hours, leaving residents stumbling through evening routines and street lights off.  

**Unexpected payouts:** Theo Bello and Cal Osei each received larger‑than‑expected payments, a surprise they admit is “its own problem.”  

**Neighbors speak:** Simone Adeyemi urged calm, while Wes Doyle hinted the news “could change things,” and Detective Ruiz warned the night feels longer now.  

Also on the block: the only thing brighter than the blackout was the glow of a TV screen in Otis Bello’s living room.
