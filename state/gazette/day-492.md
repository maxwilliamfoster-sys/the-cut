*The Cut Gazette — day 492*

## Brennan, Ruiz Clash on Main Street  

**Public dispute:** Sgt. Hal Brennan and Det. Yolanda Ruiz shouted at one another in the street, drawing a crowd that had to be shushed by bystander Jonah Bello.  

**Tip about Tiny:** A passerby warned Ruiz that Tiny Reyes had been seen talking to police, prompting her to count a stack of notes and keep a close watch.  

**Clinic confusion:** Cal Osei arrived at the clinic where the paperwork listed one injury but the patient’s condition told a different story.  

Also on the block: Officer Kohl noted “these envelopes look like trouble,” and nobody’s sure what’s inside.
