*The Cut Gazette — day 493*

## Ruiz vows to take him down  

**Harbor Bar stays noisy but peaceful.**  For several hours the bar was packed and loud, yet no incidents were reported.  

**Tensions simmer among locals.**  Overheard conversations included Rey Vasquez warning a shop owner, Tariq ‘Tee’ Bello offering advice, and several residents promising to “make a name” or “dig deeper” amid the police’s heightened presence.  

**Police scan the block.**  Officers noted activity at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic precincts, with Officer Brady Kohl seen handling paperwork nearby.  

Also on the block: the city’s “quiet” day turned into a chorus of whispered ambitions.
