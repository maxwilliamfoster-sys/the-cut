*The Cut Gazette — day 506*

## Jonah Bello Declares He's a Failure  

**Police presence steady.** Officers patrolled the Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic areas, but no incidents were logged.  

**Community whispers.** Overheard remarks from locals—Det. Yolanda Ruiz, Gus Petrakis, and others—suggest ongoing, undisclosed plans on the block.  

**Jonah’s resolve.** The Bello brother voiced feeling judged and hopeless, a personal confession that floated through the afternoon air.  

Also on the block: the radios keep humming while the officers check their pockets.
