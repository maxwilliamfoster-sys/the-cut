*The Cut Gazette — day 507*

## Power Outage Leaves Block in Darkness  

**Police presence up:** Two extra cruisers patrolled Delmar while officers reported the blackout, and Officer Brady Kohl said he’s “just trying to keep the block safe.”  

**Residents cope:** With lights out for six hours, Maren Bello tugged her blanket tighter, Kwame Osei stretched and yawned, and Dr. Yusuf Amin urged a backup generator “now.”  

Also on the block: the mystery car still occupies the corner, apparently enjoying the quiet.
