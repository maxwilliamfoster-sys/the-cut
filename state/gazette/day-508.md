*The Cut Gazette — day 508*

## Ambulance Removes Unknown From Terraces  

**Police patrols:** Officers logged 33 stops across the block, most on Terraces and Delmar, as rain kept streets slick.  

**Morning mystery:** An ambulance whisked an unnamed person out of the Terraces before sunrise; no further details were released.  

**Quiet corners:** Residents curled up in diners and apartments, listening to the rain while the block stayed unusually still.  

Also on the block: even the rain seemed to need a coffee break.
