*The Cut Gazette — day 522*

## Odette and Adaeze Clash on Street  

**Loud dispute:** Odette Amadi and Adaeze Okonkwo were heard arguing in the middle of the block, drawing a brief crowd.  

**Police watch:** Officers logged a modest presence today—15 on Riverside, 5 each on Delmar, Terraces and Civic—while a neighbor warned Iris Amadi that Junie Marsh had been seen speaking with them.  

**Quiet corners:** Several residents, from June Kowal to Nia Mbeki, spent the first snowfall indoors, nursing drinks or drifting to sleep.  

Also on the block: the snow fell faster than the gossip.
