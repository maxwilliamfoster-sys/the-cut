*The Cut Gazette — day 528*

## Officer Kohl Misplaces Long‑Closed File

**Old case resurfaced** – Officer Brady Kohl pulled a file that should have been closed years ago and was unable to put it back in its proper place.  

**Unclaimed car lingers** – An unidentified vehicle has sat on the corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity from passers‑by.  

**Block attends funeral** – Most residents turned out for a funeral on Delmar, though a few expected faces were missing.  

Also on the block: The mystery car may finally earn a parking ticket—if anyone notices.
