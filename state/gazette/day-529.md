## Marcus Calloway and Jonah Bello Clash  

**Payout surprise:** A payment to “Sticks” exceeded expectations, prompting whispers about the extra cash and its attendant hassle.  

**Street showdown:** Calloway and Jonah Bello were heard shouting at each other, loud enough to pause passersby on the block.  

**Tension in the air:** Residents quoted worries about increasing police scrutiny and personal threats, from Booker Lyle’s “fragile position” to Dez Okonkwo’s feeling of “small punishment.”  

Also on the block: the neighborhood’s bedtime chorus now includes a chorus of restless sighs.
