*The Cut Gazette — day 535*

## Pre-Dawn Ambulance at the Terraces

**Terraces departure:** An ambulance arrived before dawn to take someone away from the Terraces. Neighbors have offered no names, leaving the identity of the patient unconfirmed.

**Slow day:** Police kept a routine presence along Riverside and the Terraces, but the block was otherwise entirely quiet. Little was said on the street, and none of it was worth putting into print.

Also on the block: The sudden cold snap seems to have chilled what little conversation was left.
