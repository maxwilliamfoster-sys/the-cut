*The Cut Gazette — day 538*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Power loss:** The entire block was plunged into darkness for six hours, leaving residents and businesses scrambling for light and heat.  

**Police activity:** Officers reported a total of 27 incidents across the block, with the highest counts at Riverside (13) and Terraces (7).  

**Community chatter:** Amid the blackout, Elias Reyes was noted by a neighbor speaking with police, while Det. Yolanda Ruiz warned locals to stay close and report any movement near the dock.  

Also on the block: the diner stayed silent, but the coffee still brewed.
