*The Cut Gazette — day 540*

## Detective Ruiz Misplaces Old Case File  

**Two extra cruisers** have been parked on Delmar since this morning, coinciding with a block‑wide funeral that many expected to attend but some did not.  

**Detective Yolanda Ruiz** opened a long‑closed file and, despite repeated attempts, could not put it back, prompting a cryptic remark about “drinking it” before the precinct “burns down.”  

**Whispers among officers** suggest routine ledger checks and cash reconciliations, but no further details emerged.  

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the sirens was the empty hallway at the funeral.
