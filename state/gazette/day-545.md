*The Cut Gazette — day 545*

## Block Leaders Label Tee Threat  

**No incidents reported.**  Police patrols were noted on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but the day remained uneventful.  

**Overheard conversations.**  Detectives, doctors, and neighbors were heard asking each other about ledgers, spare parts, and homework, while a few kids were coaxed to keep quiet or play.  

**Local figures united against Tee.**  Several community leaders—Dez Okonkwo, Amara Bello, Blaise Osei, Fr. Emmanuel Boateng, Booker Lyle and Sgt. Hal Brennan—publicly called Tee a threat to their plans or positions.  

Also on the block: the only drama was a missing ledger, not a missing person.
