*The Cut Gazette — day 554*

## Tee threatens block, residents fret  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the routine patrols noted across the block.  

**Community alarm:** dozens of locals—including Dez Okonkwo, Amara Bello, and Fr. Emmanuel Boateng—have voiced that “Tee” will drive them out, ruin businesses, and spark trouble.  

**Neighborhood watch:** Cal Serrano was observed dozing in front of his TV, a quiet reminder that life carries on amid the tension.  

Also on the block: the only drama today was a coffee cup that refused to stay warm.
