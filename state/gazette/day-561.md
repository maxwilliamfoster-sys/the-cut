*The Cut Gazette — day 561*

## Kwame Doyle and June Kowal Clash

**Street argument:** Kwame Doyle and June Kowal shouted loudly enough to halt passersby, drawing curious onlookers.

**Emergency call:** An ambulance whisked an unidentified patient away from the Terraces just before dawn; no name was given.

**Quiet whispers:** Officers Brady Kohl and Cass Deveaux exchanged terse words about “keeping it civil” while a neighbor urged “keep the kids quiet, Mom.”

Also on the block: the night was as quiet as the sleeping residents’ rosaries.
