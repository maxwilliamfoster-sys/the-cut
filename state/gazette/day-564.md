*The Cut Gazette — day 564*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block, Some Missing  

**Funeral attendance:** Most of the block turned out for the service on Delmar, though a handful of familiar faces stayed away.  

**City inspectors:** Inspectors made their rounds down Delmar with clipboards, noting routine concerns as they went.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents were overheard muttering about coffee shortages, property titles and other small grievances, but gave no further details.  

Also on the block: the only thing hotter than the heat was the number of people counting receipts on the corner.
