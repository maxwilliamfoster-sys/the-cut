*The Cut Gazette — day 569*

## Local ban fuels heated opinions  

**No incidents reported.**  Police logged routine activity at Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but nothing noteworthy occurred.  

**Residents vent frustrations.**  Adaeze Okonkwo, Sgt. Hal Brennan and others claim the new haircut ban unfairly targets men, while Amara Bello, Booker Lyle and Fr. Emmanuel Boateng argue it harms business and favors women.  

**Quiet corners observed.**  Cass Deveaux lingered with coffee, Hector Delgado counted boxes, and Dr. Yusuf Amin dozed to stay warm.  

Also on the block: the cold snap has everyone gripping coffee tighter than their opinions.
