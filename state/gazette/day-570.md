*The Cut Gazette — day 570*

## Block Power Outage Leaves Streets Dark  

**Power cut:** The block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in darkness and prompting a quiet scramble for candles and flashlights.  

**Harbor Bar:** The bar stayed packed and noisy throughout the outage, while Dez Okonkwo and Jonah Bello shouted an argument in the street that caused passers‑by to pause.  

**Night‑time scene:** Several residents—Theo Bello, Femi Adeyemi, Rey and Marisol Vasquez, Kwame Osei, and even Det. Yolanda Ruiz—were spotted sleeping during the blackout.  

Also on the block: the only thing brighter than the bar’s neon sign was the neighborhood’s collective patience.
