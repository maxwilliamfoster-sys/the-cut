*The Cut Gazette — day 571*

## Hector Delgado and Rey Vasquez Clash  

**Street dispute draws a crowd.**  Delgado and Vasquez argued loudly on the corner, forcing pedestrians to stop and watch while Rey lingered in a shop window.  

**Police presence stays high.**  Five cruisers now patrol Riverside, Civic and Terraces, with two extra units stationed on Delmar since this morning.  

**Det. Yolanda Ruiz receives unexpected payout.**  The payment exceeds the usual amount, prompting quiet speculation among the block’s residents.  

Also on the block: the only thing quieter than the rain is Femi Adeyemi’s search for a larger, silent home.
