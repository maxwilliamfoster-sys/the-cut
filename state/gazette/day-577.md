*The Cut Gazette — day 577*

## Dr. Amin Helps Kwame Osei  

**Police presence spikes on Delmar.** Two extra cruisers have been parked there since morning, raising the block’s patrol count to 22.

Also on the block: the workshop stays shut while the law “takes a coffee break.”
