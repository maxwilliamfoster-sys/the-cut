*The Cut Gazette — day 583*

## Reyes Told Bello Talked to Police

**Word on the street:** Cassius Reyes was told today that Amara Bello has been seen in conversation with local officers. 

**Pre-dawn call:** An ambulance carried an unidentified person out of the Terraces before daybreak, though neighbors offered no names.

**Heavy presence:** Two extra police cruisers spent the day idling along Delmar, keeping the stretch noticeably subdued.

Also on the block: People had remarkably little to say for themselves today, and none of it was worth repeating.
