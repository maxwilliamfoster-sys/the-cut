*The Cut Gazette — day 592*

## Block Plunged into Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** A six‑hour loss of electricity left the block in darkness and sweltering heat, forcing families to pause chores and seek shade.  

**Voices in the dark:** Rey Vasquez called out to his father, “Dad, the power’s out,” while Marisol Vasquez worried about restless kids; Officer Brady Kohl noted “It’s all gone.”  

**Tee’s conviction ripples:** Neighbors cite the recent conviction as a sign of tightening police scrutiny, prompting several to reconsider ties and security.  

Also on the block: the night‑time TV flickered like a lighthouse for the sleepless.
