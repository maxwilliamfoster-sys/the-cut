*The Cut Gazette — day 596*

## Cass Conviction Sparks Community Fear  

**Malik Reyes reports a shortfall.**  The latest count fell below expectations, prompting a call for additional hiring.  

**Local voices link the conviction to police scrutiny.**  Dez Okonkwo, Amara Bello, Blaise Osei, Fr. Emmanuel Boateng, Booker Lyle and Sgt. Hal Brennan all say Cass’s recent conviction signals law‑enforcement focus on their circle.  

**Quiet moments observed.**  Residents were seen lying awake or sleeping in the hall, with Jonah Bello staring at a glass of water, underscoring the tense atmosphere.  

Also on the block: the overcast sky didn’t dampen anyone’s habit of over‑thinking.
