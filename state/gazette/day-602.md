## Officer Kohl Reopens Long‑Closed Case  

**Rosa Reyes receives an unexpected payout.**  The amount exceeds what was anticipated, prompting whispers that it may destabilize local power balances.  

**Detective Yolanda Ruiz blames Officer Brady Kohl.**  Ruiz says Kohl’s retrieval of the dormant file is shielding Booker Lyle, hindering her investigation.  

**Dez Okonkwo vows to exploit the payout.**  He declares the money a threat to his standing and plans to use it against rivals.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift crew collectively mastered the art of sleeping in front of the TV.
