*The Cut Gazette — day 603*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block

**Funeral attendance** – Most residents gathered on Delmar for the service, though a few regulars were absent.  

**Emergency transport** – An ambulance removed an unidentified person from the Terraces before dawn; no name was given.  

**Police presence** – Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the routine patrol count.

Also on the block: the new bike shop is the talk of the night‑owls.
