*The Cut Gazette — day 605*

## Police Kick Wrong Riverside Door

**Mistaken Entry:** Officers from the 9th kicked in a door on Riverside today, though it turned out to be the wrong residence. The street drew the bulk of the day's patrol activity with thirty logged checks.

**Quiet Beats:** Delmar saw seventeen police visits, while Civic and the Terraces recorded five apiece under clear skies. Neighbors kept their thoughts to themselves in the aftermath, offering little to say and nothing overheard worth repeating.

Also on the block: A day otherwise so thin on activity that there was genuinely nothing to see.
