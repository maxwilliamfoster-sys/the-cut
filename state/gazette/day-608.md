*The Cut Gazette — day 608*

## Jonah Bello’s Favor Costs June Kowal  

**Count discrepancy:** Dez Okonkwo reports the block’s tally is again short, though only by a little.  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, while the 9th unit mistakenly kicked a door on Riverside.  

**Quiet corners:** Officers and residents alike linger in half‑lit rooms, listening to creaks and sipping water, as the day passes uneventfully.  

Also on the block: the mayor’s new bike shop is still the talk of the street, even if no one knows why.
