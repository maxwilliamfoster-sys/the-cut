*The Cut Gazette — day 616*

## Etta Bello Costs Sami Amadi  

**Favors with a price.** Etta Bello did a favor for Sami Amadi that ended up costing them something tangible, while Rufus Adeyemi’s aid to Rosa Serrano carried a similar real cost.  

**Detective’s gripe.** Det. Yolanda Ruiz complained she feels singled out by the police, noting “If Lyle’s here, I’m out of luck.”  

**Quiet tensions.** Residents overheard mutters about “keeping quiet,” “breathing down my neck,” and a looming shipment that must move before nightfall.  

Also on the block: the pawn‑shop sweep still leaves the cash‑register humming.
