*The Cut Gazette — day 620*

## Dez Okonkwo Flags Harbor Bar Shortfall  

**Bar finances:** The Harbor Bar’s night crowd was lively, but Dez Okonkwo discovered the cash count was again a bit low, prompting a quiet recount.  

**Police activity:** Officers logged modest activity across the block—riverside 5, delmar 49, terraces 13, civic 5—but no incidents were reported.  

**Sgt. Brennan:** While reviewing paperwork, Sgt. Hal Brennan retrieved an old file that should have stayed closed, and he has yet to replace it.  

Also on the block: the night’s “quiet” was only as quiet as a bar’s till after a shortfall.
