## Detective Ruiz Reopens Old Case  

**File mishap:** Detective Yolanda Ruiz retrieved a long‑closed file and, unable to replace it, kept the paper on her desk, sparking quiet murmurs among the block’s officers.  

**Sabotage whispers:** Tariq “Tee” Bello was heard noting, “The kids are out, but the diner will stay open,” while Dez Okonkwo hinted Tee may soon sabotage the shop.  

**Framing claims:** Both Dot and Hector Delgado reported feeling set up over the nightstand ban, and Officer Brady Kohl suspects Cass is trying to frame him publicly.  

Also on the block: the diner’s neon sign flickered just as the auditors’ van rolled by.
