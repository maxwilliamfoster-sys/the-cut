*The Cut Gazette — day 623*

## Mysterious Car Stalls Corner for Days  

**Police report:** Officers note heightened activity on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but give no comment on the unmanned vehicle that has lingered on the corner for three days.  

**Local voices:** Residents voice frustration, with several citing “unfair” city justice and “silly rules” while the parked car remains a silent mystery.  

**Overheard at precinct:** Officer Brady Kohl remarks, “We got a lot of eyes on us,” hinting at the block’s growing scrutiny.  

*Also on the block:* Even June Kowal can’t sleep, but the car still won’t move.
