*The Cut Gazette — day 624*

## Blaise Osei Convicted, Sparks Outcry  

**Jonah Bello** arrived at the clinic where paperwork listed one injury but his condition told another, and he was told the owed money was now due earlier than agreed.  

**Local leaders** – from Sgt. Hal Brennan to Fr. Emmanuel Boateng – denounced the conviction as evidence of an oppressive city law, while Booker Lyle warned it could hurt his business.  

**Det. Yolanda Ruiz** was seen stirring bad coffee and staring at her mug, a quiet reminder that the case remains under investigation.  

Also on the block: the new haircut ban has everyone guessing who’ll need a hat.
