## Cassius Reyes Flags Short Count  

**Finance glitch:** Cassius Reyes reported the city’s cash count fell short again, though only by a small amount.  

**Mysterious vehicle:** An unidentifiable car has remained parked on the corner for three days, drawing curious glances from residents.
