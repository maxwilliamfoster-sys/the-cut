*The Cut Gazette — day 648*

## Maren Bello Spotted Talking to Police  

**Police chatter:** Residents note increased attention on several streets, with the highest count at Delmar (75).  

**Community buzz:** Workers exchange grievances—missing pallets, unpaid coffees, and a lingering milk shortage—while others voice anxiety about city policies and personal safety.  

**Quiet corners:** Several members of the Okonkwo and Adeyemi families were observed sleeping, a reminder that life on the block keeps moving even amid rumors.  

Also on the block: the only thing that’s truly disappearing is the patience of anyone waiting for that missing pallet.
