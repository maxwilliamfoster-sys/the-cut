## Officer Kohl Helps Jonah, Pays Price  

**Favor with a cost:** Officer Brady Kohl gave Jonah Bello a favor that left both men “paying something real,” according to witnesses.  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, adding to the already‑busy block.  

**Street dispute:** Marta Doyle and Kwame Osei were heard arguing loudly enough that passersby stopped to listen.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s umbrellas from drifting away.
