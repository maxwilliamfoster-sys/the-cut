*The Cut Gazette — day 653*

## Ambulance Removes Patient from Terraces  

**Inspectors patrol Delmar.**  City officials moved down the block with clipboards, checking compliance on a quiet morning.  

**Neighbors voice worries.**  Etta Adeyemi hopes to see her mother smile, while Nadia Vasquez frets about a looming fine; Gil Lindqvist urges an early wrap‑up.  

**Sleep persists.**  Several residents—including Nia Nakamura, Maren and Otis Bello, Kwame Doyle, and Tariq “Tee” Bello—were spotted asleep around the block.  

Also on the block: the night’s silence was louder than the traffic.
