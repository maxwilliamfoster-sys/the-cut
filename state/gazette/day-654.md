*The Cut Gazette — day 654*

## Dez Okonkwo Finds Cash Short  

**Harbor Bar stays packed.**  The usual crowd kept the noise up for hours, but no incident was reported.  

**Dez Okonkwo notes a minor shortfall.**  The bar’s cash count was a little low, prompting a quiet recount.  

**Rain keeps the block damp.**  Officers on patrol tossed blankets over themselves while locals listened to the storm from doorways.  

Also on the block: even the rain seemed to whisper about the missing coins.
