*The Cut Gazette — day 657*

## Nadia Vasquez and Sami Amadi Clash  

**Argument draws a crowd.**  The two were heard shouting in the street, halting passers‑by until police noted the disturbance but reported no arrests.  

**Cash count still off.**  Malik Reyes discovered his ledger was short again, a point of quiet concern among the shop owners who heard murmurs about the missing amount.  

**Harbor Bar stays lively.**  Patrons filled the bar for several hours; despite the noise, no incidents were recorded.  

Also on the block: the only thing that stayed dry was the rain‑soaked sock Jonah Bello stared at.
