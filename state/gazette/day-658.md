## Lyle and Reyes Clash in Street

**Raised voices:** Booker Lyle and Tiny Reyes drew an audience today during a heated argument out in the street, loud enough to cause several passersby to stop and watch. 

**Heavy patrol:** Delmar saw a sharp increase in police attention, with two extra cruisers idling at the curb since morning hours.

**Little talk:** Neighbors kept their opinions largely to themselves, choosing instead to block window drafts and keep the coffee hot indoors.

Also on the block: Rufus Bello insists the soap went into the dish properly, regardless of floor-splashing allegations.
