*The Cut Gazette — day 660*

## Block Plunged Into Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The whole block was left without electricity for six hours, leaving residents in the dark and forcing Rey Vasquez to keep a generator humming for Nia Nakamura’s line.  

**Debt pressure:** Cal Osei received word that the money owed is now due ahead of schedule, a reminder of the lingering cash concerns noted by Kwame Doyle and Dot Feeney.  

**Quiet on the streets:** Apart from a few murmurs—Officer Brady Kohl’s complaint about weak coffee and Detective Yolanda Ruiz’s comment on “washing away lies”—little else was heard on the block.  

Also on the block: The only thing brighter than the coffee was the glare of an envelope on Officer Kohl’s nightstand.
