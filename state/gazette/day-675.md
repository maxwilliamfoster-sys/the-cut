*The Cut Gazette — day 675*

## Rosa Serrano Sent to Clinic

**Ambulance runs:** Two unnamed patients were lifted from the Terraces before dawn, each escorted out by ambulance crews.  

**Paperwork mismatch:** Rosa Serrano arrived at the clinic; her records list one injury while the treating staff note another.  

**Mayor’s threat:** Tiny Reyes was heard asking, “Did anyone hear the mayor’s threats today?” among the murmurs of residents.

Also on the block: the rain kept the streets soggy enough that even the mayor’s voice sounded muffled.
