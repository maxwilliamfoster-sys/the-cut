*The Cut Gazette — day 691*

## Brennan and Brannon Clash on Main  

**Street argument:** Sgt. Hal Brennan and Errol Brannon shouted at each other in the street, halting passers‑by.  

**Mystery ambulance:** An ambulance whisked an unidentified person out of the Terraces before dawn; no name was given.  

**Cash count again off:** Malik Reyes reported the block’s cash count is short once more, though only by a little.  

Also on the block: even the snow seems to be waiting for someone to finish the story.
