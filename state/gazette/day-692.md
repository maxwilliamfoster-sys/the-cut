*The Cut Gazette — day 692*

## Dot Convicted, Community Reacts  

**Mystery car lingers:** An unmarked vehicle has been parked on the corner for three days, drawing curious glances but no comment from police.  

**Police presence spikes:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, joining the routine patrols as city inspectors continue their clipboard‑filled walk down the street.  

**Local opinions swirl:** Residents and officials alike cite Dot’s recent conviction as a turning point—some see it as justice, others as a threat to their businesses or safety.  

Also on the block: the only thing moving faster than the paperwork is the rumor that the car will finally move tomorrow.
