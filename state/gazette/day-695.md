*The Cut Gazette — day 695*

## Nia Mbeki Helps Chidi, Pays Price  

**Police activity modest.**  The block logged 40 officers at Riverside, 73 at Delmar, and only single‑digit numbers elsewhere, a quiet day for law‑enforcement.

**Dez Okonkwo speaks out.**  He warned that “the justice system is biased against us,” echoing a growing sense of frustration among the Okonkwo family.

**Tensions linger.**  Officers and locals were heard promising to “keep him in line” and “watch out for him,” hinting at unresolved disputes behind the hallway chatter.

Also on the block: the coffee keeps stirring faster than the gossip.
