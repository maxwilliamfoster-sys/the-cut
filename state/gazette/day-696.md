*The Cut Gazette — day 696*

## Dez Okonkwo Calls Ban Silencing  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been parked on Delmar since this morning, joining a modest patrol across the block.  

**City inspectors:** Teams with clipboards are methodically working down Delmar, noting that the latest inventory count is “short again,” though only by a little.  

**Local chatter:** Residents were heard asking each other about records, cash flow, and missing documents, while a few offered help with “the docs” and “the balance.”  

Also on the block: the only thing disappearing faster than the inventory is the patience of anyone who has to count coins on the counter.
