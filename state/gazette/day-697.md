## Police Deploy Extra Cruisers on Delmar  

**Extra patrols:** Two additional police cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, a noticeable increase in visible law‑enforcement presence.  

**Community chatter:** Several locals—including Dez Okonkwo, Amara Bello, and Sgt. Hal Brennan—are voicing that Det. Yolanda Ruiz’s recent ban is “overreaching” and “unjust.”  

**Barroom buzz:** Officers and patrons traded barbed remarks; Officer Brady Kohl hinted at a cash dispute, while Booker Lyle reminded someone they still owe him.
