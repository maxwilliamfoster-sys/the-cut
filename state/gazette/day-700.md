*The Cut Gazette — day 700*

## Extra Police Cruisers Parked on Delmar  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been sitting on Delmar since this morning, as noted twice in the log.  

**Resident chatter:** Only a handful of comments were overheard—talk of a new street plan, mayoral threats, and shift schedules floated among neighbors.  

**Rain‑bound quiet:** Heavy rain kept most block residents indoors, with several seen sleeping or staring at ceilings as the storm drummed on.  

Also on the block: The rain kept everyone’s curtains drawn tighter than usual.
