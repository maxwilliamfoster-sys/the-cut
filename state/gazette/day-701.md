*The Cut Gazette — day 701*

## Rufus Bello’s Favor Costs Otis  

**Extra police cruisers** – Two additional units have been stationed on Delmar since early morning, adding to the already‑busy block patrol.  

**Six‑hour blackout** – The entire block was without power for six hours, leaving residents in darkness and prompting a chorus of worries about safety and fragility.  

**Rufus‑Otis deal** – Rufus Bello helped his brother Otis with a task that “cost them something real,” a phrase echoed in the hallway chatter.

Also on the block: fog makes even the streetlights look like they’re hiding.
