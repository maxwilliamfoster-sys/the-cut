*The Cut Gazette — day 705*

## Officer Kohl Reopens Long‑Closed File  

**Power outage stalls block:** A six‑hour blackout left the block in darkness, halting work and leaving residents shuffling by candlelight.  

**New construction noted:** A building went up on the block today, though details remain sparse.  

**Kohl’s surprise:** Officer Brady Kohl retrieved an old case file that should have been sealed years ago and was unable to replace it.  

Also on the block: Even the shadows seemed to linger longer than usual.
