*The Cut Gazette — day 716*

## Sgt. Hal Brennan Reopens Old File  

**Police activity modest.**  Rain kept the block quiet while officers logged 31 incidents at Riverside, 54 at Delmar, and a handful elsewhere.  

**Brennan’s slip noted.**  The sergeant admitted he “still can’t close that file,” prompting a brief exchange with Dr. Yusuf Amin and a few curious onlookers.  

**Sleep‑over scene.**  Residents Marisol Vasquez, Milo Adeyemi, Gus Petrakis, Nia Nakamura, Blaise Osei and Cal Serrano were all spotted dozing as the rain fell.  

Also on the block: the rain makes even the most restless feel like naptime.
