*The Cut Gazette — day 721*

## Power Outage Darkens Block Six Hours  

**Power loss:** The block was without electricity for six hours, leaving homes and streets in darkness.  

**Neighbors chat:** Amid the blackout, Maren, Rufus, Theo and others were overheard offering help and asking for chances, though little else was reported.  

**Snow watchers:** Vic Lindqvist, Gus Petrakis, Tiny Reyes, Clemmie Bello, Etta Bello and Jonah Bello were seen sleeping and watching the first snowfall.  

Also on the block: Even the snow seemed to RSVP before the lights.
