## Mysterious Car Stalls on Corner  

**Unidentified vehicle** – A car no one can name has been parked at the same corner for three days, becoming the block’s quiet mystery.  

**Neighbors chat** – Gil warns Kwame about a write‑up, Rey complains about loud volume, and Lena asks Cassius about silence, while the Lindqvists and Ferreiras exchange kitchen banter.  

**Rain‑bound residents** – Iris, Nadia, Simone, Amara, Theo and Wes spend the afternoon listening to the steady drumming of rain on roofs and windows.
