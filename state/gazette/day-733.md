*The Cut Gazette — day 733*

## 9th Kicks Wrong Door on Riverside  

**Police patrols:** 29 officers on Riverside, 24 on Delmar, 11 on the Terraces, 5 on Civic.  

**Night‑time incident:** An ambulance removed an unidentified person from the Terraces before dawn; no name was given.  

**Local grievances:** Dez Okonkwo and Errol Brannon each cite the new nightstand ban, linking it to personal loyalty tests and a brother’s disappearance.  

*Also on the block:* The Harbor Bar stayed noisy, but nobody complained.
