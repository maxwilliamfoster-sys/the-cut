*The Cut Gazette — day 736*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** A six‑hour loss of electricity left the block in darkness, prompting residents to huddle, keep voices low, and wait for lights to return.  

**Unexpected payout:** Simone Adeyemi received a payment larger than anticipated, a detail noted by onlookers but left unexplained.  

**Neighbors adapt:** While Nadia Vasquez scrolled notes and Tariq “Tee” Bello trimmed hair, others—Elias Reyes, Dr. Yusuf Amin, and Jonah Bello—sat in quiet, eyes on the muted TV.  

Also on the block: the blackout gave everyone a rare excuse to pretend the neighborhood wasn’t a mess.
