*The Cut Gazette — day 737*

## Inspectors Patrol Delmar Amid Growing Tension  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the already‑high police attention in the area.  

**Community unease:** Several residents voiced fear after hearing that Chinedu was murdered, with comments from Booker Lyle and Sgt. Hal Brennan highlighting concerns about street safety.  

**Night watch:** Det. Yolanda Ruiz was noted dozing nearby, her mind “racing,” as others on the block slept uneasily.  

Also on the block: the overcast sky made the streetlights look even more tired than the locals.
