*The Cut Gazette — day 738*

## Iris Lindqvist Helps Kwame Osei  

**Power outage stalls block.**  A six‑hour blackout left homes and shops in darkness, but no further incidents were reported.  

**Harbor Bar stays busy.**  The bar remained full and noisy for several hours; patrons noted nothing amiss despite the storm.  

**Whispers of police talk.**  A resident warned Clemmie Bello that Simone Adeyemi had been seen speaking with police, fueling quiet speculation on the block.
