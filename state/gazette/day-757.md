*The Cut Gazette — day 757*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block’s Majority  

**Funeral attendance:** Most residents turned out for the Delmar service, though a handful who were expected stayed away.  

**Harbor Bar:** The bar was packed and noisy for several hours, but no incidents were reported.  

**Local concerns:** Dez Okonkwo voiced feeling undervalued, while Gus Petrakis warned a new ban could tarnish his business’s reputation.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift bartender heard a whisper about “burning bridges” and thought it was about the grill.
