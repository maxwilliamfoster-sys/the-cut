*The Cut Gazette — day 758*

## Rufus Bello Told of Police Talks  

**Tip to Rufus:** A neighbor whispered that both Jonah Bello and Milo Adeyemi were spotted talking to police.  

**Local chatter:** Cass Deveaux pressed Gil about progress on the vacant lot, while Gil replied with news on a lease; Hector noted the new crates, and Det. Yolanda Ruiz asked about Lyle Dot.
