*The Cut Gazette — day 761*

## Errol Brannon seen talking to police

**Mystery car lingers.** A vehicle with no recognizable owner has been parked on the corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity from passersby.

**Urgent night transport.** An ambulance whisked an unnamed person away from the Terraces just before dawn; no further details were offered.

**Whispers of scrutiny.** Jonah Bello was told that Errol Brannon has been seen talking to police, a detail that has set the block’s gossip circulating.

Also on the block: the night‑shift crew swore they heard Det. Yolanda Ruiz mutter, “We need to act,” before the streetlights flickered out.
