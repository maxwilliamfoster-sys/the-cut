## Simone Convicted, City Tightens Grip  

**Police activity modest.** 22 calls on Riverside, 5 each on Delmar, Terraces and Civic; no incidents logged.

Also on the block: the only drama was a whispered question about “2 + 2” at the Bello kitchen.
