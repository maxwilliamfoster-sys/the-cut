*The Cut Gazette — day 769*

## Cassius Reyes and Kwame Doyle Clash  

**Bar crowd stays noisy.** The Harbor Bar remains packed and loud, but no incidents are reported beyond the usual clatter.  

**Street argument draws a crowd.** Reyes and Doyle are heard shouting at each other on the sidewalk, forcing passers‑by to pause and listen.  

**Rain‑soaked residents linger.** With rain pouring, several locals—Rey Vasquez checking car parts, Rufus Bello clutching a stuffed animal, and others—are seen sheltering nearby, keeping to themselves.  

Also on the block: the rain may be steady, but the gossip is still a drizzle.
