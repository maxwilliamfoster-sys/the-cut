## Cassius Reyes Receives Unexpected Payment  

**Payment surprise:** A payout to Cassius Reyes exceeded expectations, prompting “its own problem” for the parties involved.  

**Harbor Bar scene:** The bar stayed packed and noisy for several hours, but no incidents were reported.

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the bar was the rain‑soaked snore‑contest.
