*The Cut Gazette — day 779*

## Block Blacked Out for Six Hours  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, drawing extra attention to the area.  

**Officer’s mishap:** Officer Brady Kohl retrieved an old file and was unable to replace it, adding to the day’s confusion.  

**Residents adapt:** With the lights out, Marisol and Nadia Vasquez kept dinner going, while others watched static TV and tapped feet in the dark.  

Also on the block: even the coffee‑spilling priest found the outage a good excuse for a slower morning.
