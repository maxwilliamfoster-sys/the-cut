*The Cut Gazette — day 780*

## Funeral Draws Most of Block  

**Attendance:** Most of the block turned out for the Delmar funeral, though a handful of regulars were noticeably absent.  

**Quiet chatter:** While mourners gathered, snippets of conversation floated about mugs, cartoons and upcoming shows, but little else was said.  

**Weather:** Rain fell steadily, keeping the streets slick but not stopping the turnout.  

Also on the block: even the rain seemed to RSVP.
