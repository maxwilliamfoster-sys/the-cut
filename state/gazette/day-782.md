*The Cut Gazette — day 782*

## Theo Bello Seen Talking to Police  

**Etta Bello alerted.**  A neighbor whispered to Etta that her brother Theo had been observed in conversation with detectives, prompting quiet curiosity on the block.  

**Detective Ruiz hopeful.**  “We’re close, just a few more leads,” Det. Yolanda Ruiz told a small crowd, hinting the inquiry is progressing.  

**Family chatter continues.**  Amid the rain, Theo joked about building a fort, while relatives voiced odd concerns—from watching over each other to a proposed haircut ban.

Also on the block: the rain may wash away more than the street grime, but not the neighborhood gossip.
