*The Cut Gazette — day 787*

## Jonah Adeyemi Declared Threat  

**Police focus:** Units were dispatched to Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic, but no incident was reported.  

**Community reaction:** Several residents – including Dez Okonkwo, Amara Bello, and Sgt. Hal Brennan – publicly labeled Jonah’s recent conviction as a danger to the block.  

**Harbor Bar:** The bar stayed full and noisy for hours; officials noted “nothing is wrong” despite the heightened police presence.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift crew kept a tighter inventory than ever – just in case.
