*The Cut Gazette — day 790*

## Block Plunged into Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power loss:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents stumbling in darkness and prompting a flurry of nervous chatter.  

**Missing person:** Detective Yolanda Ruiz confirmed someone is missing, urging caution as neighbors gathered in the unlit streets.  

**Cass’s conviction:** Several locals, from Dez Okonkwo to Booker Lyle, cited Cass Deveaux’s recent conviction as a sign the system—or their own futures—remain fragile.  

Also on the block: the night proved a perfect excuse for a “quiet” stroll.
