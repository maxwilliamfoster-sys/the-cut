*The Cut Gazette — day 792*

## 9th Kicks Riverside Door  

**Power outage stalls block:** A six‑hour blackout left Riverside and neighboring streets in darkness, halting traffic and delaying deliveries.  

**Neighbors condemn Vic:** Several residents—including Dez Okonkwo, Amara Bello and Sgt. Hal Brennan—publicly labeled Vic a threat and called for his punishment.  

**Quiet at the clinic:** Simone Adeyemi urged keeping the local clinic calm amid the unrest, while others were heard debating “different roads” and “starting a war.”  

Also on the block: Even the night‑shift security guard dreamed of a door that wouldn’t squeak.
