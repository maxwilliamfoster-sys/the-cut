*The Cut Gazette — day 800*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block Residents  

**Funeral turnout:** Most of the block gathered on Delmar for the service, though a few expected mourners were absent.  

**Okafor’s favour:** Chinedu Okafor helped Dez Okonkwo, a gesture that ended up costing the latter something tangible.  

**Mysterious car:** An unclaimed vehicle has been idling on the corner for three days, drawing quiet curiosity.  

Also on the block: the rain finally gave the streetlights a chance to shine.
