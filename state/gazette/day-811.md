*The Cut Gazette — day 811*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power loss:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents in darkness while conversations about police contacts continued.  

**Misdirected door:** The 9th attempted to kick a door on Riverside, only to discover it was the wrong one, prompting a brief, confused commotion.  

**Police whispers:** Both Cassius Bello and Rufus Adeyemi were told that local residents Sami and Marisol Serrano have been seen speaking with police, fueling quiet speculation.  

Also on the block: Farrah Mbeki swears she’s being watched, while Marta Doyle suspects Cass of betrayal.
