*The Cut Gazette — day 817*

## Dez Okonkwo Declares He’s Owed All  

**Harbor Bar** – The bar stayed full and noisy for hours, but nothing untoward occurred.  

**Neighborhood chatter** – Voices were heard swapping keys, asking about moving down the block, and worrying about shop security; tensions ran high.  

**Quiet corners** – Sgt. Hal Brennan was found napping on a couch, while several residents rested alone in rooms and hallways.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone indoors, so the gossip got louder than usual.
