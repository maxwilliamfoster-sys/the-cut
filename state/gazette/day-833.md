*The Cut Gazette — day 833*

## Mbeki, Ruiz Argue on Delmar  

**Inspectors** marched down Delmar with clipboards as two extra police cruisers idled nearby.  

**Cal Mbeki and Det. Yolanda Ruiz** raised voices in the street, drawing a brief crowd before the argument faded.  

**Harbor Bar** stayed packed and noisy for hours, but no incident was reported.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift looks unusually sleepy.
