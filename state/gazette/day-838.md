*The Cut Gazette — day 838*

## Isaac Adeyemi Favors Cassius Bello  

**Power outage:** The block was plunged into darkness for six hours, leaving homes and shops without electricity.  

**Debt pressure:** Iris Amadi learned the money she owes is now being demanded sooner than the original agreement.  

**A costly favor:** Isaac Adeyemi helped Cassius Bello, a gesture that left him “paying something real,” according to onlookers.  

Also on the block: the night‑shift crew spotted several Bells snoozing in a row—proof that even in a blackout, some families keep their bedtime routine.
