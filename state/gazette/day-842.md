## Delmar Funeral Draws Most Residents  

**Attendance:** Most of the block turned out for the funeral on Delmar, though a handful of regulars stayed away.  

**Quiet Observations:** Etta Bello was seen turning over and sighing softly, while Dr. Yusuf Amin rubbed his temples under the steady rain.  

**Milo Adeyemi:** The youngster wiggled, opened his eyes briefly, then hummed a low tune before closing them again.  

Also on the block: The rain kept the pigeons off the sidewalks.
