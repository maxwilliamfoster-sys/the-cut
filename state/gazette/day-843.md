*The Cut Gazette — day 843*

## Extra Police Cruisers Park on Delmar  

**Harbor Bar** – The bar stayed packed and noisy for several hours, but no incidents were reported.  

**Delmar Patrol** – Two additional police cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, drawing curious comments from locals.  

**Blockwide Quiet** – Aside from the bar, most residents—including the Bello family and several neighbors—were noted as asleep, and little else was heard.  

Also on the block: the night shift will need a new coffee supplier after the bar’s “extra loud” policy.
