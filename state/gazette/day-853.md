*The Cut Gazette — day 853*

## Malik Reyes Spotted Talking to Police  

**Power outage stalls block.**  A six‑hour blackout left homes and shops in darkness; city inspectors continued their Delmar walkthrough with clipboards despite the gloom.  

**Tensions surface in whispers.**  Residents heard Priya Raman promise backing, Rey Vasquez warn “keep your hands off my shop,” and Wes Doyle question Lyle’s intimidation, while Marta Doyle was informed of Reyes’s police contact.  

**Sleepy witnesses.**  Several locals—including Nadia Vasquez, Det. Yolanda Ruiz, Hector Delgado, and Jonah Bello—were noted asleep during the day’s events.  

Also on the block: the only thing staying awake was the overcast sky.
