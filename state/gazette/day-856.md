## Two Police Cruisers Parked on Delmar  

**Extra patrols:** Two additional police cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, drawing extra attention to the street.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents were heard muttering about counting, shoe‑tying and missing coins, but offered little concrete news.  

**June Petrakis:** The local elder was seen lingering in a dim room, eyes fixed on the ceiling.
