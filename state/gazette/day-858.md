*The Cut Gazette — day 858*

## Detective Ruiz Moves Against Booker Lyle  

**Funeral on Delmar draws most residents.** A few expected mourners stayed away, but the block’s majority gathered for the service.

**Detective Ruiz confronts Lyle over ledger.** Ruiz warned Lyle that the police are closing in, while Lyle insisted he won’t move.

Also on the block: **Even the broken‑window fixer seems to have a clear view of everything.**
