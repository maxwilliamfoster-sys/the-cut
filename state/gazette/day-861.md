*The Cut Gazette — day 861*

## Sgt. Brennan Misplaces Old File  

**File mishap:** Sgt. Hal Brennan opened a long‑closed case file and was unable to replace it, prompting a quiet stir among the precinct.  

**Wrong door:** The 9th unit forced entry on Riverside, only to discover they had kicked the incorrect door, adding to the day’s confusion.  

**Side remarks:** Residents voiced concerns—Dez Okonkwo warned of “city power cracking,” while Amara Bello said recent convictions threaten her shop’s future.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone’s hair a little longer than planned.
