*The Cut Gazette — day 862*

## Block Darkened by Six‑Hour Outage  

**Power loss:** The whole block was without electricity for six hours, leaving residents stumbling in the dark.  

**Harbor Bar:** Despite the blackout, the Harbor Bar stayed packed and noisy, with patrons claiming “nothing is wrong.”  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, a subtle reminder of heightened attention.

Also on the block: even the ceiling seems to have taken a night shift.
