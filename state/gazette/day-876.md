*The Cut Gazette — day 876*

## Nia Mbeki, Cassius Reyes clash  

**Street quarrels draw a crowd.**  Nia Mbeki and Cassius Reyes argued loudly enough that passers‑by stopped to listen.  

**Another heated exchange.**  Isaac Adeyemi and Chinedu Okafor later engaged in a similarly noisy dispute on the same block.  

**Family gossip spreads.**  Someone whispered to Etta Adeyemi that her sister Maren Adeyemi had been seen talking to police.  

Also on the block: fog makes the water look mysterious, but the street chatter is even thicker.
