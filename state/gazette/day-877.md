*The Cut Gazette — day 877*

## Lena Ferreira Says Block Held Hostage  

**Inspectors on Delmar.** City inspectors continued their walk‑down of Delmar with clipboards, noting nothing beyond the usual routine.  

**Lena’s claim.** Lena Ferreira announced she feels “held hostage” and must free her family, though no further details were given.  

**Police presence.** Officers reported modest activity on the block: riverside 5, delmar 12, terraces 8, civic 5.  

Also on the block: the only thing sleeping more than the residents is the overcast sky.
