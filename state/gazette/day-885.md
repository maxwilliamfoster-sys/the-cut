*The Cut Gazette — day 885*

## Court Convicts Booker Lyle  

**Cash shortage noted:** Cassius Reyes reports the block’s count is again a little low, though he says “enough” to notice.

**Funeral attendance:** Most residents gathered for the Delmar funeral, though a few expected faces were missing.

Also on the block: the terrace couch‑cushions are still missing their usual pile of blankets.
