*The Cut Gazette — day 886*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Most Block Residents  

**Funeral attendance** – The ceremony on Delmar attracted the majority of the block, though a handful of expected faces were missing.  

**Street dispute** – Wes Doyle and Marisol Vasquez were heard arguing loudly in the street, halting passersby who stopped to listen.  

**Quiet whispers** – Residents were overheard trading remarks about tools, shop security and a new law, all spoken in low tones as the cold snap settled in.  

Also on the block: the only thing staying warm tonight is the collective “I’m cold” chorus from the sleeping crowd.
