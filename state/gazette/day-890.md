*The Cut Gazette — day 890*

## 9th Kicks Wrong Door on Riverside  

**Funeral on Delmar draws most of the block.** A few expected faces were missing, but the crowd still filled the pews.  

**The 9th’s door‑kick turns out to be a mistake.** The resident struck a Riverside entrance that wasn’t theirs, prompting a brief police note.  

**Quiet murmurs linger among neighbors.** Residents were heard asking about the mayor, a pending appeal and the unresolved Reyes case, but little else was said.

Also on the block: the only thing louder than the overcast sky was Theo’s toe‑wiggle.
