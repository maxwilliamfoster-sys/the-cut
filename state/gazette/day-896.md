*The Cut Gazette — day 896*

## Nia Mbeki’s Debt Called Early

**Terms tightened:** Nia Mbeki received word today that what is owed has come due ahead of the agreed schedule. No further details were volunteered as to why the timeline suddenly shifted. 

**Patrol rounds:** Police cruisers kept a steady presence through the freeze, tallying seven passes through the terraces, six on riverside, and five apiece along delmar and civic. 

**Silent streets:** The cold snap appeared to keep everyone inside with their mouths shut, leaving neighbors with very little to say and nothing overheard worth setting down in print.

Also on the block: The street offered so little to look at today that the logbook stayed entirely blank.
