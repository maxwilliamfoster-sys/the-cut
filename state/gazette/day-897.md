*The Cut Gazette — day 897*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Power Outage  

**Residents complain:** Vic Lindqvist asked why the lights wouldn’t stay on, while Cal Serrano and Odette Ferreira discussed getting the generator running.  

**Quiet in the dark:** With the heat rising, families huddled in the dim glow of a TV, and the block’s usual chatter fell to a whisper.  

**Police note:** Officers logged five calls each from Riverside, Terraces, and Civic, plus one from Delmar, but reported no incidents beyond the outage.  

Also on the block: the only thing brighter than the generator’s hum was the neighborhood’s collective sigh.
