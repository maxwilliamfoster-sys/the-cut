*The Cut Gazette — day 901*

## Detectives Pull Long‑Closed File

**Sgt. Hal Brennan** and **Det. Yolanda Ruiz** each retrieved a case file that should have been sealed years ago and were unable to return it to storage.

**Overheard in the precinct** – Farrah Mbeki declared “All accounted for,” while June Amadi noted she had “taken care of the register.”

**Block chatter** – Dez Okonkwo mused about rising to power by aligning with the right patrons, and Tariq “Tee” Bello warned that Amara running the diner could boost the whole block.

Also on the block: the night shift crew is more asleep than awake, but the shelves stay full.
