*The Cut Gazette — day 904*

## Tee's Conviction Sparks Blockwide Concern  

**Local leaders voice alarm.**  Rey Vasquez, Detective Yolanda Ruiz, and several residents cited the recent conviction of Tariq “Tee” Bello as a threat to families, jobs, and safety on the block.  

**Quiet vigilance observed.**  Sgt. Hal Brennan kept watch over sleeping colleagues, while Booker Lyle lingered by the door and Cal Serrano held a glass, all noting the tension.  

**No other incidents reported.**  Aside from the murmurs, the day passed without any recorded events.  

Also on the block: the night shift’s lull was broken only by the hum of a lone refrigerator.
