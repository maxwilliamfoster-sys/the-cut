## Brennan’s Favor Costs Ruiz  

**Police presence:** Officers logged 15 each on Riverside and Delmar, with five each on Terraces and Civic as the first snow blanketed the block.

**Local whispers:** Odette Amadi hinted at “a new batch,” while Dot Feeney reminded Vic Lindqvist of an unpaid tab, and Rey Vasquez warned a “loose end” remains.  

Also on the block: The snow fell faster than the gossip.
