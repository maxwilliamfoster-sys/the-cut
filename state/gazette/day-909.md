*The Cut Gazette — day 909*

## Two Police Cruisers Parked on Delmar  

**Harbor Bar stays noisy.**  Patrons reported a packed, loud evening but no incidents.  

**Extra police presence noted.**  Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s routine patrols.  

**Quiet whispers heard.**  Several locals muttered cryptic remarks about power, safety and rent while the bar buzzed.

Also on the block: the night’s volume could have drowned out the police radios.
