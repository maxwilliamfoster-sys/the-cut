*The Cut Gazette — day 917*

## Delmar Patrols Increase, Count Still Low  

**Police presence:** Two extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the block’s modest police attention tally.  

**County clerk’s trouble:** Dez Okonkwo reports the paperwork count is short again—just enough to be noticed.  

**Neighborhood watch:** Residents heard a handful of muted remarks, from “Your family’s safe, but you’re not quiet” to “Can I help with the numbers later?”  

*Also on the block:* Cal Doyle’s TV stare‑off rivals the police cruiser lineup.
