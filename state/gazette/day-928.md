*The Cut Gazette — day 928*

## No incidents reported today  

**Police activity modest.**  Officers logged a total of 62 calls across the block, the highest count on Delmar (47) and only a handful elsewhere.  

**Residents mostly quiet.**  Overheard conversations ranged from Rey Vasquez urging “keep the line moving” to Nia Osei’s plea for “that room,” but no concrete developments were shared.  

**Sleep on the streets.**  Several locals—Cal and Dev Mbeki, Rufus and Theo Bello, and Rufus Adeyemi—were seen sleeping in public areas.  

Also on the block: the overcast sky kept the mood as flat as the morning coffee.
