## Nia Osei and Cassius Adeyemi Clash  

**Street argument:** Nia Osei and Cassius Adeyemi were heard arguing loudly enough to stop pedestrians in the middle of the block.  

**Community concerns:** Residents such as Adaeze Okonkwo, Amara Bello and Sgt. Hal Brennan warned that recent legal pressure and crackdowns are making the neighborhood feel unsafe.  

**Morning observers:** Milo Adeyemi and Theo Bello stood by the breakfast crowd, while Farrah Mbeki was seen counting a float.  

Also on the block: The rain may wash out the float, but not the gossip.
