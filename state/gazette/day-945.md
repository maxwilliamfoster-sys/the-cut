*The Cut Gazette — day 945*

## Harbor Bar stays noisy, no incident  

**Police presence:** Officers patrolled the Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic blocks today, but reported no disturbances.  

**Bar scene:** The Harbor Bar was packed and loud for several hours; patrons and staff reported everything ran smoothly.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents were heard muttering about plans, water, scholarships and the thunder, while a few lingered at their windows and dishes.  

Also on the block: the rain kept everyone indoors, so the gossip was a little dryer than usual.
