*The Cut Gazette — day 949*

## Delmar Funeral Crowds Block Streets  

**Funeral attendance high.** Most residents turned out on Delmar, though a few expected mourners were absent.  

**Hearse mishap.** The procession’s hearse was double‑parked past the deli, prompting comments from Errol Brannon.  

**Police presence.** Two additional cruisers have remained on Delmar since morning, adding to the block’s usual patrol count.  

*Also on the block: the coffee’s still fresh, but the kettle’s gone cold.*
