*The Cut Gazette — day 956*

## Power Outage Plunges Block Into Darkness  

**Police presence:** Extra cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since morning, adding to the 25 officers already on the block.  

**Community response:** With six hours of darkness, residents like Jonah Bello and Marta Doyle slept, while Marisol Vasquez kept guests fed and Femi Adeyemi stayed hydrated.  

**Local chatter:** Voices on the street—Hector Delgado, Cassius Reyes, and Sgt. Hal Brennan—repeated the mantra “no surprises” as the power flickered back.  

Also on the block: the rain kept the streets soggy, but nobody complained about the extra puddles.
