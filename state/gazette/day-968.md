*The Cut Gazette — day 968*

## Extra Cruisers Parked on Delmar

**Heavy presence:** Two extra patrol cruisers have been sitting on Delmar since early this morning, keeping a steady watch on the street. 

**Brief reprieve:** The Harbor Bar was packed to the gills and loud through the evening, though for a few hours at least, nothing went sideways.

**Thin log:** Rainy conditions kept most people indoors and uncharacteristically tight-lipped, leaving neighbors with little to say and nothing overheard worth printing.

Also on the block: A full tavern with zero trouble suggests the weather did everyone a favor.
