*The Cut Gazette — day 969*

## Block Darkened by Six‑Hour Outage  

**Power loss:** The entire block was without electricity for six hours, leaving the Harbor Bar and nearby homes in darkness until service was restored.  

**Police presence:** Two additional cruisers have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, prompting quiet comments from residents about the extra attention.  

**Cash count:** Bar manager Elias Reyes noted the cash drawer was slightly short again, though the discrepancy was minor.  

Also on the block: the stray cat that Otis Bello pets seems to have claimed the empty shelves as its new throne.
