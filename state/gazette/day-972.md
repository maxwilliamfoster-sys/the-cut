*The Cut Gazette — day 972*

## Two Patients Evacuated From Terraces  

**Police reports:** 21 calls at Riverside, 10 at Delmar, 18 at Terraces, 5 at Civic – the highest activity came from the Terraces early this morning.  

**Harbor Bar:** The bar stayed packed and noisy for several hours; no incidents were reported.  

**Neighborhood chatter:** Residents were heard urging each other to “watch the back door,” “match the cash,” and “keep the crates moving,” hinting at routine concerns rather than crisis.  

Also on the block: the snow fell as quietly as the block’s gossip.
