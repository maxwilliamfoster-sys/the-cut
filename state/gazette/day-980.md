*The Cut Gazette — day 980*

## Block Endures Six‑Hour Blackout  

**Power outage:** The block lost electricity for six hours, leaving homes and businesses in total darkness.  

**Mysterious notices:** Two doors on the Terraces were posted with letters from an unfamiliar company, prompting questions among neighbors.  

**Tensions rise:** Several locals were overheard exchanging veiled threats and promises, suggesting underlying disputes.  

Also on the block: The barber shop’s late‑night lullaby was a snore, not a shave.
