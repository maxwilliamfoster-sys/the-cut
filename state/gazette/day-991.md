*The Cut Gazette — day 991*

## Detective Ruiz Reopens Cold Case File  

**File mishap:** Det. Yolanda Ruiz retrieved a long‑closed case file and was unable to return it to storage, drawing police attention across several streets.  

**Misdirected entry:** The building’s 9th floor resident tried a door on Riverside, only to discover it was the wrong one.  

**Quiet block:** Aside from a few murmurs about schedules and a new bedtime rule, most residents were asleep listening to the rain.  

Also on the block: Even the rain seemed to have a better attendance record than the locals.
