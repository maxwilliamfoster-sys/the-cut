*The Cut Gazette — day 992*

## Inspectors and Police Patrol Delmar Street  

**City inspectors** continue their clipboard sweep down Delmar, noting nothing out of the ordinary.  

**Two extra police cruisers** have been stationed on Delmar since this morning, adding to the routine presence recorded across the block.  

**The Harbor Bar** remains full and noisy, but no incidents have been reported.  

Also on the block: the snow may be first, but the night’s lull is louder than the bar’s music.
