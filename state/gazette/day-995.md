*The Cut Gazette — day 995*

## Officer Kohl Opens Long‑Closed File  

**File mishap:** Officer Brady Kohl pulled a file that should have been sealed years ago and was unable to put it back, drawing police attention on Riverside, Delmar, Terraces and Civic.  

**Morning chatter:** As the fog rolled in, locals traded nervous comments about the storm and coffee—Det. Yolanda Ruiz even begged for “more eyes‑open brew.”  

**Block quiet:** Apart from the brief mutterings, little else was heard on the block.  

Also on the block: the fog is the only thing that’s managed to stay invisible longer than the paperwork.
