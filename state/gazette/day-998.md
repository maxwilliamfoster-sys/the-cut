*The Cut Gazette — day 998*

## Delmar Funeral Draws Block  

**Sgt. Hal Brennan** received a warning that a debt is due sooner than expected, prompting a terse exchange with Det. Yolanda Ruiz.

Also on the block: rain kept everyone humming, but the lamps stayed unlit.
